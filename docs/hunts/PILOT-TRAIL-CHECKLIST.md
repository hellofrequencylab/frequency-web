# Free Trail pilot field kit (FH-019)

Acceptance pending: two named field runs. Status lives in the [build backlog](../BUILD-BACKLOG.json). Use this blank kit to plan and record a pilot. The pilot lead owns the go or stop call and records the results after each run.

## Opening checklist

- Name the pilot lead, creator, player, local placement operator and support lead for each run. Agree on the walk window, meeting point, support contact and a way to stop the test.
- Record the approved environment, serving commit, Trail revision and current switch states. Use the [Hunts switch runbook](../RUNBOOKS.md#frequency-hunts-switches) and record who can pause the Trail and restore the approved switch state. Keep rewards and other capabilities off unless the pilot lead has a separate approval.
- Choose a free Trail with three or more stops and a free finish. Walk it before inviting anyone. Get permission for each stop, check opening hours and access, and agree on an accessible fallback. Use the [creator checklist](CREATOR-FLOW.md#publish-checklist).
- Confirm the approved player and placement URLs on the intended host. Keep the Trail unlisted for the closed pilot unless the owner approves public listing. Check QR print, NFC readback and the printed short code at every stop using the [placement kit](PLACEMENT-KIT.md).
- Capture the legacy control results with Hunts off. Assign iPhone and Android coverage, a weak-signal stop, and a second device for resume. Use the [accessibility script](ACCESSIBILITY-REPORT.md).

## Onsite tests

Run the complete sequence twice and record the names and roles for each run.

| Step | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Creator drafts, previews and publishes the approved revision | Preview records no progress or grants. The approved player link opens the intended published revision. |
| Player scans a QR, taps an NFC tag or types the short code | All available paths reach the same placement. Opening a placement alone creates no credit. A signed-out visitor can sign in and continue in the intended Hunt. |
| Player claims and continues | The explicit claim credits the intended stop once. A retry or repeat scan creates no duplicate credit or grant. Locked clues and places stay hidden. |
| Player loses signal or changes device | Offline progress stays pending until confirmed. The second device resumes the same run and shows the same confirmed revision. |
| Operator uses the agreed stop drill | Paused or revoked proof cannot advance the run. Existing confirmed progress remains visible. The player can reach the agreed support contact. |
| Player finishes | The free path completes, the result agrees with the authoritative record, and there is no duplicate grant. |

## Legacy controls

Repeat the same approved controls after the pilot switches are enabled and again after the agreed rollback. Record before and after observations for a legacy QR campaign, a Regulars check-in and stamp, the expected legacy reward outcome, and a read-only legacy ticket listing. Use only approved test accounts and fixtures. The commerce control ends at the listing. A changed result stops the pilot.

## Support drill

1. Simulate an inaccessible stop or an unexpected pending claim. Have the player describe the issue using the approved test channel.
2. The support lead records the time, Trail revision, stop and non-sensitive evidence reference. The operator checks whether to pause or revoke the placement and confirms that the player’s existing progress stays visible.
3. The pilot lead decides whether to resume, reroute or stop. Record who acted, which settings were restored, and the legacy control results after rollback. Capture screenshots with personal data and raw proof removed.
