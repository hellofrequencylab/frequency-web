# Product-side drafts for D4, D7 and D8 (FH-023, FH-035, FH-045)

Status is the ledger. These three rows need a human with legal, privacy or finance authority to sign, so this page is the product side only, drafted 2026-11-10 under the owner's delegation. It is a starting position for that person, not an approval, and the rows stay open.

## D4 contact policy (FH-023, Privacy and CRM)

- **Opt-in per named Space, channel and purpose.** An unchecked box, shown after the benefit is earned or from a voluntary follow link. Email only to start, using the verified account email resolved server-side.
- **Equal outcomes.** Decline and accept give the same progress, access, rewards and prices. The copy never trades a reward for consent.
- **Records.** Each opt-in stores consent version, time, source and the named Space. A second merchant's choice is a separate record. No sponsor gets a combined roster.
- **Withdrawal** suppresses first, then updates scope and downstream audiences. Global suppression stays authoritative. Erasure never re-enables sends.
- **Essential messages** (a reward ready, a closed stop) use essential content only and are checked independently of marketing consent.
- **Player area** shows consent history, withdrawal, and data access and deletion intake.
- **Needs a human:** jurisdiction and exact retention periods, California privacy and email-rule review, and the adults-only versus youth policy.

## D7 one-seller funds flow (FH-035, Finance and legal)

- **One accountable seller** for the first paid pilot, selling an Experience Pass as a tier on a canonical Event ticket. Destination charges through the existing ticket and Stripe path; no new money rail.
- **A Hunt claim never moves money or activates an entitlement.** A signed Stripe delivery settles the canonical ticket or order; the pass is projected from that, one entitlement per valid seat.
- **Refunds and capacity** follow the canonical rules: full refund revokes unused entitlements, partial refund needs an explicit seat mapping, a used benefit opens a service case rather than reopening inventory.
- **Daily reconciliation** during the pilot, by currency and provider account, with finance owning exceptions. Freeze sales if payee authority drifts.
- **Needs a human:** seller and payee identity, tax responsibility, charge and refund allocation, ticket terms, provider readiness, and a list of live payment evidence gaps by path with owners.

## D8 beneficiary and sponsor model (FH-045, Legal and finance)

- **Free play.** Donating is optional, separate and receipted. Public progress separates promised from paid.
- **Approved beneficiary and a capped sponsor pledge**, with a verified payee and documented settlement. Settled gifts are reported apart from pledges.
- **No chance-based prize mechanics.** Any raffle or draw design fails template review pending dedicated legal authorization.
- **Needs a human:** beneficiary status and payout capability, agreement and reporting terms, tax copy, donor consent, jurisdiction, and the refund policy with a beneficiary contact.
