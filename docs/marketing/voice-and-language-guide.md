## 1. The idea

Frequency is a collective where people boldly create together in a meaningful space of love. We help community builders, coaches, guides, healers, teachers, yogis and mindful people gather, collaborate and build circles, not walls, toward a better world to live in together.

It is not about filling a room. It is about meaningful connection.

Two lines, two jobs:

| Line | Job | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Build circles, not walls.** | The idea and the stance. | Hero and section lines, the story, about, social. |
| **Get people together. Do things on purpose.** | The tagline. Plain and repeatable. | Site footer, email footer, sign-offs. |

(To confirm: the two can swap.)

## 2. Who we talk to, and the pain behind each

Lead every page with the reader's pain point, in their words. Then show the way through.

| Reader | Their pain (their words) |
|---|---|
| Member | "It's hard to make friends as an adult." "I moved here and don't know anyone." "I have a hundred contacts and no real friends." "I'm always wired." |
| Coach, healer, guide | "I'm good with people and buried in logistics." Six tools, chasing payments, losing touch. |
| Studio, instructor | People come to class and drift. No way to know who is new, who stopped coming. |
| Event host | A great night, then the crowd disappears. Starting over each time. |
| Venue | Many groups use the space; nobody knows who they are. |
| Non Profit | Supporters care but go quiet between campaigns. |
| Network leader | Chapters scattered, each doing its own thing. |

## 3. The voice

**One plain voice, everywhere.** Bold in conviction, warm in the ask, casual in the sentences.

- **Pain first.** Name the real difficulty before offering anything. Never shame the reader for it; most people feel it (41% of US adults report loneliness, per Stanford's 2025 survey).
- **Bold means a stance, not hype.** "Build circles, not walls." Not urgency, not superlatives.
- **Inviting means permission.** "Come as you are." "Start with one Thursday."
- **Specific over inspirational.** One real detail per block: a chair, a walk, a Thursday.
- **Love, plainly.** The word is welcome ("a space of love"). Never decorated.
- **Casual mechanics.** Contractions, short sentences, sentence case, no em dashes, almost no exclamation points.

## 4. No hard claims

Speak to the need, not to a guarantee.

- No outcome promises: no "you will feel," "you'll grow," "double your," "never again," "guaranteed," "heals."
- No claims about what energy work, astrology or any practice does to a person. Say what we do and what people may notice: "Some people find...", "Try it and see."
- No invented numbers or testimonials. Use real, current figures only, and cite them.
- Facts about the product and price are fine and should be said plainly (for example, no cut of your bookings, month to month).
- Soft verbs: help, make room for, give you a place to, keep, remember. Avoid: boost, skyrocket, maximize, ensure.

## 5. Seasons, rhythm and energy: said like a regular day

Frequency's programs follow the seasons, equinoxes, solstices and the astrological calendar, with consistent meetups. Treat all of it as calendar fact, not belief, the way a farmer or chef talks about the season.

- State what is happening and when: "Autumn equinox gathering, Sept 22."
- Say what we do with it: "Day and night are equal, so we slow down and take stock."
- Use astrology as a calendar label ("Aries season," "new moon"), with no commentary on what it means for the reader. No predictions, no "the stars say."
- Each season affects everyone in their own way; invite people to do it their own way.
- **Rhythm** and **season** are house words. Rhythm is our most ownable word.
- Energetic and nervous system words (energy, nervous system, regulate, settle, calm) are ordinary vocabulary here, used where they fit and explained in a line the first time. Not hushed, not sensational, not a headline hook.
- Never: unlock, elevate, transform your life, manifest your dreams, awaken, tribe, ascend.

Test: a yogi, an astrologer and a skeptical neighbor can all read it aloud.

## 6. The word "community"

Use it. People search for it, and our audience calls themselves community builders.

1. Put it in page titles, H2s, meta descriptions and the first 100 words.
2. Pair it with something concrete in the same sentence (a place, a person, an activity). It never carries a headline alone.
3. Prefer the doing form: *community builders, building community, gathering.*
4. Rotate with circle, gather, neighbors, belonging, together.
5. Intent phrases to own: *how to build community, community events near me, find your community, spiritual community, wellness community, start a community group, community builder.* Volumes are unconfirmed; check Search Console before betting a headline on one.

## 7. Headlines by door (pain-point first, no hard claims)

Formula: **name the difficulty or the person, then the way through, in one line.** Drafts, current line first:

| Door | Today | Draft |
|---|---|---|
| Home, finding | Find local groups and events near you. | Making friends as an adult is hard. Start with one Thursday. |
| Home, hosting | Host local events and groups people come back to. | Bringing people together shouldn't take six apps. |
| Coaches and healers | Your practice without six logins | You're good with people. Keep the paperwork out of the way. |
| Studios | Every class, every member, one place. | Class is where people meet. Give them a reason to know each other. |
| Event hosts | Sell the tickets. Keep the room. | A great gathering deserves a way to remember the people in it. |
| Venues | Fill the room, and know who filled it. | A home for every group that meets in your space. |
| Non Profits | Raise more. We take no cut. | The people who believe in your work deserve to be known. We take no cut. |
| Networks | (current) | Every chapter is its own circle. Keep them connected. |

("We take no cut" is a plain product fact and stays.)

## 8. Features as story

Name a feature only inside the story of what someone gets to do. Product names (Space, Crew, Circle, Zaps) stay as in [`NAMING.md`](../NAMING.md).

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| Contacts holds every guest, a list you keep | Remember who walked in, and welcome them back by name. |
| Check-in | Greet people at the door and know who came. |
| One room, one list | Everyone who gathers with you, in one place. |
| Run the studio, not the back office | Spend your time with your people, not your paperwork. |
| Everything you run, circling you | Your sessions, your people and your payments in one place. |
| Bookings + CRM | Let people sign up and pay without the chasing. |

## 9. Word system

- **Own:** circle, gather, rhythm, season, together, belonging, practice, heart, regular, every Thursday, show up, come back, community (with a concrete partner).
- **Plain verbs:** meet, sit, walk, make, share, host, invite, thank.
- **Retire:** fill the room, full house, packed, software-for headlines, back office, logins, "one list" lines, and the hype list in §5.

## 11. Review checklist

1. Does the first line name a person or a real difficulty, not a function?
2. Is there one concrete image (a chair, a walk, a Thursday)?
3. Any hard claim, promise or invented number? Remove it.
4. Could a yogi, an astrologer and a skeptic all read it aloud?
5. Is "community" next to something real?
6. Is every feature told as what someone gets to do?
7. No em dashes, sentence case, no hype words.
