# Free Spotlight ad pack

Copy and asset slots for the "Get a Free Spotlight" doorway. Voice: [`voice-and-language-guide.md`](voice-and-language-guide.md) (pain first, plain words, no outcome promise, no timing claim, no em dashes). Every ad lands on `/launch/start` with the utm values the button carries (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign=free-spotlight`, optional `utm_content`), built by `freeSpotlightHref` in `lib/launch/entry.ts`. The first-touch cookie records them on arrival. Nothing here goes to a pixel beyond the campaign and the surface: no persona, Journey, Circle topic or wellness answer.

## The ad

| Part | Words |
|---|---|
| Headline | One page to send people to. |
| Alternate headline | Your links, in one place. |
| Body | You do good work, and your links are scattered across six places. A Spotlight puts who you are, what you offer and how to reach you on one page. A free Space comes with it. |
| Short body (small placements) | One page for who you are and what you offer. Free. |
| Button | Get a Free Spotlight |
| Fine print | Free. We ask you to make an account when you publish, not before. |

## Asset slots

| Slot | Size | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| Feed square | 1080 x 1080 | A finished Spotlight on a phone (390 x 844, the design system's iPhone ratio), a real Space's page, not a mock-up. |
| Story / reel | 1080 x 1920 | The same phone, the page building beside a form, no voice-over claim. |
| Link preview | 1200 x 630 | The headline over a Spotlight, the Frequency mark, no price. |

## Rules for any variant

- Say what it is and what it costs (free). Do not say how long it takes until the admin funnel shows a real median.
- No testimonial or number that is not a current, real, cited figure.
- No "AI writes your page". Vera quotes and arranges the person's own words.
- A Space's site theme never appears styling anything but a picture of that site.
