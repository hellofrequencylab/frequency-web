## ADR-1632: A repeatable capture node credits once per member day: its ledger key carries the member's own calendar day, while once-per-user and first-scan-only nodes keep the (node, actor) key (LIVE-654)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · backlog `LIVE-654` (closed here), filed by the 2026-09-29 replan ([ADR-1624](DECISIONS.md)) out of DEF-DOMAIN · day resolution per the member-day helper (`lib/member-day.ts`) · re-tested first per [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md) · numbered 1632 by the orchestrator's assignment

**Context.** Re-tested on `main` at `3be7bedd4` on 2026-09-29: `captureNode` (`lib/engagement/capture.ts`) wrote the `node_capture` ledger row under `node:<nodeId>:<actorProfileId>` for every node, and its header still carried the "repeatable nodes need a request-scoped key" TODO. The verifier (`lib/engagement/verify.ts`) lets a `repeatable` node through every time, so the ledger alone collapsed the second scan: it returned `already_captured` before the `captures` insert and the zap award. A `repeatable` node therefore credited a member once ever, and a Space check-in node (`lib/spaces/checkin.ts` creates them `repeatable`, "a member may check in to an event more than once over time") logged a member's first check-in and refused every later one. The premise held. The admin QR studio offers three rules and labels this one only "Repeatable", with no stated cadence.

**Decision.**

1. **A repeatable node's window is the member day.** `captureWindowKey(nodeId, actor, captureRule, day)` returns `<nodeId>:<actor>` for `once_per_user` and `once_global`, and `<nodeId>:<actor>:<day>` for `repeatable`. `captureNode` reads `capture_rule` with the node and, for a repeatable node only, resolves the day with `resolveMemberDay(actor)`: the member's stored `home_timezone`, else UTC. A scan on a new day writes a new ledger row and credits the node's `zaps_value`; a double tap or a retry the same day collides and returns `already_captured`, exactly as before.
2. **The day is the server's, never the device's.** `CaptureAttempt` does not gain a client key. A nonce from the scan would let a member mint a fresh key per tap, and a device-sent day could be backdated; the server-resolved member day has neither hole and is the same day the practice log keys on.
3. **The Space check-in timeline follows the same window.** Its `recordSpaceMemberActivity` key becomes `checkin:<window>`, so a member checking in on a later day lands on the Space's timeline again, and a same-day retry still does not double-log.
4. **What stays once per (node, actor).** The North-Star `practice.verified` event (`practice_node:…`) and the `in_person_checkin` trust signal keep their base keys. Neither is a credit; repeating them daily would change metric and trust weight, which this row does not rule on.
5. **No economy value moved.** `zaps_value` is unchanged and still set per node by the operator; a check-in node stays at 0. The row fixes how often a declared-repeatable node credits, not what it pays. No migration: `capture_rule` already exists.

**Rejected.** A request-scoped nonce threaded through `CaptureAttempt` (the 2026-07-21 Spaces audit's shape, and one of the row's two options): unbounded repeat credit on a QR that can be screenshotted, and a double tap would only collapse if the client reused the nonce. Keeping the zap credit on the base key while recording each capture (the same audit's other half): it leaves "Repeatable" crediting once ever, which is the bug. A new `once_per_day` rule beside `repeatable`: a fourth rule with no node that wants the old behaviour.

**Consequences.** ✅ A repeatable node credits once per member per day, and a Space check-in counts and logs every event day. ✅ Once-per-user and first-scan-only nodes behave exactly as before. ⚠️ An existing repeatable node with a positive `zaps_value` now pays that amount once a day per member instead of once ever; an operator who wanted once ever should switch it to "Once per member". ⚠️ A member with no `home_timezone` rolls over at UTC midnight.
