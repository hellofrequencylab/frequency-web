## ADR-1731: A Collective membership counts in every member Space, and a co-hosted Event's tickets split through the Market's separate-charges plan

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-12 · backlog `LIVE-767` · phase 8 of [ADR-1709](DECISIONS.md) · builds on [ADR-1576](DECISIONS.md) (the funds-flow seam) and [ADR-1614](DECISIONS.md) (the transfer ledger) · migration `20270346008100_event_revenue_splits.sql`

**Context.** ADR-1709 promised Collective "connection that works": one membership across a network of member Spaces, and a share of the money when Spaces co-host. By 2026-10-12 member Spaces (LIVE-763), the network page and report (LIVE-764, LIVE-766) and shared listings (LIVE-765) had shipped, and the help page named shared membership and revenue splits as the one piece still on the way. The ticket checkout was a destination charge to one host (`transfer_data.destination`); the Market already took two sellers through a platform charge with `transfer_group` and paid each by transfer from `commerce_order_transfers`.

**Decision.** (1) **Shared membership is read-time and follows the member-Space rule.** `sharedMembershipScope` asks `memberSpacePlan` with the child's plan pinned to `free`, so the answer is the relationship alone: parent active on a Collective plan, same owner on both sides, child active, neither root, parent not itself a member Space. With no open row of their own in a member Space, a viewer's ACTIVE membership in the parent counts there, labelled `source: 'collective'` with the Collective it came from. A waitlist spot does not travel. Nothing is written to `space_memberships`; detaching the Space or the parent's downgrade ends it on the next read. `getMyMembership` carries the fallback, so every surface that reads it (the Space front door, the people page, the member directory, the join surface) honours it; the members-only ticket tier gate and the paid-members Circle door read the same helper. A membership bought in the member Space itself stays that Space's own. (2) **Only the home Space's owner sets shares, only for accepted Collaborators, at most 9500 bps together, every payee payable.** A share names an accepted `event_space_shares` Space; the home Space keeps at least the platform's top network rung (500 bps); a payee whose owner account is not ready under `payoutsLive` is refused at set time, and dropped at checkout with its share staying home, so a sale never waits on a Collaborator's account. (3) **A ticket with a payable share takes the Market's separate plan, not a parallel one.** The checkout writes a `commerce_orders` row (`owner_kind = 'split'`, `funds_flow = 'separate'`, `metadata.split` in the shape `planTransferShares` reads, plus `ticket_event_id`) before the Stripe session, builds the platform charge with `transfer_group` and no `transfer_data` or `application_fee_amount`, and the settle flips the order paid and pays each Space from the ledger. The platform fee is the one the home Space's rung priced on the whole gross (the receipt is unchanged); it is taken from every share pro rata, floored, remainder to the home Space, so the shares sum to the charge and their fees to the fee the row records. A refund is a plain refund on the platform charge and reverses each transfer pro rata through the split-refund path, by PaymentIntent, the same way a Market refund does; the buyer's Orders list leaves the ticket record out, and each Space's earnings view shows its share.

**Rejected.** A second transfer ledger for tickets (two ledgers drift, and the reconciler, the refund and the seller's view already read one). Pricing each Collaborator's share at its own rung (the rung belongs to the Space whose sale it is; the home Space's receipt stays one number). Writing an inherited row into `space_memberships` for the member Space (a detach would then need a sweep, and the plan inheritance it mirrors is read-time for the same reason). Journey splits in the same change: the Journey checkout does settle through `lib/commerce/checkout.ts`, but a split there needs its own table, an owner surface on the Journey editor and synthetic seller groups in the pricing loop, so it is its own row.

**Consequences.** `scripts/probes/collective-shared-membership.mjs` holds the rule, the sum invariant and the refusals for LIVE-767. `event_revenue_splits` is service role only. Journey splits stay on the help page's still-being-built list.
