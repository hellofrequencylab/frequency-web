## ADR-1733: Space setup: a new Business or Nonprofit Space walks its owner through seeding before the Circles manager

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-12 · backlog `LIVE-901` · **Amends** [LIVE-261](BUILD-BACKLOG.json)'s landing (`pnpm ledger:view LIVE-261`) · builds on [ADR-986](DECISIONS.md) (the Studio kernel: one manifest, composed surfaces), [ADR-990](DECISIONS.md) (one Spark runner) and [ADR-1249](DECISIONS.md) (the governed create, which `createSpace` already takes and this change leaves alone).

**Context.** Owner ruling, 2026-10-12: *"I set it up and thought I would be taken through the seeding process, but it just created it and dumped me in a circle. Double check and make sure that starting a space takes people through the seeding wizard process."* The premise was re-tested on main before the work. `createSpace` (lib/spaces/provision.ts) redirected every new Space to `/spaces/<slug>/manage/circles`, the landing LIVE-261 chose on 2026-09-09 so that a new owner saw hosting rather than the console's CRM command center. That ruling fixed the wrong first screen and left the next one unbuilt: a Space four seconds old has no tagline, no story, no logo, no contact details and no offerings, and nothing asked for them. `SPACE_MANIFEST` (lib/studio/entities/space.ts) already declared the sections such a walk needs (model, identity, story, contact, links, offerings) and which prose Vera drafts (`veraDrafts`), the Spark kit (components/studio/spark) already carried the shell, and `docs/STUDIO.md` still read "Wizards on the kit: none yet". The only seeding flow was the staff Business Seeder (app/(main)/admin/business-seeder, lib/importer/materialize.ts), which researches a business from its website and is deliberately not a member door: `space.ts` leaves `url` out of `accepts` on purpose.

**Decision.**

1. **One pure rule for where a new Space lands, and for which steps setup walks.** `lib/spaces/setup.ts` owns both. `postCreateLanding(type, slug)` sends a console type (`isConsoleSpaceType`: business, nonprofit) to `/spaces/<slug>/setup` and every other type to `/spaces/<slug>/manage/circles`, LIVE-261's landing unchanged. `spaceSetupSteps(manifest)` reads the step order off the Space manifest's own sections: story, identity, contact and links, offerings, then "First things", the one step the manifest cannot declare. A section the manifest drops takes its step with it; no step is invented beside the manifest. `createSpace` redirects through the rule; the comments at provision.ts, create-space-form.tsx and bundles.ts that still said `/manage` or `/manage/circles` now say this chain.
2. **The walk is a Spark on the kit, and it saves on every Continue through the Space's existing update actions.** `app/(main)/spaces/[slug]/setup/page.tsx` is a Server Component gated on `resolveSpaceManageAccess` (canManage only: a staff previewer is read-only and is denied rather than shown a form it cannot submit) and `isConsoleSpaceType`, notFound otherwise. `space-setup-spark.tsx` composes `SparkShell`, the in-shell twin of `WizardShell` (same `WizardProgress` and button vocabulary; `WizardShell` supplies its own full-screen canvas for onboarding outside the app shell, and this route renders inside it). Story writes `updateSpaceProfile` (tagline, about) and `setSpaceBusinessInfo` (the story); Identity writes `updateSpaceProfile` (display name) and saves the logo and the accent the moment they resolve through `setSpaceImages`, `uploadSpaceImage` and `setSpaceAccent`, exactly as the Identity and Branding form does; Contact and links and Offerings write `setSpaceBusinessInfo`. Nothing is duplicated and leaving mid-way loses nothing. Autosave is off (`draftScopeKey={null}`): every step already saves, so a resumed local draft could only argue with the saved truth. The finish lands on `/spaces/<slug>/manage/circles`; "Skip setup for now" goes there too.
3. **Vera drafts what the manifest says she drafts, through the seam that already exists, and never blocks.** The tagline and the story carry a "Draft with Vera" button calling `suggestTaglineAction` and `draftSpaceBioAction` (app/(main)/spaces/copilot-actions.ts over lib/ai/space-copilot.ts, which falls back to plain text when AI is off). A draft fills the field for the member to edit and is saved only when they continue. The short About and the offering blurbs are marked `veraDrafts` too but have no seam today; they are typed. **What stays staff-only:** website research. No URL door opens here; the Business Seeder keeps it.
4. **"First things" offers two doors, each skippable.** "Create your first Circle" links to the existing Circle wizard (`/circles/new`); "Add your first Event" opens the Event Spark hosted by this Space (`/events/new?space=<id>`). Both are links into flows that already exist; the setup builds neither.
5. **Older Spaces get one door, not a menu item.** `ProfileCompletenessCard` takes `setupHref` and shows "Finish setting up" while the score is under 100; the identity editor passes it for console types. The admin and Space menu catalogs are untouched (docs/MENU-CONTRACT.md).
6. **The two probes that read the redirect line move with the landing.** LIVE-261's grepped it for `/manage/circles`, which the amended line no longer carries; it now runs `scripts/probes/space-setup.mjs`, which asserts the finish href and the non-console landing both still say `/manage/circles`. PROG-R9's clause 7.4 did the same grep; it now checks that `createSpace` redirects through `postCreateLanding` and that the setup rule still finishes on hosting. Both rows keep measuring their consequence (a first run that lands on hosting, never the CRM), not the old spelling of it.

**Rejected.** Reusing the Business Seeder for members (its door is a website, which is the research path the manifest refuses; its output is a provenance ledger a member's own facts do not need). A client-state modal over the Circles manager (ADR-1017: a create surface is a URL). Writing a Space draft table for the walk (every step already has a column or a `profileData` key to land in). Asking the name and handle again (creation settled them; `spaceSetupFields` drops `spark` fields).

**Consequences.** `lib/spaces/setup.test.ts` pins the step order, the dropped-section rule, the spark exclusion and the landing per type; `lib/spaces/provision.test.ts` pins that the happy path redirects into setup. `scripts/probes/space-setup.mjs` runs the real rule and reads the seams. `docs/STUDIO.md`'s "Wizards on the kit" cell no longer says "none yet".
