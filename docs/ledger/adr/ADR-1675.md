## ADR-1675: The Practitioner persona promises what a practitioner can sell today, not "paywalled Programs" (LIVE-709)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-30 · backlog `LIVE-709` (closed here) · owner ruling 2026-09-29 ("Practitioner re-scope", OWN-086, recorded by [ADR-1624](DECISIONS.md)) · re-tested first per [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md) · amends the Practitioner row of [ADR-163](DECISIONS.md) System 2 as written in `docs/ROLES.md` · builds on [ADR-914](DECISIONS.md) (selling is not a tier), [ADR-1397](DECISIONS.md) (only a paid Space may price a Journey) and [ADR-1474](DECISIONS.md) (journeys is a money path) · no migration

**Context.** The Practitioner partner card on `/partners/join` (`PERSONA_META.practitioner` in `lib/personas-core.ts`) read "Host paywalled Programs + gamify your clients' progress, with a private Channel & Circles under the Frequency brand", and its tools were `[]`, so a practitioner the team verified had nothing to press. The intake persona (`lib/onboarding/personas.ts`) opened with "Host your programs: spin up a program in minutes". Re-tested on `main` at `f7f7d3075`: the row's premise said Programs were retired, and that is only half true. [ADR-864](DECISIONS.md) brought the word back as a Business Space's Channel with a Chapter blueprint, which a Space runs from `/spaces/<slug>/settings/program`. But nothing sells one. There is no price on a Program, no product kind for one, no checkout, and Programs are not one of the six money paths (`PayoutChannel` in `lib/billing/payout-prompt.ts`). So the promise still named a thing nobody can sell, and "in minutes" was untrue of a feature that needs the Business plan.

**Decision.**

1. **The Practitioner promise names only live money paths, each with the rule it runs on.** Tickets to your events and products in the Market from any account (ADR-914). Paid bookings and memberships from a Space. Journeys from a paid Space (ADR-1397). The words are the canon's: Market, Space, Journey ([NAMING.md](NAMING.md) §Commerce and §Money in).
2. **The card's tools open the real doors:** Host an event (`/events/new`), List a product (`/market/sell`), Open a Space (`/spaces/new`) and Receive payments (`/settings#payouts`, the canon's account-menu name for payout setup). They light up on verification, as every partner tool does.
3. **The intake persona says the same thing in its own voice.** The first reel beat is "Get paid for your craft" and the first track line is "Sell tickets, bookings and Journeys". The following beats (a following that comes back, a free Space on the calendar) were already true and stay.
4. **What is not built is not promised.** Client gamification and a private Frequency-branded Channel for a practitioner are not built. They leave the promise and stay as planned work in `docs/BUILD-LIST.md` 3.3.
5. **A test holds it.** `lib/personas-practitioner-promise.test.ts` fails if "paywalled Programs" or "paid Programs" reappears in any persona copy (partner cards, intake personas, persona tags), if the Practitioner copy names a Program or a paywall at all, if the promise stops naming a money path that `lib/billing/payout-prompt.ts` declares, or if a Practitioner tool opens no page.

**Rejected.** Building a paywall on Programs so the old promise comes true (the owner ruled a re-scope, and gating a Chapter is a product call nobody has made). Keeping "Programs" without "paywalled" (a practitioner reads it as the thing they sell, and a Program is a Business Space's Channel, not an offering). Linking only `/spaces/new` (tickets and Market products need no Space, and hiding that pushes a practitioner towards a setup they may not need).

**Consequences.** A practitioner who claims the persona reads what they can sell today and, once verified, gets four tools that each open a real page. `docs/ROLES.md`, `docs/LEAD-FLOWS.md` (which also still said "worldwide marketplace") and the `persona_practitioner` tag description match. No help article carried the promise, so none changed. `CONNECT_WIRED` and the Practitioner's place in `MONEY_PERSONAS` are unchanged.

**Rows.** `LIVE-709` (closed here).
