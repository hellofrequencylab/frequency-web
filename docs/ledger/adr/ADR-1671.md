## ADR-1671: The HYG-149 and HYG-153 probes count their own files in-process instead of spawning the whole adoption ratchet (HYG-155)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-30 · backlog `HYG-155` (filed and closed here) · follows the LIVE-034 probe-cost pattern · no migration

**Context.** HYG-149 and HYG-153 closed with `node scripts/check-adoption.mjs` as their probe. That script reads and comment-strips all 5,061 source files under `app`, `components` and `lib` on every spawn, which costs 2.5 to 4.9 s of CPU depending on the runner (`stripComments` alone is about 1.2 s locally). `scripts/backlog-contract.test.ts` holds every probe in the full sweep under a 4.5 s per-probe ceiling, and says raising it is not the fix. On 2026-09-30 from 01:07Z the `test` job failed that assertion on #3079, #3086, #3081 and #3073 (HYG-149 at 4.86 s), none of which touches the adoption ratchet.

**Decision.**

1. **Each probe measures its own row, in-process.** Both import `loadConfig`, `stripComments` and `countEntry` from `scripts/check-adoption.mjs`, so the counting is the ratchet's own and cannot drift from it.
2. **HYG-149** counts the five classes it fixed over the ten files its detail attributes, and holds each at its count on main after the fix (literal-radius 23, raw-button-bg 1, raw-input 0, subtle-tiny-type 0, literal-display-type 0). At `8b0b1909e`, before the fix, it reads 32, 3, 3, 1 and 2 and fails. A moved or deleted file throws and fails.
3. **HYG-153** requires `components/push/install-card.tsx` to carry its stated exclude reason, and counts `bespoke-cards` over its own `*-card.tsx` scope against the entry's baseline. A walk that finds fewer than 20 card files fails rather than passing on nothing. At `b9aff5df2` it fails.
4. **The whole-corpus ratchet keeps its homes:** the advisory step in `ci.yml` on every PR and the weekly maintenance sweep on main. No ceiling or baseline moved.

**Rejected.** Raising the per-probe ceiling (the test forbids it, and it would hide the next expensive probe). Making `stripComments` faster (a change to the blanker moves the corpus fingerprint of every adoption baseline, which is a larger change than the failure asks for). Keeping one probe on the whole ratchet (a single spawn already exceeded the ceiling on a slow runner).

**Consequences.** The two probes cost 73 ms and 127 ms wall locally, where each cost seconds. A regression in the whole ratchet outside those files is no longer a done-row regression. It still shows in the advisory scoreboard on every PR and in the weekly sweep, which is where HYG-129 put whole-corpus findings with no author on the PR.

**Rows.** `HYG-155` (filed and closed here).
