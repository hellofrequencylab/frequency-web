## ADR-1738: "Build circles, not walls." is the core belief, "What's happening" replaces "What's on", and the public site's sides read Browse Community and Host Community

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-13 · backlog `LIVE-918` · owner-ruled · amends [ADR-1023](DECISIONS.md) (the Circle tab's label, not its route) · beside [ADR-944](DECISIONS.md) (the tagline, unchanged)

**Context.** The Frequency design system handoff of 2026-10-13 (the "Frequency site frontend setup" project) changed the messaging canon in the design kit: the core belief "Circles, not walls." became "Build circles, not walls."; "What's on" became "What's happening" in all nav, menus, labels and copy; the marketing side toggle "Reading as: People / Experiences" became "Browse Community / Host Community"; and the hero headlines moved to search terms ("Find local groups and events near you.", "Host local events and groups people come back to."). The owner ruled the same day: "Change all the naming now in the docs."

**Decision.** (1) docs/NAMING.md gains one section holding all four: the belief line, the label, the toggle words, and what each retires. (2) The belief line is a belief line, not the tagline: `SITE_TAGLINE` stays "Community Collective" (ADR-944), because the handoff names it the core belief and the tagline feeds the logo lockup, the OG card, the `<title>`, llms.txt and the email footer, none of which this round touched. (3) "What's happening" is sentence case in copy and menus and "What's Happening" as the Circle tab, beside Circle Stats. The `/whats-on` route stays, so no saved link breaks; route ≠ label is already twice precedented (ADR-1023). (4) "What's on your mind?" is a composer prompt, not the label, and stays; NAMING.md carries the collision guard. (5) docs/CONTENT-VOICE.md §7d gains the rule the headlines follow: lead with the words people search for and ask for no weekly commitment. (6) The code follows in the same change: the nine member-facing strings that said the old label now say the new one, held by `LIVE-918` and `scripts/probes/whats-happening-label.mjs`, which reads string literals and JSX text and fails if the old label comes back.

**Rejected.** Renaming the `/whats-on` route (it breaks every shared Circle link to gain a URL nobody reads). Replacing `SITE_TAGLINE` with the belief line (a different job, and an owner call the handoff did not make). Making "What's happening" a named calendar surface in NAMING.md, as the handoff offers as optional (no surface carries that name yet).

**Consequences.** Canon and code agree from this change, and the probe holds them together on every `check:backlog` run. Past ADRs that quote "What's On" (ADR-1023 and the rail notes after it) are history and are not edited.
