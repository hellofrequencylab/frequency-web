## ADR-1691: A paid split order receipts the buyer with the lines grouped by seller, and tells each seller the transfer ledger pays about their share only (LIVE-706)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-30 · backlog `LIVE-706` · re-tested first per [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md) · builds on [ADR-1614](DECISIONS.md) (the transfer ledger, LIVE-622) and the LIVE-344 receipt seam · no migration

**Context.** Re-tested on `main` at `7cba7d186`. `recordCommerceOrderFromSession` flips a split order (`owner_kind` `split`, `funds_flow` `separate`, LIVE-621) to paid, runs `settleSplitOrderTransfers` (one `commerce_order_transfers` row per seller share), then calls `sendOrderReceipts`. There, `resolveSeller` returned null for `split`, so the buyer's receipt said "Your order from Frequency" with the line "Frequency can see the order now and will send it on", which is false: each seller ships their own lines and Frequency ships none. The seller half then logged "no seller to notify for a paid order" and returned, so no seller heard about the sale. The row's probe failed on `main`.

**Decision.**

1. **A split order has its own half.** `sendOrderReceipts` routes `ownerKind === 'split'` to `sendSplitOrderReceipts` before anything else runs. The single-seller path below that line is unchanged, and a test pins the two messages it sends against the exact text `main` rendered, and that it never reads the transfer ledger.
2. **Who is notified is the ledger's answer.** Each row `listOrderTransfers` returns gets one `notifyEarner` (one bell row, one email), addressed as the single-seller notice addresses a Space or a member. Its figures come from that row only: "Your sale" is the share's gross (`amount_cents + platform_fee_cents`), "Network fee" is its `platform_fee_cents` (dropped when 0), and "You receive" is `amount_cents`, which is what the transfer pays. It lists only that seller's lines and says the order had items from other sellers, which they send.
3. **Which lines are whose is the items' answer.** The order's items are read with their product's owner and grouped by `sellerKey`, the same key the checkout and the ledger plan on. The buyer's receipt puts each seller's lines and their price under the seller's name, then the total, and says each seller sends their own part, so it may arrive in more than one delivery. A seller the ledger pays whose lines could not be read is still named with their share's gross; a line whose product can no longer be read is listed under "Also in this order".
4. **Idempotency is the same settle flip as before.** The receipts run only for a row this delivery flipped `pending` to `paid`, after the settle has planned the transfer rows. A second settle or a redelivered webhook flips nothing and sends nothing; the wiring test pins the order (transfers, then receipts) and the replay.
5. **Every miss is loud and none costs the buyer's receipt.** An unreadable ledger (`listOrderTransfers` throws rather than read as "nobody") and a split order with no rows each log that no seller was notified. A row whose seller cannot be resolved logs and the loop moves to the next seller.

**Rejected.** Planning the shares again from `metadata.split` when the ledger has no rows (the row's contract is the ledger; the reconciler plans a missed order later, and a second source of "who is owed" is the drift ADR-1614 exists to prevent). Adding `funds_flow` to `SettledOrder` (the schema ties `owner_kind` `split` to `funds_flow` `separate` both ways, and it would widen the settle's diff beside LIVE-705).

**Consequences.** A share whose fee took its whole gross gets no transfer row, so that seller's lines are on the buyer's receipt and no notice goes; no rung prices a fee that high today. The seller notice's "Open Orders" button lands on the seller's usual console; seeing a split order there is LIVE-624's, and marking it sent is LIVE-705's, so this notice does not promise "mark it sent". Help: `content/help/connecting/marketplace.md` says a cart can hold more than one seller's items and the receipt lists them by seller; a CHANGELOG line.

**Rows.** `LIVE-706` (closed here).
