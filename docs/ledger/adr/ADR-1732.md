## ADR-1732: Every Space gets a public address at its Space; a paid plan may use its own domain; the signed alias still binds a thread underneath

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-12 · backlog `EMAIL-022` · builds on [ADR-812](DECISIONS.md) (the ticketed reply loop) and the EMAIL-011/012/013 packets · migration `20270346008400_space_inbox_addresses.sql`

**Context.** The owner ruled on 2026-10-12: "Every account: if they message through Frequency they can have a reply address at their Space, like hello@<slug>.frequencylocal.com. If they have premium, they can use their own email (own domain)." Before this, a Space on a free plan sent every conversation from the Frequency sender, a paid Space could send from its own verified domain after a manual DNS wizard (EMAIL-011/012, `lib/spaces/email-domain-onboarding.ts`), and the only address a Space had that could receive was the hidden per-thread alias `<slug>.r2.<key>.<hmac>@<reply domain>` (EMAIL-013, `lib/comms/tenant-reply-alias.ts`), which exists to bind one reply to one conversation and is never shown. Nobody could write TO a Space. Resend receives mail for every address on a verified domain once that domain's MX points at it, each subdomain is verified on its own, and the platform already runs the `frequencylocal.com` zone at Vercel for Space websites (LIVE-782), so the missing piece was one button, not a product.

**Decision.** (1) **Three addresses, three jobs.** The **Space inbox address** `hello@<slug>.<SPACE_EMAIL_INBOX_ROOT>` is the public door, for every plan: people write to it, and once verified the Space's conversations go out from it and it is the recommended Reply-To. The **own domain** stays the paid plan's sender (EMAIL-011/012, rule unchanged: `resolveSpaceEmailDefaultIdentity` returns a custom identity only on a paid plan with the entitlement) and wins over the inbox address when chosen. The **signed alias** (EMAIL-013) still rides underneath as the Reply-To on a thread that has one: it is what binds a reply to its exact conversation, and it is never the public address. (2) **Provisioning is platform work, not owner work.** `provisionSpaceInboxAddress` (`lib/comms/space-inbox-address.ts`) creates the `<slug>.<root>` domain at Resend with receiving on, writes the MX, SPF and DKIM records it returns into the root zone through the same Vercel client the website domains use (`upsertZoneDnsRecords`), stores the row before DNS so a DNS fault never loses the provider domain, and reports a state. It is idempotent (an existing provider domain is reused, records are re-written safely, a verified address is only reported) and never throws to the caller. (3) **Inbound routes on the root alone, before the alias.** A message whose one Space-address recipient matches a `verified` row opens a NEW conversation in that Space (`openOrGetConversation` with `forceNew`, because the sender address is a claim and must never adopt an existing thread), with the sender as a contact of that Space (created with consent `unknown`, source `space_inbox`, never mass-mailable by that fact). An unknown, pending, failed or paused address quarantines through the EMAIL-013 store (`inbox_address_unknown`, `inbox_address_unverified`), never the contact-match fallback. (4) **Two switches.** `SPACE_INBOX_ADDRESSES_ENABLED` (envFlag) turns on provisioning, the Settings card and the outbound From; `SPACE_EMAIL_INBOX_ROOT` names the zone. Routing reads only the root, so an address that was verified keeps receiving when the switch is turned off, the same posture EMAIL-013 takes with issued aliases. A `paused` row stops both halves for one Space.

**Rejected.** Receiving on the apex or on `reply.` for every Space (the apex MX is the humans' mailbox and `reply.` is the alias domain; a per-Space subdomain keeps each Space's records and reputation its own). Using the inbox address for campaigns from day one (campaigns keep the Frequency or own-domain sender until the address carries a reputation; the fallback is conversation-only). Reusing an open thread for a second mail from the same sender (the From header is not an identity; the alias path has the alias, this path has nothing). Gating the inbox address by plan (the ruling says every account).

**Consequences.** New table `space_inbox_addresses` (service role only, one per Space, one per domain, bounded states). `space_email_reply_quarantine.reason` admits the two inbox reasons. `resolveSpaceEmailDefaultIdentity` now returns the inbox From for conversations when nothing custom is set, on every plan, which `resolveSpaceConversationFrom` already consumes. The own-domain door on Space Settings › Email is confirmed and a free plan reads "Use your own email address on a paid plan" with the billing link. The probe `scripts/probes/space-inbox-address.mjs` holds the rules; the switch stays off until the EMAIL-022 pilot in `docs/RUNBOOKS.md`.
