## ADR-1683: The member CRM screens get their NAMING and CONTENT-VOICE pass: a contact is a contact, Hosts not stewards, Members not Community, Community Resonance not "Space CRM" (LIVE-660)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-30 · backlog `LIVE-660` (closed here) · re-tested first per [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md) · applies [NAMING.md](NAMING.md) §Connection layer and §CRM / messaging surfaces and [CONTENT-VOICE.md](CONTENT-VOICE.md) §3, §5 · copy only, no behaviour change, no migration

**Context.** LIVE-660 said the member-facing CRM screens never had the copy pass the rest of the product got. Re-tested on `main` at `451c500f0`: the premise held. The surfaces read, in member copy, "steward" (an internal role word no member is ever told they are) on the Network visibility choice, its helper text and the shared-contact page; "Space CRM" in the prompt on My Contacts, where NAMING.md names a Space's CRM **Community Resonance**; "harvest someone's details" in the empty state and "harvests every detail" on the scanner; a contact called a "profile" in four places (the **New profile** button twice, **No profiles yet**, **Save profile**) and a back link called **Profiles** on two pages, while the page they return to is titled **My Contacts** and a Frequency profile is a different thing entirely; a Title Case **QR Scan** tab beside a sentence-case "QR scan" source label; and a Network hub tab labelled **Community** for the directory NAMING.md (ADR-868) names **Members**, beside a **Contacts** tab that lands on a page titled My Contacts. No member-facing string carried an em dash; every one on these files was in a comment. The capture doors (`/checkin`, `/exchange`, `/intro`, `/unlock`) were already in voice and are unchanged.

**Decision.**

1. **A contact is a contact.** The primary action reads **Add a contact** (the button on My Contacts, the empty state's action, the creator's title), the empty state **No contacts yet**, the save button **Save contact**, and the save error "Could not save this contact. Try again." The back link on a contact and on the creator reads **My Contacts**, the name of where it goes. "Profile" stays for a member's Frequency profile only.
2. **Hosts, not stewards.** Every member-facing "steward" on these screens becomes **Host** (NAMING.md's host+ ladder), in the Network visibility option ("Network (local Hosts can find them)"), the Sharing helper text, and the read-only shared-contact page. The code keeps `connectionsOwnerId` and its steward comments: the word is internal.
3. **Canon names.** The prompt on My Contacts names **Community Resonance** and links **See Spaces**. The Network hub tabs read **Members · Friends · My Contacts**, the names of their nav rows in `lib/nav-areas.ts`, and `/network`'s coded fallback title becomes **Members** so the tab and the page it opens agree (an operator override still wins). The chip for a contact promoted into the shared list reads **In Frequency contacts**, and the confirm button **Add to Frequency contacts**, so neither reads as "in My Contacts". "Lead" leaves the promote copy ("marked unconfirmed").
4. **Plain verbs.** "Harvest" goes ("their details land here in a few seconds", "picks up every detail"), "parse" becomes "make sense of", and the Crew tease drops "pipeline". The QR tab is sentence case, and the private contact card on a member's profile learns the `qr_scan` source it was printing raw.
5. **Help follows the code.** `content/help/connecting/save-by-scanning.md` names the **QR scan** tab and `find-people.md` the **Members** directory.

**Consequences.** The row's probe walks every non-test source file under `app/(main)/network/contacts`, `app/(main)/connections`, `app/(capture)` and `components/connections`, plus the hub tabs and the capture form and shell, strips comments, and fails on an em dash, "steward", "Space CRM", "harvest", a contact called a profile, "QR Scan" or a Community tab; it then requires the tabs to match the nav rows, the page to say My Contacts / Add a contact / No contacts yet, the prompt to name Community Resonance, both back links to say My Contacts, and `/network` to fall back to Members. It fails on `main` (at the "harvest" arm) and passes here. `/network`'s operator-editable description and the Space help article's "Space Contacts" wording are outside this row.

**Rows.** `LIVE-660` (closed here).
