## ADR-1735: Launch marketing rulings: category line, end of comped Crew, Non Profit billing, the Suite Builder

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-13 · plan [`docs/LAUNCH-MARKETING-PLAN.md`](LAUNCH-MARKETING-PLAN.md) §12 · amends [ADR-944](DECISIONS.md) (tagline use), [ADR-1703](DECISIONS.md) §3 (the comped-Crew flip) and the setup-preset rule 2 of [ADR-1294](DECISIONS.md) / OFFER-MODEL §3

**Context.** The launch marketing audit of 2026-10-13 (eight specialist auditors, `.claude/agents/`) found five category names in use, every member comped to Crew with no end date, verified nonprofits granted their plan free, a setup-preset rule that hides Contacts, Email and Shop from new businesses, and feature grouping built as category pickers rather than a recommendation. The owner ruled on the eight open questions the plan listed.

**Decision.**
1. **One category line:** "Local groups that keep meeting." is the category line in metadata, page H1s and llms.txt. "Community Collective" is kept as the logo lockup descriptor only and is no longer the explanation of what Frequency is.
2. **Comped Crew ends at public launch.** `BETA_OPEN_ACCESS` and `BETA_MEMBERS_GET_CREW` turn off at launch. Members comped during beta keep Crew for 60 days, then see the contribute-what-you-want picker.
3. **Non Profit is charged.** New verifications unlock the $39 Non Profit checkout instead of a free grant. Spaces already granted keep the plan free until a date set in the implementing row.
4. **Setup rule 2 is amended.** A suite or bundle may switch on `crm`, `email` and `shop`, which are metered, not gated. `program` stays Business depth and may not be switched on by a bundle. A bundle still never touches money.
5. **"Add when you're ready" tools start off**, one switch away, for every operator. The weights are revisited after the first 20 real setups.
6. **The public Suite Builder is "Build your suite"** at `/build-your-suite` (checked against NAMING when it ships). Its CTA is "Build your suite".
7. **Home headline direction:** "Your people are closer than you think." with the subhead "Small groups that meet every week, near you. Join free. Businesses host free." The other §7.2 rewrites are the starting draft.
8. **Crew is positioned as support first:** "Back Frequency", with the Supporter mark, a monthly Boost and uncapped Vera; the host kit is a bonus. Members' hosting asks point at a free Space first.

**Rejected.** Keeping comped Crew after launch (it leaves the Member to Crew rung with no buyer). Free Non Profit as a rung (it earns nothing and contradicts the published $39 price). Turning every tool on by default (the sort would carry no meaning).

**Consequences.** Each ruling is implemented by its own backlog row in the phase the plan assigns it (§11). Ruling 2 changes `lib/core/beta.ts` and `lib/onboarding/funnel-script.ts` and needs a grace-period mechanism for existing comps. Ruling 3 changes `lib/spaces/nonprofit-verification.ts` and the billing page. Ruling 4 changes `lib/pricing/bundles.ts` and OFFER-MODEL §3. Ruling 1 changes `lib/site.ts`, `app/llms.txt/route.ts` and `NAMING.md`.
