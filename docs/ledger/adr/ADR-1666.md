## ADR-1666: A member's Vault stats say whether they earned more Zaps this week than last, summed from the ledger at read time, with no snapshot table and no red arrow (LIVE-685)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · backlog `LIVE-685` (filed out of `DEF-DOMAIN` by the owner replan, [ADR-1624](DECISIONS.md)) · re-tested first per [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md) · numbered 1666 by the orchestrator's assignment · no migration

**Context.** The Crew gamification spec deferred a week-over-week delta to "Phase 4", and the row proposed a weekly snapshot (a cron row per member) to compare against. Re-tested on `main` at `2d3fd02f2`: nothing under `app/(main)/crew`, `lib/economy` or `lib/quest` tells a member how this week compares with last. The closest thing is the Circle board (`lib/quest/effort.ts`), which compares a member's last 7 days with the median of their four weeks before, per circle, and never on the member's own stat row. The member's stat row is the Vault's (`/crew/store/ledger`, "How you earned"): four `StatCard`s, season Zaps first.

**Decision.**

1. **Read time, not a snapshot.** `zap_transactions` is an append-only ledger of `profile_id · amount · created_at`, indexed on `profile_id`. A member's last 14 days is a small indexed read on a page that already reads that table. A snapshot table plus a cron would be a second copy of a number the ledger already produces, and a copy that drifts the first time a row is corrected. No migration.
2. **The Circle board's windows.** `lib/economy/week-over-week.ts` (pure) sums "this week" (the last 7 × 24 hours) and "last week" (the 7 days before) with `weekBucket` from `lib/quest/effort.ts`, so the two surfaces never disagree about what a week is. Rolling windows compare two whole weeks on every day; a calendar week would read every Monday morning as a drop. Non-positive rows (corrections, clawbacks) are not effort and are skipped, the board's rule.
3. **Zaps, not Gems.** Zaps are the Quest's measure of showing up (season standing is Zaps), and the card the comparison hangs on is the Zaps card.
4. **One tile, not a fifth.** The Zaps · season card keeps its value and gains a detail line ("N this week") and a delta. The stat grid stays four across, so nothing wraps into an orphan tile.
5. **Calm copy, never a deficit** ([CONTENT-VOICE](CONTENT-VOICE.md), "Warm"). A busier week reads "N more than last week" with the up arrow. An equal week reads "Same as last week". A lighter week reads "Last week: N", flat: no red, no down arrow, no "less" or "behind". The member sees both numbers and draws their own conclusion.
6. **Fail quiet.** `getZapWeeks` (in `lib/economy/ledger.ts`, user-scoped client, RLS owner-only) returns null on a read error, and the card then shows no comparison rather than a false zero.

**Rejected.** The weekly snapshot table and cron the row proposed (a copy of derivable data; it would also need a backfill before the first comparison could show). A calendar-week window. A fifth StatCard. A red "down" delta for a lighter week.

**Consequences.** A member opening The Vault sees this week's Zaps and how they compare with last week's. The read is one extra indexed query on that page only (not the shell, not a layout). `lib/economy/week-over-week.test.ts` pins the windows, the correction rule and the three copy arms; the row's probe runs the real module and reads the page's wiring, and fails on `main`.

**Rows.** `LIVE-685` (closed here).
