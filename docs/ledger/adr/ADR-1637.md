## ADR-1637: The packed-cache ratio is 0.55, still one whole-archive number, rounded up from the 2026-09-29 pair that under-read (HYG-136)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · backlog `HYG-136` (closed here, filed by `HYG-135`) · re-derives the constant [ADR-1113](DECISIONS.md) settled at 0.53 · does not raise Vercel's 1.50 GB ceiling or the 0.10 GB reserve ([DEPLOY-SAFETY.md](DEPLOY-SAFETY.md) §10, rule 2) · the compiler-cache growth that makes the trim fire often stays `HYG-140`

**Context.** `check:cache-budget` can only measure raw bytes. Vercel weighs the packed archive against a 1.50 GB ceiling and discards the whole cache, `node_modules` included, above that. The conversion is one constant, `PACKED_PER_RAW`, last settled on 2026-08-19 from a build that printed both halves (raw 2.513 GB, packed 1.32 GB ⇒ 0.5254, rounded up to 0.53). The file's own rule is that under-reading is the dangerous direction: a late trim means Vercel rejects a cache that should have been trimmed.

HYG-136 was filed from a 2026-09-29 production pair: the gate estimated 1.38 GB and Vercel uploaded 1.42 GB. At 0.53 that implies raw 1.38 / 0.53 = 2.604 GB and a true ratio of 1.42 / 2.604 = 0.5454. The real archive had already passed the 1.40 GB trim point. Headroom against the ceiling was 0.08 GB. The mix had moved: `node_modules` held near 959 MiB while the turbopack cache grew, which is the compiler-heavy mix the constant is supposed to describe.

HYG-015's done probe still required the constant to sit in 0.52–0.54, the 2026-08-24 band. Raising past 0.54 without re-pointing that probe would make a closed row fail `check:backlog`.

**Decision.**

1. **`PACKED_PER_RAW = 0.55`.** Re-derived from the 2026-09-29 pair and rounded **up** from 0.5454, the same one-decimal-up rule that took 0.5254 to 0.53. Over-estimating costs a cold compile. Under-estimating loses the install. `PACKED_PER_RAW_MEASURED` is `2026-09-29`. The ceiling stays 1.50 GB and the reserve stays 0.10 GB.
2. **Still one whole-archive ratio.** `node_modules` and the Turbopack cache do not compress alike, and Vercel uploads one archive, so this gate cannot take two packed readings. Splitting the constant without those readings would be a number nobody can check. The mix moving is why 0.53 drifted; the remedy for trims that fire often is the compiler cache's growth (`HYG-140`), not a second ratio.
3. **HYG-015's band moves with the constant.** Its probe still requires a named `PACKED_PER_RAW`, the composition line that prints `node_modules` and `.next/cache` separately, and the instruction to compare against `Uploading build cache`. The two-sided band is now 0.54–0.56, around 0.55. Lowering toward 0.264 is still the original error. Reverting to 0.53 now fails both probes.

**Rejected.** Raising the ceiling or shrinking the reserve to make the 1.42 GB upload look green. Separate per-part ratios without two packed measurements. Leaving the constant at 0.53 because LIVE-175 closed on a resting mix that agreed within 1%: that close said re-open when a loaded mix misses by more than 1%, which this pair did.

**Consequences.** The same 2026-09-29 tree now estimates 2.604 × 0.55 = 1.43 GB, past the trim point, so the fail-safe fires before Vercel is handed 1.42 GB. `HYG-140` owns stopping the compiler cache from growing into that trim on every warm build. The next drift is still the pair this file prints: raw line beside `Uploading build cache`.
