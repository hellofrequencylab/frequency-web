## ADR-1863: Four launch doors, and "Decide later" in onboarding (LIVE-1130)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-22 · backlog `LIVE-1130`

**Context.** Daniel narrowed the 1 December North County launch to four niches: coaches and healers, studios, event hosts, and nonprofits. The code still served six `/for` doors, so two (community builders, teachers) competed with the four on the nav, the pricing doors and the funnel destinations. Sign-up and Create a Space also had no visible way to pass on a choice.

**Decision.** Retire the community-builders and teachers doors. `/for/community-builders` redirects to `/for/event-hosts` and `/for/teachers` to `/for/coaches-and-healers` (permanent). Their funnel configs, pricing doors, nav rows and Space-create destination rows go. A stale `?seq=communities` or `teachers` falls back to the general landing. The sign-up fork and the Create-a-Space goals step each get a "Decide later" choice that clears the picks and keeps the default persona and general setup. No new admin screens: funnels stay under Growth and Campaigns, onboarding under Onboarding controls.

**Rejected.** A new niche or funnel admin (the existing Funnels, Campaigns, Persona nurture and Onboarding controls pages already cover it). Deleting the Space Modes behind the two doors (other Spaces still use them).

**Consequences.** No migration. Old inbound links keep working through the redirects. The mobile-menu pin test lost the community-builders row because that nav entry is gone.
