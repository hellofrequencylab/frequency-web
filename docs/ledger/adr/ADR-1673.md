## ADR-1673: The Space email acceptable-use policy ships as Frequency's live policy without counsel review, linked from the Turn on email card (LIVE-729)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-30 · backlog `LIVE-729` (filed and closed here) · owner ruling on `OWN-085`, 2026-09-30: "Ship without counsel review" · supersedes decision 2 and amends decision 6 of [ADR-1658](DECISIONS.md) (`LIVE-707`) · re-tested first per [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md) · no migration

**Context.** LIVE-707 (ADR-1658, #3066) drafted a plain-language acceptable-use policy for Space email at `app/space-email-policy/page.tsx` and kept it out of the product until a lawyer had read it: robots noindex and nofollow, absent from `app/sitemap.ts`, linked from nowhere, opening with "DRAFT. Not in force, and no lawyer has read it yet.", closing with eleven questions for counsel, and carrying one bracketed placeholder for the appeal reply time. ADR-1658 decision 6 said going live meant removing the banner and the questions, letting the page be indexed and sitemapped, and linking it from the Turn on email card. On 2026-09-30 the owner ruled on OWN-085: ship it without counsel review. Re-tested on `main` at `f7f7d3075`: the page, its robots, the sitemap, the card and the Terms were exactly as ADR-1658 left them. Two gaps the draft had listed as counsel questions have since closed in code: every commercial email prints the platform postal line (LIVE-728, ADR-1663) and a suspended Space sends nothing (LIVE-727, ADR-1662).

**Decision.**

1. **The page is Frequency's policy.** The draft banner, the "Draft for counsel review" eyebrow and the questions for counsel are removed. The page reads "Last updated: September 30, 2026", the date the ruling made it live.
2. **Indexed, like the other policies.** `robots: { index: true, follow: true }`, with its own `openGraph` and `twitter` blocks as `/privacy` and `/terms` carry, and an entry in `app/sitemap.ts` beside them. A published policy is public reference: a Space's recipients, and anyone checking what Frequency allows, should be able to find it. `check:seo` reads it as advertised and indexable.
3. **Draft-only wording is settled without inventing a commitment.** The prohibited list says "We may add to this list" instead of calling itself a draft. The appeal placeholder becomes "A person reads every appeal and writes back": the owner set no reply time, so the policy promises none.
4. **What the code now keeps is stated.** "Every email ends with Frequency Labs Holdings' postal address" (read from `PLATFORM_POSTAL_LINE`) and "A suspended Space sends no email at all" (read from `spaceEmailHold`). The page test pins each against its module, beside the daily cap, the complaint line, the admin default and the import consent state.
5. **The Turn on email card links it, and adds no consent step.** Above the checkbox: "Read the Space email policy before you turn it on." The link opens in a new tab so the card keeps its state. The checkbox, its words and `setSpaceEmailEnabled` are unchanged; nothing new is recorded. ADR-1658 specified a link and no acceptance step (acceptance by name with a date was one of the questions for counsel, not a decision), so none is added.
6. **The Terms point at it.** Section 4 (acceptable use) of `app/terms/page.tsx` says a Space that sends email is also bound by the Space email policy, and the Terms' Last updated moves to September 30, 2026. There is no separate policies index page; the footers' Legal links are unchanged.
7. **LIVE-707's probe is narrowed, not deleted.** It asserted the hidden-draft state this change retires. Its fragment now measures what LIVE-707 built and still holds: the page and its rule sections, no em or en dash, and the cap and complaint line read from the code. LIVE-729's probe carries the live-state arms.

**Rejected.** Keeping the page noindex while linking it from the card (a live policy that asks search engines to ignore it reads as something to hide, and `check:seo` would then list it as consciously excluded for no reason). Requiring the owner to tick a second box that names the policy, or recording the date they agreed (a new consent flow nobody specified; it would need a migration and a decision on Spaces that already have email on). Adding it to the member and marketing footers' Legal column (a nav change on every page for a policy only Space owners act on; the card and the Terms reach the people it binds). Promising an appeal reply time (the owner has not set one). Closing OWN-085 here (another PR does).

**Consequences.** Space owners see the rules before they turn email on, and the rules are the ones the code keeps. The page is crawlable and in the sitemap. No schema, action or consent record changes. If the owner later wants acceptance by name, or counsel reviews the policy, that is a new row.

**Rows.** `LIVE-729` (filed and closed here). `LIVE-707` probe amended (fragment). `OWN-085` untouched. Tests: `app/space-email-policy/page.test.tsx`, `components/spaces/email/email-enable-card.test.tsx` (new).
