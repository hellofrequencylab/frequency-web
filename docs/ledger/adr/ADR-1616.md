## ADR-1616: A seller sees their share of a split order where they see every other order, and the operator sees each transfer's state and can send a stuck one again (LIVE-624)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · `LIVE-624` (child 4 of 4 of `PROG-D8`, closed here) · builds on [ADR-1614](DECISIONS.md) (the transfer ledger, whose §7 named the operator retry) and [ADR-1576](DECISIONS.md) (the funds-flow seam) · beside [ADR-1575](DECISIONS.md) (the seller's fulfilment door) and [ADR-1565](DECISIONS.md) (the pro rata rule) · numbered 1616 as assigned by the orchestrator of the 2026-09-29 afternoon wave, against main and every open pull request's head

**Context.** A split order (funds_flow `separate`) is written with owner_kind `split` and both owner ids null. Re-tested before writing (ADR-1082): `listOrdersForSeller` filters `owner_profile_id`, `listSpaceOrders` and `spaceEarningsSummary` filter `owner_space_id`, so a split order was invisible to every seller it pays and its whole amount sat under nobody's earnings. The operator's `/admin/marketplace/orders` read `listAllOrders` and knew nothing of `commerce_order_transfers`, so a transfer past the reconciler's ceiling was a `commerce.transfer.stuck` log line and nothing else. No migration: every read here is the service role reading a service-role table from a surface that already authorized its caller.

**Decision.**

1. **A seller's claim on a split order is their transfer row, and a seller read returns their view of it.** `listOrdersForSeller` and `listSpaceOrders` union the orders a seller owns with the split orders carrying a `commerce_order_transfers` row for them, matched on `owner_kind` AND the owner id (a Space row that also names a profile never lands in that profile's list). Each such order comes back as that seller's view (`sellerViewOfSplitOrder`): their lines only (a line's seller is its product's owner, now joined), `amountCents` and `platformFeeCents` replaced by their share's gross and fee, fulfilment recomputed from their lines, and `share` carrying gross, fee, net, reversed cents and the transfer state. Every existing sum over `amountCents` therefore adds the share and never the cart, and no other seller's lines, figures, ids or error leave the module on a seller read. An unreadable ledger is logged and leaves the owned orders on screen.
2. **`spaceEarningsSummary` sums the share.** A fourth arm beside tickets and gifts: gross is the transfer plus its fee, the fee is that fee, a refunded order moves the share to refunded, and a partial refund comes off the share pro rata to its part of the order (the ADR-1565 rule). Shares never count toward the network slice: a split order records one source for the whole cart, and that figure must never be overstated.
3. **The surfaces.** The Space Shop Orders tab and the maker console render `OrderShareNote` under a split order: "Your share of a N-seller order", the net after the fee, and where the payout stands (on its way, paid out, delayed and retrying, reversed). Its fulfilment control is read-only there, because `setOrderFulfillment` binds to the order's owner columns and a split order has none, so a door would only refuse. The buyer's `/orders` lists a split order's lines under each seller's name.
4. **The operator ledger and the retry.** `/admin/marketplace/orders` renders `OrderTransferLedger` under every split order, and only under a split order, at `[data-order-transfers]`: each row's seller, net, fee kept, attempts, transfer id, reversed cents, last error, and Stuck when an open row is at the attempt ceiling; an unreadable ledger says so and never reads as "no transfers". A planned or failed row carries `retryOrderTransferAction` (requireOperator), which calls `retryOrderTransfer` in `lib/commerce/transfer-retry.ts`. It refuses a landed row and a row of an order that is refunded or never completed, then, on a row at the ceiling, lifts `attempts` to ONE under the ceiling, compare-and-set on the value read and an open status, and hands the order to the same `executePlannedTransfers` the settle and the reconciler use. It never resets attempts to zero: the adoption lookup that stops a retry after Stripe's key window from paying twice runs only when attempts is above zero. `lib/commerce/transfers.ts` is unchanged.

**Rejected.** A member read policy on the ledger (a money table stays service role; the two seller reads are already authorized by their surfaces). Showing a seller the whole cart with their share beside it (the other sellers' figures are not theirs). Resetting a stuck row's attempts to zero (skips the adoption lookup). A second payment path for the operator (every payment still goes through `executePlannedTransfers`). Counting a share toward the network slice.

**Not here.** The seller's sale notice and the buyer's receipt email for a split order (lib/commerce/order-receipt.ts still sends the buyer half only), and a fulfilment door for a split order (whose single fulfilment status no one seller can move today); both are follow-ups. The refund button's pro rata wording belongs with the reversal itself (`LIVE-623`).
