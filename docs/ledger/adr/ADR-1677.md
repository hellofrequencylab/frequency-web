## ADR-1677: Doomscroll mode is dropped, not built, and nothing a member, a designer or a planner reads promises it any more (LIVE-672)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-30 · backlog `LIVE-672` (closed here, dropped) · re-tested first per [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md) · amends [ADR-155](DECISIONS.md) (its "Doomscroll mode (the release valve)" paragraph and the matching Consequences sentence are withdrawn; the Portal Loop principle stands) · no migration, no code

**Context.** ADR-155 promised a member-toggled Doomscroll mode that would strip every activation prompt and show only content, backlogged as a brand statement. It was never built. LIVE-672 (filed 2026-09-29 by the ADR-1624 replan) asked for it to ship. The owner ruled on 2026-09-30: **"DROP the feature; do not build it."** Re-tested on `main` at `f7f7d3075` before changing anything: no member-facing code, setting or copy offers the mode. Every `doomscroll` hit under `app/`, `components/`, `lib/` and `content/help/` is about the habit (the `/loneliness` pillar's "How do I quit doomscrolling?" section and FAQ, its `lib/page-editor/templates/loneliness.ts` spec, the `/llms.txt` entry, and the help guide "How to stop doomscrolling (without quitting your phone)"), and none of them names a mode, toggle or setting. The promise lived in three places people still read as current: `docs/ENGAGEMENT-MARKETING-ENGINE.md` (a paragraph presenting it as the plan, "Build: BACKLOG §F"), `design_handoff/BRIEF-04-MARKETING-AND-FUNNELS.md` (a positioning angle, "Not a content casino", whose whole pitch was the toggle), and the DAWN settings mockup `design_handoff/dawn/ui_kits/screens/settings.html` (a "Doomscroll mode" switch in the Quiet beat).

**Decision.**

1. **The feature is dropped.** There is no Doomscroll mode, no prompt-layer toggle and no `profiles.meta` flag for one. ADR-155's principle (optimize for activation, never dwell-time; every mechanic answers "does this serve activation, or just dwell?") is unchanged. Only the release-valve feature is withdrawn.
2. **The three live promises are removed.** The engagement doc's paragraph becomes a short note that the toggle was proposed and dropped, pointing here, so nobody reads ADR-155 and re-files it. The brief's "Not a content casino" row is deleted outright, since the angle had no pitch without the toggle; the Anti-feed and Screen-time inversion rows already carry the same stance. The mockup's switch row is deleted; the Quiet beat keeps Weekly digest and Quiet hours. Because BRIEF-04 is part of the orientation set sent to Claude Design, deleting its row is also the message to DAWN; the next outbound handoff should name the settings mockup change so a re-export does not bring the switch back.
3. **Copy about the habit stays.** Helping someone quit doomscrolling is a Seeker-track pillar ("Life after the feed", CONTENT-VOICE §7a) and promises no feature. The help guide, the `/loneliness` sections and FAQ, the persona line "I doomscroll and I hate it", and the llms.txt entry are untouched.
4. **History stays as written.** ADR-155's text in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, the superseded `docs/BACKLOG.md` §F item and the frozen 2026-08-17 census (`docs/TASK-INVENTORY-2026-08-17.md` §8e) are records of what was once planned and are not rewritten. The backlog row is what records the drop.
5. **The row closes as `done` with the ruling quoted.** `scripts/check-backlog.mjs` accepts only `open | done | parked | blocked`. `parked` means "later", which is the opposite of the ruling, so the terminal status is `done` and the detail says DROPPED.

**Rejected.** Building the mode (overruled). Parking the row (it would come back in every `pnpm backlog` run as work still owed). Adding a `dropped` status to the checker (a schema change to the one list for one row; the detail carries the ruling). Scrubbing every "doomscroll" word (it would delete the habit-quitting SEO and help content, which promises nothing). Rewriting ADR-155 in place (ADRs are amended by later ADRs, not edited).

**Consequences.** The row's probe scans every text file under `app/`, `components/`, `lib/`, `content/`, `public/`, `design_handoff/` and `docs/` plus the root `*.md` files (5,600 files, about 0.25 s CPU) for a Doomscroll mode, toggle, switch or setting, in any spacing or casing (`doomscrollMode` and `doomscroll_mode` included). It exempts only `docs/DECISIONS.md`, `docs/BUILD-BACKLOG.json`, `docs/ledger/`, the superseded `docs/BACKLOG.md` and the frozen census. It fails on `main` naming the three files above, passes after this change, passes when "How to stop doomscrolling" is added, and exits 79 (cannot look) when it finds fewer than 3,000 files. If the owner ever reverses the ruling, the new ADR supersedes this one and the probe is replaced, not loosened.

**Rows.** `LIVE-672` (closed here, dropped).
