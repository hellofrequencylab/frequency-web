## ADR-1651: The Space console works one-handed on a phone: the daily doors sit in a bottom thumb bar, every console control is a 44px target, and a tool's description no longer hides in a hover tooltip (LIVE-704)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · backlog `LIVE-704` (filed by the `HYG-138` owner replan, WM mobile-site lane) · re-tested first per [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md) · numbered 1651 by the orchestrator's assignment · no migration

**Context.** MOBILE-NAV-PLAN §3 deferred "a thumb-friendly console pass" for the Space `/manage` and `/settings/*` consoles and no row owned it until `HYG-138`. Re-tested on `main` at `fb91c7b48`, by rendering the real `SpaceManageConsole` with every tool on and measuring it in Chromium under an iPhone profile (coarse pointer) at 320, 390 and 430px. Nothing ran off the side (HYG-037 and LIVE-529 hold). What failed was the hand: all six section pills measured 34 to 36px tall; every daily door (members, email, calendar, shop) sat at the top of the screen, in the one band a thumb holding the phone cannot reach; each tool row's description lived only in a `title` attribute, which a phone never shows (0 of 4, 0 of 5 and 0 of 8 descriptions visible on the three tool tabs); and the order fulfilment door put a small "Mark shipped" button beside two fixed-width inputs. The primitives were already right (`Button`, `IconButton`, `Checkbox` and `Select` carry `tap-target`, LIVE-469), and the roster and orders were already lists, not tables. So this is a layout pass inside the existing shells, not a new console.

**Decision.**

1. **A thumb bar for the daily doors, in slot 0c of the mobile stacking contract.** `components/spaces/console-thumb-bar.tsx` renders below `md` only, on the standalone `/manage` page only (the in-place Manage panel under a Space profile does not ask for it). It is the contract's full-bleed page action bar and follows rule 7 exactly: background pinned at `var(--tab-bar-h)`, content padded by `calc(var(--lane-rise) + 0.5rem)`, so the Zap catch and the chat tab ride on its foot and never cover a door. Its doors are fixed controls, so they take the coarse 44px (`min-h-[44px]`, 51px tall) and never the per-generation `--tap-min` dip. The page pads its foot (`max-md:pb-18`) by the bar's own height above the lane. Measured: at 320, 390 and 430px the doors sit 121 to 172px above the bottom edge, inside the contract's 35dvh reachable band and above the lane's top.
2. **The doors are catalog rows, not a menu.** `lib/spaces/console-thumb.ts` names four `SPACE_MODULES` ids (`space.people`, `space.comms`, `space.calendar`, `space.services`) and nothing else: label, icon and href come from the rows `resolveSpaceMenu` already gated for the viewer, so a tool the viewer cannot use or the owner hid is absent from the bar too, and the bar follows the owner's Module Manager order. No catalog row, rail item or menu-debt count changes ([MENU-CONTRACT](MENU-CONTRACT.md)). Four is the cap: 70px columns at 320px keep "Your people" whole.
3. **Every console control is a 44px target under a coarse pointer.** The section pills, the tool rows (top-level and nested) and the hub search results take `tap-target`, which is 44px under `(pointer: coarse)` and unchanged for a mouse.
4. **No hover-only information.** A tool row shows its description as a quiet second line under `pointer-coarse:`; a mouse keeps the dense one-line row and the `title`.
5. **The fulfilment door stacks on a phone.** `OrderFulfilmentControl` lays carrier, tracking and the step button out full width below `sm`, so "Mark shipped" is a thumb-wide target; from `sm` it is the side-by-side row it was. It is the one door the Space Shop, the maker console and the operator Orders page share.

**Rejected.** A horizontal scroller for the section pills (LIVE-529 retired exactly that on the Space menu: a drag with no visible scrollbar hides destinations). A floating action button (the stacking contract has no slot for one, and one button cannot carry four doors). Pinning the bar at `--tab-bar-clearance` (the 2026-08-31 gap under the RSVP bar). Adding the bar to every `/settings/*` sub-page (those are single-task FocusTemplate forms whose submit already sits at the end of the flow; the console is where an operator chooses what to do next).

**Consequences.** On a phone the console opens with its four daily doors under the thumb, and every console control clears 44px. Desktop renders are unchanged: `tap-target` is 32px for a fine pointer and every affected control was already taller, and the descriptions stay in the tooltip. The mobile visual baseline of the Space console (`app-space-console`) changes (taller pills, the thumb bar); `pr-compare` is advisory and the baseline is recaptured by the runner. `test/e2e/space-console-phone.spec.ts` measures the four readings (no horizontal scroll, 44px controls, doors in the reachable band above the lane, descriptions shown) on the `mobile` project against a preview; `components/spaces/console-thumb-bar.test.tsx` pins the selection and the bar's geometry; the row's probe runs the real selection over the real catalog and fails on `main`.

**Rows.** `LIVE-704` (closed here).
