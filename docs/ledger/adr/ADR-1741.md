## ADR-1741: Headings take the theme's fonts; there is no per-heading font pick

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-13 · owner-directed · extends [ADR-1737](DECISIONS.md) (the design switch)

**Context.** Every design block in both editors (the Puck page editor and the Space entity-block editor) offered a per-heading "Header font" select (Anton, Nunito, Playfair, Space Grotesk, Caveat), and the Display heading block a "Font" choice. The pick was written as an inline `fontFamily`, which beat every theme rule, so a Space page under Frequency still showed Anton. The first fix forced the design's face with `!important`, which also overrode the Space's own theme fonts. The owner ruled: remove the per-heading pick; a Space keeps its theme and can change its primary theme fonts.

**Decision.** (1) The `headerFont` and `font` fields are gone from every design block, and nothing renders an inline heading face; a value stored on an older block is ignored on read (no migration). Headings take `font-display`, which the theme sets. (2) A Space's font set (lib/theme/space-themes.ts, picked in its branding) is the one place its fonts change. Under DAWN it rides `data-space-theme` as before. Under Frequency, components/spaces/accent-scope.tsx emits `data-space-fonts` for any set other than the house default, and app/themes/frequency/space-page.css swaps the display face (titles, the Space name) and the reading face; case, weight and size stay Frequency's. The Space's accent colour still does not apply under Frequency. (3) The `!important` on Space page headings is removed.

**Rejected.** Keeping the per-heading pick under DAWN only (two behaviours for one field); forcing the design face everywhere (it took away the owner-facing font choice).

**Consequences.** Measured 2026-10-13 on /spaces/royaltemple under Frequency: its Editorial set renders Fraunces headings and the Space name over a Nunito body. Marketing pages edited in /edit/* lose the per-heading pick too and follow the site design.
