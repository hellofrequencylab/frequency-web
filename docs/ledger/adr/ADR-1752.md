## ADR-1752: One explainer, one operator guide: the public site merges its duplicate pages and links the pages it kept (SCAN-1017)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-19 · backlog `SCAN-1017` · extends [ADR-1747](DECISIONS.md) (the 2026-10-14 page merges) and [ADR-1737](DECISIONS.md) (the Frequency design's coded pages)

**Context.** After the 2026-10-14 merges the site still advertised 31 static marketing URLs. `/about` (the founding narrative, 600 words) and `/what-is-frequency` (a 2,100-word explainer with a twelve-question FAQ) told the same story, and the explainer still described four Pillars and Channels the Frequency pages no longer show. `/tools-for-community-builders` repeated `/how-to-build-community` in internal nouns (Runs, Dispatch, Zaps, Gems) and was linked from one card on `/spaces`. Under the Frequency design nothing linked the six `/for` doors, nothing but `/pricing` linked `/networks`, and the pillar guides CONTENT-VOICE §7 describes as a cross-linked cluster had no site-wide link. The owner asked for a tight, strategic page set with a best-practice count for search and discovery.

**Decision.** (1) `/what-is-frequency` 308s to `/about`, which takes five of its answers (what it is, how it works, whether it is social media, how it makes money, where it is available) as an Accordion FAQ, so one URL carries the explainer and the FAQPage node. (2) `/tools-for-community-builders` 308s to `/how-to-build-community`, which carries the four jobs as one section and one FAQ answer in plain nouns. (3) `/subscribe` is noindex,follow and off the sitemap: a retention page, not a search landing. (4) The kept pages get their links: the `/spaces` type cards open the four doors, the footer gains Networks and a Guides column, and the header hint reads "Free, Business, Collective, Network". (5) The redirects are route-level `permanentRedirect` stubs, not `next.config` rules, because the LIVE-912 and LIVE-913 probes read both page files by path and `redirect-shadow.test.ts` refuses a rule over a live route; the templates stay as source, as the-lab's and the-quest's did under ADR-1747. (6) All six niche doors stay: the LIVE-911 probe pins six trustLines, and each is a distinct funnel once something links it.

**Rejected.** Deleting the two page files (two done rows would regress). Merging `/about` into `/what-is-frequency` instead (`/about` is the URL people and the member shell already use, and the explainer's URL is the one that carried the stale taxonomy). Cutting the home page's Story band and swapping the audience pages' headlines (both are design-handoff pages; left for the owner). Dropping the community-builders and teachers doors (the probe count, and the pricing persona strip, pin six).

**Consequences.** 28 indexable static marketing URLs. `scripts/check-seo.mjs` allowlists the two stubs with the reason. `EDITABLE_PAGES`, `render-path-bodies.txt` and the fingerprint baselines drop the two slugs. The SCAN-1017 probe fails if the sitemap re-advertises a merged page, a stub stops redirecting, About loses its FAQ, the footer loses Guides, or the type cards stop linking the doors.
