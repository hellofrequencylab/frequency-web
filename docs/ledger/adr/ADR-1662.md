## ADR-1662: A suspended or archived Space sends no email, and what it had queued is cancelled or held, never lost silently (LIVE-727)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · backlog `LIVE-727` (filed and closed here) · found while drafting the Space email acceptable-use policy (`LIVE-707`, PR #3066) · amends the anti-spam gate list at the top of `lib/spaces/email.ts` · no migration

**Context.** Staff suspend a Space from `/admin/spaces/[id]` (`setSpaceStatus`), which flips `spaces.status` to `suspended` (the column's CHECK is `active | suspended | archived`). The member-facing reads already wall a Space that is not active: `getVisibleSpaceBySlug` returns null, so its pages, its conversation inbox and the member composer's slug gate all go dark. The email send path does not resolve a Space by slug. It resolves by id through `getSpaceById`, which reads every status on purpose (the operator surface needs it). Re-tested on main at 60567b67e before the change: nothing between `getSpaceById` and `sendRawEmail` read `status`. So a suspended Space's scheduled campaigns (`campaigns-send-due.ts`), drip steps (`drip-runner.ts`), event Manage broadcasts (`broadcast-actions.ts`), owner composer sends (`campaigns.ts`, `email-drafts.ts` `sendSpaceEmailDraft`) and the bulk jobs it had already queued (staggered by `bulkRunAfter`, so a large campaign drains over hours) all kept going out. The member composer's conversation send (`sendSpaceEmailDraftAsConversations`) is a `'use server'` action keyed by space id with the same gap. Automations only enroll into drips, so they ride the drip path.

**Decision.**

1. **Only an active Space sends.** `spaceEmailHold(status)` in `lib/spaces/email.ts` is the one pure decision: `active` sends; `suspended`, `archived`, and anything unreadable hold. Fail-closed, like the kill switch beside it. Archived is included because an archived Space has no business mailing either.
2. **The delivery core refuses before it queues.** `deliverSpaceCampaign` is the one function both entry points (`sendSpaceCampaign` for the owner, `sendSpaceCampaignSystem` for the cron, the drip runner and the event broadcast) pass through. It checks status first and returns `SPACE_NOT_ACTIVE_EMAIL_ERROR`. Nothing is queued, nothing is ledgered, and `space_email.refused_space_not_active` is logged.
3. **The drain re-checks right before the provider.** `runSpaceCampaignEmail` is the single place a Space campaign email reaches `sendRawEmail`. Each job now carries its `spaceId` (a job queued before this change is resolved through its `outreach_sends` row). A Space that is not active, or cannot be identified, cancels the job: nothing is sent, the ledger row moves `queued` to `failed` with a note saying the Space was suspended or archived, and `space_email.drain_skipped_space_not_active` is logged. A status read that errors throws, so the outbox retries and nothing is sent on a blip.
4. **Drips hold, they do not stop.** Every other seam refusal stops a drip enrollment, which for a suspension would throw away every contact's place in the sequence. The drip runner now tells the not-active refusal apart by its exported message and releases the enrollment back to `enrolled` at the same step, due again in an hour (`SPACE_HOLD_RECHECK_MS`), logging `cron.space_drips.held_space_not_active`. When staff reactivate the Space, the step it was on sends. `DripRunResult` gains `held`.
5. **The conversation composer carries its own copy of the gate.** It mails through the ticketed conversation system, not the campaign seam, so `sendSpaceEmailDraftAsConversations` checks `spaceEmailHold` before it reads the draft or opens a conversation.

A scheduled campaign that comes due while its Space is suspended is stamped `failed` with the refusal as its `send_error` (the existing refusal path), so the operator sees why it did not go out and can reschedule after reactivation.

**Rejected.** Turning `email_enabled` off on suspend (it would lose the owner's own setting on reactivation, and misses jobs already queued). Filtering by status inside `getSpaceById` (the operator surface needs suspended Spaces to resolve). Leaving the drain unguarded and relying on the up-front check (a campaign queued before the suspension would still drain for hours). Holding queued campaign jobs by throwing (they would retry until they dead-lettered, which reads as an outage).

**Consequences.** Suspending a Space stops its email at once, including mail it had queued. Drip contacts keep their place. Reactivating resumes drips within the hour. A cancelled campaign email is visible as `failed` in the Space's send history with the reason. Platform email (`lib/email-studio`, the root Space) is unchanged because the root Space cannot be suspended. Not covered: conversation replies batched in `lib/comms/outbound-batch` in the few minutes before a suspension, since that inbox is already walled by the slug gate for new replies.

**Rows.** `LIVE-727` filed and closed here (P1, lane live). Tests: `lib/spaces/email.test.ts` (the pure decision, both entry points, the drain cancel, a pre-change job, a status read error, an unknown or deleted Space), `lib/spaces/drip-runner.test.ts` (hold at the same step, then resume after reactivation), `lib/spaces/email-drafts.test.ts` (the conversation composer gate).
