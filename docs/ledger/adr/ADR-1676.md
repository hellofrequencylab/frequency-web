## ADR-1676: A selling persona goes Active only with its member's payout account behind it, and activation binds that account (LIVE-696)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-30 · backlog `LIVE-696` · owner approved the build 2026-09-30 · re-tested first per [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md) · builds on [ADR-175](DECISIONS.md) (one Connect account per profile) and ADR-163/165 (the persona ladder) · no migration

**Context.** A partner persona climbs claimed → verified → active. `verified` lights every partner tool; `active` is the money state for Practitioner and Organization. Re-tested on `main` at `f7f7d3075`: `lib/personas.ts` and `lib/personas-core.ts` still called the per-persona Connect binding a stub, and `CONNECT_WIRED = false` refused every move to `active` for every program (SITE-AUDIT BUG-7). The refusal was safe, but it was a constant, not a check: flipping it would have let a money persona go Active with no account behind it, and nothing told the member what was missing. Meanwhile Connect had landed: each member has one Express account (`profiles.stripe_account_id`, mirrored `stripe_charges_enabled`), onboarded at `/settings/billing`, and `profile_personas.stripe_account_id` has existed since `20260608060001`.

**Decision.**

1. **One pure gate.** `personaActivationVerdict(persona, payout)` in `lib/personas-core.ts` (client-safe). A money persona is allowed Active only when the member's account has an id AND charges enabled, and the verdict carries that id to bind. No account, an unfinished one, or a failed read (passed as null) refuses with `PERSONA_NEEDS_PAYOUT`, which names the payout account and Billing. Collaborator and Business take no money, so the ladder alone decides them. `canStaffTransition` is the ladder only; `CONNECT_WIRED` and `awaitingConnect` are removed.
2. **The action checks fresh and binds.** `transitionPersona` reads `getConnectStatus(profileId)` at the moment of activation rather than trusting the page, refuses with the verdict's sentence, and writes `profile_personas.stripe_account_id` in the same update as `state: 'active'`. A failed read degrades to "no account" in `getConnectStatus`, so it refuses: fail closed.
3. **The existing account, not a new system.** The binding is the member's one Connect account, the same one every money path pays. No second onboarding, no per-persona Stripe account. A separate account per legal entity stays unbuilt.
4. **Both readers are told.** `/admin/personas` reads the member's flags in the queue query, shows No payout account yet / Payout account ready / Payout bound, offers Activate only when the gate passes, and counts verified money programs waiting on an account. `/partners/join` tells a verified Practitioner or Organization it goes Active once they have a payout account, links `/settings/billing#payouts`, and renders the shared `PayoutPromptCard` (LIVE-233), which starts onboarding inline.

**Rejected.** Flipping `CONNECT_WIRED` to true (activates with nothing behind it). A per-persona Stripe account or a new binding table (a second onboarding for the same human; the column already exists). Gating on `charges && payouts` (the brief and the question are "can this seller take money"; payouts readiness stays the payout prompt's concern). Gating the sell paths on persona state (they already check Connect at checkout and do not read persona state).

**Consequences.** A verified money persona can now reach Active, which it never could, but only with a charges-enabled account, and the row records which account. Nothing that was lit goes dark: `verified` still lights every tool. An account that later loses charges does not demote an Active persona; every sell path still checks Connect at checkout. Tests: `lib/personas.test.ts` (the verdict) and `app/(main)/admin/personas/actions.test.ts` (enabled activates and binds; not enabled is refused with the sentence and writes nothing).

**Rows.** `LIVE-696`.
