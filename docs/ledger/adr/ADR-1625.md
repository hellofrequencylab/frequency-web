## ADR-1625: Daily backups are enough for now; PITR revisited at 1,000 profiles or regular paid orders (OWN-082)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · backlog `OWN-082` (closed here), the sixth child of `DEF-HARDEN` under [ADR-1561](DECISIONS.md) · owner ruling 2026-09-29 ("Daily is enough") · the procedure and the dated facts live in [`RUNBOOKS.md`](RUNBOOKS.md) section 7 · files `OWN-084` (a backup for uploaded files, a ruling because it costs money) · numbered **1625** by the orchestrator's assignment

**Context.** Until today nobody had read which backup tier the database is on or rehearsed a restore, so the RPO and RTO were unknown numbers and section 4 of the runbook said UNKNOWN four times. The Management API returns no backup field, so the facts came from the owner's dashboard screenshots and a rehearsal, with the restored copy checked by the orchestrator's read-only SQL. Read on 2026-09-29: project Frequency Community (`azsqfeonabsbmemvddqd`, us-west-2, Postgres 17), organisation on the Pro plan; scheduled backups daily and physical, taken about 11:45 to 11:51 UTC, 8 listed (22 to 29 Sep), so about 7 days retained; point-in-time recovery **not enabled** ("available as an add-on"). The rehearsal restored the 29 Sep 11:48:49 UTC backup to a new project: clicked 18:14 UTC, ready 18:26 UTC, so the **database RTO is 12 minutes**, click to ready. The copy matched production on 60 profiles, the newest profile (2026-09-27 16:48), 526 `storage.objects` rows and roughly the size (255 MB against 261 MB), and its migration ledger ended at `20270345009410` (745 rows) against production's `20270345009700` (748), because three migrations were applied at 16:28 UTC, after the backup. That is the **RPO of up to 24 hours**, shown rather than inferred. Supabase's restore dialog and Backups page list what a restore does not carry: storage files and storage settings, Edge Functions, auth settings and API keys, extensions and database settings, read replicas; and database backups do not include objects stored via the Storage API.

**Decision.**

1. **Daily backups are enough for now. PITR stays off.** Today's losses from a day's rollback are small: 60 profiles, and paid orders are not yet a daily event. The add-on buys an RPO of minutes at a monthly cost the product does not yet need.
2. **Revisit at 1,000 profiles or when paid orders become regular, whichever is first.** Either one makes a lost day of writes cost real members or real money. The revisit is a new ruling with the then-current price, not an automatic switch.
3. **Always restore to a new project.** The in-place Restore button beside a backup overwrites production and loses every write since that backup; it is never used for a rehearsal, and for a real incident only when the project itself is unrecoverable and the owner says so. A few wrong rows are a forward fix read out of a restored copy.
4. **The whole-site RTO is longer than 12 minutes and unmeasured.** Moving the site to a restored project also needs the Vercel Supabase variables and keys re-pointed, auth settings re-entered (every member signs in again), the one Edge Function redeployed, and the storage files, which have no backup at all. The runbook lists each step; nobody has timed them.
5. **Rehearse once a quarter**, next before launch day (2026-12-21), and whenever the plan, the compute size or the PITR add-on changes. Delete the scratch project the same day: it bills at production's compute ($14.83 a month shown) while it exists and a paid-tier project cannot be paused.

**Rejected.** Turning PITR on now (a monthly cost for an RPO the current member count does not need; the ruling is to wait for the trigger). Leaving the facts in an ADR only (a restore is decided in the hour it is needed, so the dated facts sit in the runbook beside the procedure). Treating 12 minutes as the site's recovery time (it is the database's; the manual steps after it are the larger part and are unmeasured). Buying a storage-file backup in this change (it costs money, so it is `OWN-084`, a ruling).

**Consequences.** ✅ `RUNBOOKS.md` section 4 no longer says UNKNOWN; section 7 carries tier, retention, RPO and a measured RTO with the date, the step-by-step restore, the not-restored list and the rehearsal cadence. ✅ `MAINTENANCE-AUTOMATION.md` section 5's "confirm PITR/backups" is done. ⚠️ Up to a day of writes can be lost, by decision. ⚠️ Uploaded files (avatars, post images, Loom originals) exist in one place only; a deleted bucket or project loses them for good until `OWN-084` is ruled on.

**Rows.** `OWN-082` closed here. `OWN-084` filed (owner, ruling). `DEF-HARDEN` stays open while `LIVE-551` is not done.
