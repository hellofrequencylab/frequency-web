## ADR-1682: The iOS readiness rows park with the native app, and 24 P3 rows park until after launch (HYG-157)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-30 · backlog `HYG-157` (filed and closed here) · owner multiple choice at ~01:45Z (the iOS readiness rows) and the P3 sort approved at ~01:50Z · companion to `HYG-156`, which records the same day's rulings on the owner rows; split off to keep each PR under the size gate · amends [ADR-1650](DECISIONS.md) (the app readiness active on WM) for ten of its rows · no migration

**Context.** ADR-1650 kept the iOS app readiness active on the WM wave while the native build itself (`DEF-MOBILE`) stayed parked. Ten of those rows are build work that only matters once a native client exists. Separately, the P3 band held rows that no member will miss before launch, and they were spread across waves as if they were scheduled.

**Decision.**

1. **"Park with the native app"** (~01:45Z). `LIVE-714`, `LIVE-716`, `LIVE-717`, `LIVE-718`, `LIVE-719`, `LIVE-720`, `LIVE-721`, `LIVE-722`, `LIVE-723` and `LIVE-726` are parked. They reopen together when the native Expo/React Native build (`DEF-MOBILE`, parked to 2027-01-05) is started. `LIVE-713` stays active on WM, and `LIVE-715`, `LIVE-724` and `OWN-092` are already done. `DEF-MOBILE`'s notes and detail name the ten.
2. **The P3 sort** (~01:50Z): park until after launch, reopening on the first real paid order or the first six weeks of live Spaces, whichever comes first. `PROG-GD6`, `LIVE-693`, `LIVE-664`, `LIVE-665`, `LIVE-667`, `LIVE-708`, `LIVE-671`, `LIVE-673`, `LIVE-663`, `LIVE-682`, `LIVE-669`, `LIVE-668`, `LIVE-676`, `LIVE-677`, `LIVE-675`, `LIVE-679`, `LIVE-678`, `LIVE-690`, `LIVE-691`, `LIVE-662`, `LIVE-658`, `LIVE-710`, `LIVE-692`, `LIVE-695`. The same sort's drop of `LIVE-659` is recorded by `HYG-156`. Left for their own PRs and not touched: `LIVE-656`, `LIVE-709`, `LIVE-696`, `HYG-139`, `LIVE-681`, `LIVE-666`, `LIVE-684`, `LIVE-689`, `LIVE-660`, `LIVE-674`, `LIVE-655`, `LIVE-672`.
3. **How it is recorded.** One edit fragment per row (ADR-1635). Each carries `parkedUntil` and its trigger in `notes` and a REVISIT TRIGGER paragraph in `detail`, keeps its probe (parked rows are not probed), and leaves its wave by itself.
4. **`HYG-146`'s probe is amended** because the ruling changes what its done means: it required the ten rows active, and now holds them with the native holds. A parked row whose notes cite this ADR counts as ruled, which covers `PROG-GD6` (the phone door) and `LIVE-671`, whose titles match its mobile-web pattern. `HYG-138`'s hold on `PROG-GD6` is amended by `HYG-156`; until that merges, HYG-138 reads the base file, where `PROG-GD6` is still open, so neither PR depends on the other's order.

**Rejected.** Parking the whole WM wave (LIVE-713 is mobile-site work that ships now). Lowering the P3 rows further instead of parking them (a priority says how urgent a row is; the ruling is about when, and a park with a trigger says that).

**Consequences.** WM holds the mobile-site work and nothing of the native build. The open list sheds 34 rows. When `DEF-MOBILE` starts, the ten reopen as one group; after launch the 24 reopen on whichever trigger fires first.

**Rows.** `HYG-157` (filed and closed here). Parked: the ten iOS readiness rows and the 24 P3 rows named above. Notes updated: `DEF-MOBILE`. Probe amended: `HYG-146`.
