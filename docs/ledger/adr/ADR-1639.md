## ADR-1639: joinRoom checks the caller belongs to a Circle, Hub, Nexus, Outpost or Channel before writing them into that room (LIVE-651)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · backlog `LIVE-651` (closed here) · SEC-5 of the Idea Inbox audit · extends [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md) (re-test the premise) · no migration

**Context.** `joinRoom` selected `id, visibility`, refused `private`, and upserted a `room_members` row through the admin client for every other visibility. `rooms_visibility_check` also allows `circle`, `hub`, `nexus`, `outpost` and `channel`, each with a `scope_id`. RLS does not stand in the way of that upsert. So a member who was not in a Circle could join its Circle room by calling the action with the room id. Posting was already re-gated (`sendRoomMessage` asks for Channel tune-in or a `room_members` row). Joining was not. The compose UI only mints public and private rooms, so the hole is the scoped rooms that already exist.

**Decision.**

1. **`joinRoom` reads `scope_id` with visibility.** A public room stays open to anyone signed in. A private room still needs an invite. A `circle` / `hub` / `nexus` / `outpost` / `channel` room asks `belongsToRoomScope` before the upsert. Any other visibility fails closed.
2. **Belonging is the existing roster, not a new table.** A Circle: an active `memberships` row, or the Circle's host. A Hub: an active member of a Circle in that Hub, or the Hub's guide. A Nexus: an active member of a Circle under that Nexus, or the Nexus's mentor. An Outpost: an active member of a Circle under a Nexus on that Outpost. A Channel: a `topical_channel_memberships` row. Missing `scope_id`, a missing scope row, an inactive membership, or a thrown read is a no.
3. **The refusal is one plain line**, in house voice, naming the scope (`You have to belong to this Circle to join its room.` Tune into this Channel for a Channel). Private keeps the copy it already used.

**Rejected.** Leaving the hole because posting is gated (joining still plants a `room_members` row the member should not have, and a Circle room is not a public square). Treating a Channel room as public-join (tune-in is the Channel door; `room_members` is not how a Channel is joined). A migration or an RLS policy on `room_members` insert (the action already uses the admin client; the check belongs next to the upsert).

**Consequences.** A member who does not belong to a scoped room cannot join it by calling the action. A public room is unchanged. A Circle host, Hub guide or Nexus mentor can still join their scope's room without holding a `memberships` row. The probe reads `joinRoom` and fails while it never reads `scope_id` or never compares it to a scope. Tests in `lib/messages/room-scope.test.ts` cover each visibility against a mocked admin client.

**Rows.** `LIVE-651` (closed here). Untouched: posting (`sendRoomMessage`), invites (`inviteToRoom`), the compose UI (still public/private only).
