## ADR-1736: Network: a fourth site theme, a section family that draws its own bands, and a Collective seeded from a handoff

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-13 · backlog `LIVE-906` · beside [ADR-1726](DECISIONS.md) (the site style), [ADR-1727](DECISIONS.md) (one render path, the theme is the stylesheet) and [ADR-1734](DECISIONS.md) (Wildheart)

**Context.** The owner asked for The Group, a San Diego business community, built as a Collective with its chapters, its calendar, its merch and every page written, from a full design handoff (tokens, a Homepage, a member portal and the verbatim site content), and for the handoff's look to join the theme library as "Network". The follow-up asked for many passes until the theme matched the design system exactly. The handoff's Homepage is a composition the generic page sections cannot carry: a hero with a pinned next-event card, stat tiles, a solid accent band with a countdown, an events list beside a month and an "Up next" clock, a dark chapters band, circle cards with their next dates, tier cards with a raised featured tier, priced offerings, a media band, merch cards and a coffee card, set in flat full-bleed bands joined by waves.

**Decision.** (1) **Network is a site theme, built the Wildheart way.** `SiteThemeId` gains `network`, the editor gains a live `Network` theme with its contract entry, the values live in one token block (lib/theme/network.ts) and the skin reads tokens only. A synced Space page wears the Modern font set (ADR-578); Bricolage Grotesque, Outfit and Instrument Serif load on the website root only, and the signature face reads the root layout's Caveat rather than a second copy. (2) **Schemes, not just accents, chosen by the accent the owner already sets.** Four schemes (Marigold, the default, then Ocean, Lagoon, Coral) each re-point the whole page; a brand accent equal to a scheme's accent wears that scheme, any other hex is the accent alone on Marigold's grounds. No new stored field and no visitor picker: the owner's existing accent control is the switch. The handoff's subtle text step moves one shade per scheme to clear AA on the fill. (3) **A themed section family draws its own band.** The Network blocks plan as a new `own` kind: drawn by their own render without the narrow wrapper `other` puts around a block, so a band runs full width, and a Network hero owns the page h1 like a hero section. Generic blocks still plan and render as before in every theme. (4) **A Collective's chapters are member Spaces, its events tenanted to the Collective and hosted by the chapter.** The page blocks read a Space's events by tenancy, so the Collective's Home and calendar list every chapter's meetings, and each chapter's Calendar page lists its own through the hosting axis. (5) **The seed is a script that prints one transaction.** scripts/seed-the-group.mts holds the content; scripts/seed-the-group.sql is its output and the probe fails when they drift. Nothing is written to production by the change itself, and the website stays unpublished until the owner publishes it.

**Rejected.** A per-visitor scheme picker stored in the browser (the handoff's design-review control; a site's scheme is the owner's call). Restyling only the generic sections (measured against the handoff, they cannot carry its composition). Chapter events tenanted to the chapter (the Collective's Home would lose them, since the blocks read tenancy only). A sixth member Space for South OC (no confirmed day, time or place, and the plan includes five).

**Consequences.** scripts/probes/network-theme.mjs holds the registration, the root-layout rule, AA in every scheme, the block family and the seed's brief facts. test/the-group-site.render.test.tsx renders every page for screenshot passes beside the handoff. The open items the handoff marks for June ship behind TODO notes in the seed, never as invented values.
