## ADR-1658: Space email has an acceptable-use draft for counsel, kept out of the product until it is reviewed (LIVE-707)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · backlog `LIVE-707` (closed here) · unblocks `OWN-085` (stays open with the owner) · owner ruling OWN-085 "Draft the AUP for review" · no migration

**Context.** Space email can send (lib/spaces/email.ts), but no acceptable-use policy existed. The Turn on email card asks the owner to tick one box ("I have permission to email these people and will follow anti-spam rules"), its header comment said the counsel-gated AUP was deferred, and the send backbone listed "AUP/DPA legal copy" as out of scope. OWN-085 asked the owner to have an AUP reviewed; the owner ruled that an agent drafts it first. Re-tested before the change (ADR-1082): no policy text for Space email anywhere in app/, components/, content/ or docs/.

**Decision.**

1. **The draft lives where the legal pages live.** `app/space-email-policy/page.tsx`, a static FocusTemplate page built like `app/terms` and `app/privacy`, so the reviewed version can go live by editing one file.
2. **It cannot pass for live policy.** It opens with "DRAFT. Not in force, and no lawyer has read it yet.", says it is not legal advice and not part of any agreement, and names the Terms and the checkbox as what applies today. `robots: { index: false, follow: false }`; no entry in `app/sitemap.ts`; nothing in app/, components/ or lib/ links it. The enable card's UI is unchanged; only its header comment names the draft and says why it is not linked.
3. **Every rule is what the code does.** Who sends: `canEditProfile` plus the per-Space `email` function (default minimum role admin), behind the `spaces.email_enabled` kill switch, off by default and turned on only with the acknowledgment. Who receives: a marketing send needs the contact `subscribed` in that Space (a join, a contact-form tick, a lead magnet, an unlocked QR offer, an accepted warm intro); an import lands `unknown` and is skipped; the one relaxed lane is an event host mailing that event's own guests with topic events. Limits: `DAILY_SEND_CAP` (500 a day, UTC, every plan) and the plan's monthly `space_email` allowance; a skipped recipient counts against neither. Unsubscribe: RFC 8058 one-click, a per-Space suppression, a per-topic mute, and a read-only suppression list. Bounces and complaints: suppressed platform-wide and for the Space by the Resend webhook; the Email panel warns above 0.1% complaints. Turning email off: the same kill switch, which platform staff can flip and which also stops scheduled sends and sequences.
4. **What is policy and not code is labelled as such.** The prohibited-content list, the promise to tell the owner why by email, and the appeal address are policy; the page says the list is a draft for review, and the appeal reply time is a bracketed placeholder for the owner.
5. **The questions for counsel travel with the draft.** The last section of the page lists them and says it comes out before publication: sender of record and controller or processor; whose postal address must appear; replies to a no-reply sender; the event host exception under CAN-SPAM, CASL and PECR; a join as consent; one complaint stopping every Space; enforcement with no automatic threshold, and Space suspension not stopping email; the prohibited list for a wellness community; open and click tracking consent; acceptance by name with a date; the appeal reply time.
6. **Going live is a separate change.** When counsel has reviewed it, an agent removes the banner and the questions, lets the page be indexed and sitemapped, and links it from the Turn on email card. That closes OWN-085.

**Rejected.** A markdown file under docs/ (a second home for legal text, and it would need a planning-doc declaration for a document that is not a plan). Linking the draft from the enable card now (the ruling was a draft for review, and a linked draft reads as the rules). Importing `DAILY_SEND_CAP` into the page (it would pull the service-role send backbone into a static legal page; the test and the probe read the constant instead, so the two cannot drift). Fixing the two gaps found while reading (below) in this PR (one row per PR).

**Found, not fixed here.** Suspending a Space (`setSpaceStatus` to suspended) does not stop its email: `deliverSpaceCampaign` never reads `spaces.status`. The plain campaign composer (`renderCampaignHtml` in lib/spaces/campaigns.ts and campaigns-send-due.ts) and the drip runner footer carry an unsubscribe line and no postal address; the block editor's footer carries Frequency Labs Holdings' address. Both are listed as counsel questions on the page.

**Consequences.** The owner has one page to hand to counsel. No Space owner sees it, no crawler indexes it, and nothing a Space agrees to changes. The draft's daily limit is checked against the backbone's constant by the LIVE-707 probe and by the page test, so a change to the cap turns them red until the sentence follows.

**Rows.** `LIVE-707` closed here. `OWN-085` unblocked (status open, blocked_by removed, pointed at the draft). Tests: `app/space-email-policy/page.test.tsx`.
