## ADR-1740: The Frequency design system installs from its export into its own --fq- namespace, and DAWN's names alias to it

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-13 · backlog `LIVE-909` · owner-directed · extends [ADR-1737](DECISIONS.md) (the design switch) · beside [ADR-1739](DECISIONS.md) (the element library contract)

**Context.** The owner is finalising the Frequency design system in Claude Design and wants to install it locally, switch the platform to it by hand, and see what breaks. ADR-1737 built the switch on the assumption that a design redefines DAWN's own token names. The export (`Frequency Design System.zip`, 2026-10-13) is its own system with its own vocabulary, and the owner ruled that Frequency must not be squeezed into DAWN's names. Its names also collide with the app's: `--text-body` is a font size in the export's typography.css AND a colour in its themes.css (so even inside the export the colour wins), while the app reads `--text-body` as its body font size; `--accent`, `--ease-out`, `--font-sans`, `--text-meta` and the radius names collide too. Loaded as-is, the export would resize the whole app's text.

**Decision.** (1) **One command installs an export:** `pnpm design:install <export.zip | folder>` (scripts/install-frequency-design.mjs) vendors the locked core verbatim to design_handoff/frequency/ (photos and b-roll stay out of git), generates app/themes/frequency/core.css, and copies the wordmark, round icon and icon sprite to public/brand/frequency/. Re-running with a newer export replaces it. (2) **Its own namespace:** every custom property in the generated core is renamed `--fq-<name>`; Frequency keeps its words, one prefix away from the app's. (3) **Scoped to the switch:** Commons tokens on `:root[data-design="frequency"]`, Commons-at-night on `.dark[data-design="frequency"]`, Stage on `[data-surface="stage"]` inside it, energy on `[data-energy]` inside it; the export's two night candidates are not installed (themes.css decides night per theme); the Google Fonts @import is dropped; element defaults go in `@layer base` so the app's utility classes still win. (4) **The alias layer** (app/themes/frequency.css, hand-kept) points DAWN's names (--color-*, the radius roles, the --design-* layer, motion, map paint) at --fq- tokens, and switches DAWN's signature effects off with their levers. It is declared on the root AND on every Stage surface, because a custom property that reads var() resolves where it is declared. (5) **Stage surfaces:** the marketing layout and the home page carry `data-surface="stage"` (inert under DAWN). (6) **Faces:** lib/theme/design-fonts/frequency.ts registers Unbounded, Instrument Serif (preload off) and Hanken Grotesk through next/font, loaded only on a Frequency build. (7) **Off-CSS surfaces:** the browser chrome (Commons sand, Thermal night), lib/theme/palette.ts (OG, email, Wallet) and lib/theme/brand-assets.ts take Frequency's values. (8) **The local switch:** `pnpm design:use frequency|dawn` writes the one `.env.local` line and rewrites the font module and manifest colours; restart `pnpm dev`. Production is the same variable on Vercel plus a redeploy. (9) lib/theme/design.test.ts now holds: the alias layer declares only DAWN names or names the core declares, reads only --fq- names the core declares, loads after the core, and the core is scoped, namespaced and @import-free.

**Rejected.** Mapping the export's values straight into DAWN's names (the owner ruled it out, and a re-export would mean a hand diff every round). Loading the export's sheets unscoped (they set `html` font size and `--text-body` site-wide). A runtime switch (ADR-1737's reasons hold).

**Consequences.** Switching is two commands and a restart. Measured 2026-10-13: under Frequency /pricing and / render on Stage with Frequency type and the light wordmark (the export's --logo-filter, wired through `data-brand-wordmark`); under DAWN nothing changed. Open for the owner: the export's own `--text-body` collision (typography vs themes) should be fixed upstream in Claude Design; the alias layer keeps DAWN's type sizes and rank spectrum until the owner maps them; what breaks on the member app under Commons is the owner's local review.
