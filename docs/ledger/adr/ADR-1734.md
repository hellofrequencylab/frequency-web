## ADR-1734: Wildheart: a third site theme and the astrology calendar block family

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-12 · backlog `LIVE-902` · beside [ADR-1726](DECISIONS.md) (Frequency and Bold) and [ADR-1727](DECISIONS.md) (one render path, the theme is the stylesheet)

**Context.** The owner brief of 2026-10-12 asked for a third look in the theme library, built the way Bold is built, from a complete design handoff (tokens, fonts, a symbol library, an astrology calendar engine and the 2027 calendar data): "Light, ancient and organic. Colour: parchment and vellum grounds, violet as the primary, madder and blush for warmth. Type: IM Fell English for headings, Garamond for body text. Shape and texture: soft petal-shaped corners, arches for photos, grain and gentle gradients. Witchy edge: zodiac, alchemical and moon symbols in one violet ink. Never emoji." Its signature: "The astrology calendar. A turning wheel of months, signs, seasons and moons sorts every festival, moon and Circle Night. It's the scroll-through on the homepage, the sky map on the year page, and the date line at the top of every page." A later ruling the same day made it a STOCK theme: "The Wildheart theme will be a stock theme in the Frequency library. Please use generic content that's gender neutral and about empowerment." The Space that commissioned it launches on the stock Frequency theme and the owner applies Wildheart by hand.

**Decision.** (1) **Wildheart is a site theme, not a font set.** `SiteThemeId` gains `wildheart` (lib/theme/site-style.ts), the editor gains `Wildheart` in `WEBSITE_THEMES` and `WEBSITE_THEMES_LIVE` (lib/sites/editor/state.ts) with its theme-contract entry, so the website editor's Theme tab offers it beside Frequency and Bold. Space themes stay typography-only (ADR-578): Wildheart's Space page wears the Classic font set, and its colour, shape, texture and faces live in the website skin alone, as Bold's do. (2) **Values live in one place.** Every colour, gradient, texture, radius and motion value is in `WILDHEART_TOKENS_CSS` (lib/theme/wildheart.ts) and the theme-contract entry; the skin (components/sites/wildheart-css.ts), the symbols and the calendar blocks read `--wh-*` and DAWN tokens only, so a token round is a values-only change. The handoff's exact values are kept: the seven neutrals, the violet set, madder and blush, the season fills and inks shared with Bold, petal radii 14 / 24 / 40 with the tucked bottom-right corner, the arch, one symbol ink, breath-paced motion. (3) **The faces load on the website only.** IM Fell English (the owner's files, next/font/local), EB Garamond, Hanken Grotesk and Noto Sans Symbols are registered in components/sites/wildheart-fonts.ts and applied on the Wildheart site root, never in app/layout.tsx, so no other route carries them (DEPLOY-SAFETY). (4) **The astrology calendar is a block family on the shared registry.** `lib/theme/astrology-wheel.ts` is the pure engine (the handoff's sun and moon approximations, read on UTC parts) and `astrologyWheel(year, items)` the one model; three Server Component blocks render it (components/page-editor/blocks/wildheart.tsx: the scroll-through strip, the SVG sky map, the date line), registered in lib/page-editor/config.tsx under one category the website surface offers, reading the Space's events through `metadata.space` like every Space block. The Wildheart chrome draws the date line under the header on every page. (5) **Never emoji.** Every glyph carries U+FE0E; the moons are SVG paths; lib/theme/wildheart-symbols.test.ts fails on an emoji-presentation codepoint without the selector. (6) **Nothing applies the theme on its own.** Only a saved `siteStyle.theme` of `wildheart` reads as Wildheart; a legacy font set never does.

**Rejected.** A `wildheart` Space font set (it would register the faces on the root layout for every route, and a Space page is typography only). A client-side turning wheel on Home (the handoff's scroll-driven rAF loop): the strip scrolls with CSS scroll snap and the sky map is static SVG, so the family ships no client JS; a turning wheel can return as a client island with its own weight gate. Program copy in the theme's defaults (a stock theme carries no Space's facts).

**Consequences.** `scripts/probes/wildheart-theme.mjs` holds the registration, the root-layout rule, the wheel and the generic-words rule for LIVE-902. The editor canvas falls back to Georgia and system faces (next/font is applied on the site page, outside the canvas iframe). The handoff's Vision and Calendar admin pages, its logo lockups component, and the 2027 program schedule are the Space's content, not the theme's, and stay out.
