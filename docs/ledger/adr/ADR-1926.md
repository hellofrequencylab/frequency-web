## ADR-1926: Consent evidence outlives the Launch it was given in

**Status:** Accepted 2026-10-28. Builds on [ADR-1111](DECISIONS.md).

**Context.** `launch_list_attestations` recorded the consent answer given before an imported email list, but its `launch_id` cascaded on delete, so removing a Launch (or its owner) erased the evidence the record exists to keep.

**Decision.** Keep the minimal evidence: wording version, answer, time and the original launch and source ids as plain uuids (`launch_ref`, `source_ref`). Foreign keys detach (`ON DELETE SET NULL`); triggers stamp the refs, bind a source to its Launch, and refuse any edit or delete; `service_role` can only read and insert. Owner reads end when the evidence detaches. Retention period, legal holds, subject requests and purge authority stay undecided and no automated purge exists.

**Consequences.** Deleting a Launch no longer deletes consent evidence. Privileged database owners can still bypass the controls, so a later purge is a deliberate, audited operation that needs its own decision.
