## ADR-1636: A split order records each seller's transfer as a row, creates it against the charge under an idempotency key, retries the ones that did not land, and marks a reversal Stripe made outside the product (LIVE-622)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · backlog `LIVE-622` (closed here) · child 2 of `PROG-D8` under [ADR-1565](DECISIONS.md) · stands on [ADR-1576](DECISIONS.md) (the funds-flow seam, `LIVE-621`) · beside [ADR-1377](DECISIONS.md) (the settle) and [ADR-246](DECISIONS.md) (the Labs ledger) · numbered 1636 because 1635 is the highest fragment on this tree (HYG-145)

**Context.** Re-tested on this tree after #3040: no file calls `stripe.transfers.create`, no migration creates a table that holds a transfer, and `app/api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts` acked every `transfer.*` event through its default arm. `LIVE-621` made a two-seller cart checkout as one platform charge carrying `transfer_group`. Until this row, that charge sat on the platform and paid nobody. A destination charge is atomic; separate transfers are N follow-up calls that each fail on their own, so the state of each intended transfer has to be a row of its own.

**Decision.**

1. **One table, `commerce_order_transfers`.** File `20270345009800`, not the `9610` the row named (9600-9649 is the D7 lane; 9700 is the funds-flow column this stands on). Columns: the seller (kind, profile, space, connected account), the net cents, the fee priced at checkout, `status` in planned / created / failed / reversed, unique `stripe_transfer_id`, `reversed_cents`, `attempts`, `last_error`. Amount strictly greater than zero. Unique per (order, account). On-delete restrict from the order. RLS on, no member policy, grants revoked from anon and authenticated, matching `financial_transactions`. A destination-charge order never has a row.
2. **One module, `lib/commerce/transfers.ts`.** `planTransfersForOrder` writes the planned rows from `metadata.split` (the shares LIVE-621 priced), and refuses a plan whose nets plus the platform fee do not equal the order amount. `executePlannedTransfers` calls `stripe.transfers.create` with `transfer_group` = the order id, `source_transaction` = the charge, and `idempotencyKey: "transfer:" + row.id`, one row at a time. A created transfer appends a Labs `payout` ledger line keyed on the transfer id, so the finance dashboard sees money leaving. A destination order is a no-op. `ensureTransfersForOrder` is what settle calls, after the paid flip and before stock decrement, fail-soft so a Stripe error cannot 500 a settled payment into a redelivery that flips no rows.
3. **The reconciler is a cron.** `/api/cron/reconcile-transfers` every 30 minutes, `cronBudget(40)`, heartbeat under its own name, opted out of the Healthchecks twenty because a miss delays a seller payout by one cadence and the next run retries the same rows. It re-plans any paid separate order that has no rows, retries planned and failed rows under eight attempts, and logs each row over the ceiling as stuck, one line each, never silently.
4. **The webhook reads a reversal.** `transfer.reversed` stamps `reversed_cents` and status reversed on the matching row. `transfer.created` is a no-op confirmation: the row flipped to created when we called `transfers.create`. A redelivered reversal of an already-reversed row writes nothing.
5. **The file is authored only.** Applied to no database by this change. The owner applies it after review with execute_sql then an insert into `supabase_migrations.schema_migrations` at version `20270345009800` ([ADR-1111](DECISIONS.md)). Until then `check:migrations` reads it as authored and never applied.

**Rejected.** Creating the transfer on the Checkout Session (Stripe will not attach N transfers to one PaymentIntent). Paying from platform available balance without `source_transaction` (the charge is the money). A per-line refund or a seller surface (LIVE-623 and LIVE-624). Raising the Healthchecks cap to monitor this job (the ranked twenty stay the twenty).

**Consequences.** A paid split order now records what each seller is owed and tries to pay them. A transfer that fails is a row with an error, retried for four hours, then a log line. A reversal made in the Stripe dashboard is visible on the row. Refunds still do not reverse these transfers (LIVE-623). Sellers still do not see their share (LIVE-624). The liability position is unchanged: the platform is merchant of record.

**Rows.** `LIVE-622` (closed here). `LIVE-623` and `LIVE-624` unblocked on the ledger existing. `PROG-D8` open, proven by its children.
