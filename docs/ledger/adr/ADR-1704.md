## ADR-1704: Two owner rulings prepared for approval: a protected Loom asset left on the default download policy is staff-only at the door, and the five help twins of the marketing guides point their canonical at the guide (SCAN-652, SCAN-673), and LIVE-693 retires by owner ruling

**Status:** Proposed · 2026-10-05 · backlog `SCAN-652` and `SCAN-673` (both closed by this PR, each with its probe passing) and `LIVE-693` (retired here by the owner's ruling of 2026-10-05, which is Accepted as of that date whatever happens to the two prepared rulings) · becomes Accepted when the owner approves the PR that carries it; if the owner picks another option on either row, the row is reopened and this entry is amended in the same PR · stands on [ADR-1594](DECISIONS.md) (protection and policy are independent knobs) and [ADR-1596](DECISIONS.md) (the download door) · follows SCAN-669 (the lead link from each twin to its guide)

**Context.** Both rows carry `ownerAction: ruling`: the code is small and the choice is the owner's. Rather than hold two P2 rows on a question, this PR implements the option an engineer would pick on each, states it here, and leaves the owner one decision per row: approve, or name another letter.

`SCAN-652`. `download_policy` defaults to `open` (20260920000000_library_dam.sql) and protecting an asset does not tighten it, so a protected master was one GET away from anyone who had its id, signed out included, and the picker hands the id to every picker user. The options were (a) protected and open reads as members, (b) as staff, (c) protecting sets the policy to staff unless the operator chose otherwise, (d) keep as is and say so in the drawer.

`SCAN-673`. Five member help articles under `find-your-people` duplicate the intent of the guides `/loneliness`, `/calm-down-fast` and `/friendship-as-an-adult`, each side self-canonical and in the sitemap. The options were (a) keep both, (b) a `canonical:` front-matter key on the five twins pointing at the guide, (c) retire the five with 308s.

**Decision.**

1. **SCAN-652 takes (b) at the door, not in the data.** `effectiveDownloadPolicy` in `lib/library/download-door.ts` reads a protected asset whose stored policy is the default `open` as `staff`; a protected asset whose operator picked `members` or `staff` keeps that pick, so the two knobs stay independent except for the default nobody chose. The written `library_downloads.policy` is the policy the door applied. The drawer says it in one line under Protected. (a) was rejected because LIVE-580 says a protected image is never its original, and a member is still anyone who signed up; (c) was rejected because it rewrites a column on a flag flip and is the data-side version of the same rule; (d) leaves the defect.
2. **SCAN-673 takes (b).** `lib/help/content-core.ts` reads an optional `canonical:` key into `HelpArticle.canonical` (empty for every article without it, so nothing else changes), the article page emits it as the canonical link, and `app/sitemap.ts` leaves an article with a foreign canonical out, because a URL a site asks crawlers to rank elsewhere does not belong in its sitemap. The five twins carry the key. (c) was rejected because the twins carry member-facing feature steps the guides do not; (a) keeps the competition.

3. **LIVE-693 retires: SMS stays transactional only.** Put to the owner as multiple choice on 2026-10-05 (keep SMS event-only, or add a lifecycle SMS category under the same consent gate), the answer was "Retire LIVE-693". [ADR-255](DECISIONS.md) stands as written: SMS carries the two event categories, dispatches and events, and lifecycle never goes to SMS, so automations keep email and push and no SMS action type is added. The P3 thread's SMS work stays behind its flag for the event categories only; DEF-A2P still gates every send.

**Consequences.** A protected Loom asset uploaded today and never touched in the drawer cannot be downloaded by anyone but the Loom team until an operator picks Members; the test suite pins both the tightening and the operator's pick surviving it. The five twins drop out of `/sitemap.xml` and declare the guide as canonical; their pages still render and still link to the guide first. Both probes pass on this tree; `pnpm check:backlog` holds the rows closed.
