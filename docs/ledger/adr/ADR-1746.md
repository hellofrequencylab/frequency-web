## ADR-1746: One admin IA on every surface, every catalog gate matches its page, Default Funnel is a Funnels tab, Your reach is Marketing

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-14 · owner-ruled (the five questions ADR-1745 put to the owner) · amends [ADR-1745](DECISIONS.md) (the consolidated IA) and [ADR-1313](DECISIONS.md) (Your reach on Content & Programs) · relates [ADR-264](DECISIONS.md) (the ten dashboard domains), [ADR-1090](DECISIONS.md) (Funnels)

**Context.** ADR-1745 made the operator console seven job sections and left five things for the owner: the `/admin` dashboard still drew ten ADR-264 domains (Programs, Content, Rewards, Community, Growth, Acquisition, Resonance CRM, Marketing, Vera AI, Operations); the member rail's Admin rows still read Dashboard, Programs, Operations and Market admin; Space "Your reach" sat on Content & Programs by an ADR-1313 ruling; the Default Funnel (`/pages/splash`) was a separate catalog row and page beside Funnels (`/pages/sequences`); and `scripts/check-gate-parity.mjs` held eleven `FROZEN_GATE_DEBT` rows, ten of which ADR-1745 papered over with a per-item `gate` override in `ADMIN_NAV_SPECS`.

**Decision.** (1) **Your reach moves to Marketing**, next to Reach: `space.reachreceipt` declares `hub: 'marketing'` and nests under the Reach box again (`parent: 'space.reach'`), so it and its box share a tab; the Space Marketing tab draws it as a tool card. (2) **The `/admin` dashboard is the console's seven sections**, same order, same labels: `ADMIN_GROUP_SPECS` is derived from `ADMIN_NAV_SPECS`, each card bucketed under its console group heading, so a page has one home on both. `AdminDomainKey` is `overview · community · content · growth · crm · vera-ai · platform`; the per-leaf `adminGroups` tags are gone. Every card stays reachable: Resonance CRM lists the Roster first (the console reaches it by its landing), and Overview carries no cards because the dashboard is the overview. The exec dashboard's area order and labels follow (Vera's read, Community, Content, Growth, Platform); cookie ids are unchanged. (3) **The member rail Admin rows carry the section names** (`lib/nav-areas.ts`): Dashboard is Overview, Programs is Content, Operations is Platform, and Market admin is Commerce, its console group, nested under Platform. Keys are unchanged, because `area_permissions` is keyed by them. (4) **Default Funnel is a tab of Funnels**: `/pages/sequences?tab=default` renders the same live-preview editor and save/reset actions (moved to `app/(main)/pages/sequences/_default/`), the `onboarding-splash` catalog row is removed, and `next.config.ts` sends `/pages/splash` to the tab with a 301. (5) **Every catalog gate matches its page**, fixed on the CATALOG side: connections admin; sms janitor + platform; nonprofit-verifications janitor; content-tips janitor; marketing-control-panel admin + marketing; crm-pipeline janitor; crm-marketing admin + marketing; page-layout admin; business-seeder and listing-seeder admin + structure. `beta-command` had already lost its row (2026-08-19). `FROZEN_GATE_DEBT` goes from 11 to 0 and the per-item `gate` override leaves `ADMIN_NAV_SPECS`.

**Consequences.** Visibility moves toward each page's gate: hosts and community staff stop seeing Creator tips, marketing staff and hosts stop seeing Pipeline, hosts stop seeing the Control panel; plain admins gain Connections, Page layout and the Resonance CRM Marketing tab; structure staff gain both seeders. Nothing a page admits is hidden, and nothing it refuses is offered. `lib/menus/gates.test.ts` pins the dashboard equal to the console, every leaf a card, the rail labels, and the redirect; `scripts/check-gate-parity.test.ts` pins the empty list against the real catalog through an `io.frozenGate` seam. `check:menu` frozen debt is unchanged at its count. ⚠️ `admin_header` renders from the DB when it has rows; a stored row still naming `/pages/splash` lands on the tab through the 301.
