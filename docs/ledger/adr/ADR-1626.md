## ADR-1626: A Circle, Hub, Nexus, Outpost or Channel room lets in only someone who belongs to its place, checked in the app before the admin-client write, and the invite door asks the same of the invitee (LIVE-651)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · backlog `LIVE-651` (SEC-5 of the Idea Inbox audit, docs/TASK-INVENTORY-2026-08-17.md §8q; filed out of DEF-DOMAIN by the replan, [ADR-1624](DECISIONS.md)) · extends [ADR-1149](DECISIONS.md) (scoped rooms require identity) and the SEC-1 private-room invite rule · re-tested first per [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md) · numbered **1626** as assigned by the orchestrator

**Context.** Re-tested on `main` at 3be160428 from the repo (no database access; how many scoped rooms exist in production was not measured). `joinRoom` (`app/(main)/messages/rooms/actions.ts`) selected `id, visibility`, refused `private`, and upserted a `room_members` row through the admin client for every other kind. `rooms_visibility_check` allows public, private, circle, hub, nexus, outpost and channel, and a scoped room carries its place in `scope_id`. RLS lets any signed-in member READ a scoped room's row (20270334000000 chose identity over membership so browsing works), and a `room_members` row then opens the messages (`am_room_member`). So the hole is not at the RLS or RPC layer: it is one admin-client write with no membership question in front of it. `inviteToRoom` had the same shape one step removed: any member of a Circle room could add an accepted friend from outside the Circle.

**Decision.**

1. **The gate lives in the app, before the write, in one module.** `belongsToRoomScope(admin, room, profileId)` in `lib/messages/room-scope.ts` answers whether a profile belongs to the scope of a scoped room. `joinRoom` now reads `scope_id` with `visibility` and asks it for the caller before the upsert; anything that is not plainly `public` must pass, so a kind this build does not know is refused too. No migration: RLS already does what ADR-1149 decided, and the write that skipped it is the admin client's.
2. **What "belongs" means, per kind.** Circle: an active `memberships` row in that Circle, the Circle's `host_id`, or an active `stewardships` edge on it. Hub: an active membership in a Circle whose `hub_id` is the Hub, the Hub's `guide_id`, or an edge on it. Nexus: an active membership in a Circle under a Hub of that Nexus, the Nexus's `mentor_id`, or an edge. Outpost: an active membership in a Circle under a Nexus of that Outpost, or an edge (an Outpost has no leader column). Channel: tuned in (`topical_channel_memberships`), the gate `sendRoomMessage` already posts behind. The walk goes UP from the caller's own memberships, so its reads are bounded by how many Circles one member is in, not by the size of a Nexus.
3. **Fail-closed.** A scoped room with no `scope_id`, an unknown kind, or any read that errors answers "does not belong". Staff get no override here: a wrong refusal is a member asking why, a wrong admission is a stranger inside a Circle's conversation.
4. **The invite door asks the invitee.** `inviteToRoom` on a scoped room refuses an invitee who does not belong to the place, with a plain line. Private and public rooms keep their rules (admin-only for private, SEC-1; friends only for both).
5. **Refusals use the canon's verbs.** You join a Circle, Hub, Nexus or Outpost and you tune into a Channel (docs/NAMING.md): "This room is for members of its Circle. Join the Circle first." and "Tune into this Channel to join its room." No em dash.

**Rejected.** Tightening `rooms_read_public_or_member` to membership (ADR-1149 kept browsing a Circle's rooms open on purpose, and it would not close this hole: the write is the admin client's). A SQL `can_join_room` RPC and a migration (the only writer is this action; a second copy of the place-tree walk in SQL is a second place to drift). Resolving the whole place's roster with `resolvePlaceTreeProfileIds` (a Nexus-sized read to answer a yes/no about one person). A staff bypass (not asked for; staff read rooms through admin tooling).

**Consequences.** ✅ `app/(main)/messages/rooms/actions.join-scope.test.ts` runs the real action and the real check on an in-memory place tree: a stranger is refused at each of the five scoped kinds with no row written, and a Circle member (tuned into the Channel) gets into all five; a lapsed membership, a scope-less room and an unknown kind are refused; a host gets in by the leader column and an Outpost steward only where its edge is; a public room stays open; the invite door refuses an outsider and adds an insider. ⚠️ The room page still shows **Join** to a non-member of a scoped room; pressing it now reaches the refusal instead of the room. Hiding the button for outsiders is an affordance follow-up, not the boundary. ⚠️ `room_members` rows written before this change for people outside a room's place stay until they leave; nothing here removes them (not measured; no production read).

**Rows.** `LIVE-651` closed here.
