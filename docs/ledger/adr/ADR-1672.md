## ADR-1672: Owner rulings of 2026-09-30 on the owner rows (HYG-156)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-30 · backlog `HYG-156` (filed and closed here) · owner multiple choice at ~01:35Z (the owner rows), ~01:45Z (the iOS readiness rows) and ~01:50Z (the P3 sort) · amends [ADR-1624](DECISIONS.md) ("Unpark all but launch") for the rows named below and [ADR-1650](DECISIONS.md) (the app readiness active on WM) for ten of its rows · no migration

**Context.** The OWNER section of `pnpm backlog` held asks that had waited for weeks, several of them unparked by the 2026-09-29 replan (ADR-1624) with nothing new to do. The owner answered each by multiple choice. Two answers are contradicted or qualified by a reading, and both are recorded beside the ruling rather than in place of it.

**Decision.** Each ruling, quoted, and what it does to its row.

1. **`LIVE-455`** (the Phase 11 six-week field test): *"Remove this field test".* Closed as dropped. check:backlog has four statuses (open, done, parked, blocked), so a dropped row closes as `done` with DROPPED in its detail and a manual probe that says nothing was proven. The start reading of 2026-09-21 stays in its detail; the PROG-R11 instrument (`scripts/maintenance/prove-it.mjs`) and the weekly sweep's Phase 11 reading stay, so the series keeps accumulating with no field test behind it.
2. **`OWN-011`** (Google OAuth verification for contact import): *"Park until 75 users".* Parked; revisit when Google identities in `auth.identities` reach 75 (the unverified cap is 100; about 32 on 2026-08-19). The row's own correction says the cap counts `contacts.readonly` grants (1 of 100), which is never more than the identity count, so this trigger fires early, never late.
3. **`DEF-A2P`** (A2P 10DLC SMS registration): *"Park until SMS is scheduled".* Parked; file at least 3 weeks before any SMS feature ships, because carrier review runs 10 to 15 days.
4. **`OWN-062`** (cost baseline and unit economics figures): *"Park until revenue".* Parked until the first paid production order (`commerce_orders` or `event_tickets` paid above zero). ⚠️ `LIVE-234` read one settled `event_tickets` row on 2026-09-21, so the trigger read literally was already met; the ledger reads it as the first paid order after 2026-09-30, and the owner is asked on the row whether that one ticket was meant to count.
5. **`LIVE-060`** (the quarterly moderated research round): *"Park until launch".* Parked to 2026-12-21.
6. **`LIVE-234`** (prove each money loop once in production): *"Wait for real customers".* Parked to the first real sales (Returning Home, 2026-10-23, or the first settled row of any unproven loop). Its probe is unchanged and it stays P0: the cost is unchanged and the owner chose when to pay it.
7. **`OWN-074`** (Temple of Aset has no Stripe Connect account): *"Already handled".* **Not closed.** A production read at 01:37Z shows the host of Returning Home (2026-10-23 19:00Z) still has `stripe_account_id` null with charges and payouts disabled. The ruling and the reading are both on the row; it stays open until the reading changes, and its earlier `parkedUntil 2026-10-01` note is replaced.
8. **`LIVE-273`** (the `SUPABASE_ACCESS_TOKEN` expiry): done. The owner rotated it on 2026-09-30 to `github-actions-frequency-web-2`, project-scoped to Frequency Community, read-only with the secret-bearing capabilities off, expiring 2027-09-28; both old tokens are deleted. Proven by CI, not in the tree: check:migrations on #3089's run at 01:28Z printed "Migration ledger parity: 749 repo file(s) ⇄ 749 applied row(s)". No probe can read an Actions secret, so the close is a recorded reading. The new expiry replaces the old one in `docs/RUNBOOKS.md` and `.github/workflows/maintenance.yml`.
