## ADR-1698: The practices table's row icons are 24px targets around unchanged icons (LIVE-737)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-30 · backlog `LIVE-737` (filed and closed here) · cause (c) of `LIVE-213`, named in #3109 · follows LIVE-643 (#3032) · no migration

**Context.** `/admin/content/practices` read 40 serious+ axe `target-size` violations against a baseline of 0, on desktop and mobile. LIVE-643 put `PracticePlacementButton` (a `p-0.5` button around a 14 px Compass, 19.1 px) beside each unplaced row's View link (a bare link around a 12 px ExternalLink, 12.8 px), `gap-1.5` apart. Neither is 24 px, and neither has 24 px of clear space, so WCAG 2.2's 2.5.8 fails both.

**Decision.** Both become `inline-flex h-6 w-6 items-center justify-center` boxes. The icons keep their sizes. The View link takes the same rounded hover fill the placement button already has, so the two read as a pair. The four `admin-content-practices` baselines are recaptured on the runner, since each row's title line is now 24 px tall.

**Rejected.** Raising the a11y baseline to 40 (a ratchet that rises for a regression stops being a ratchet). Moving the placement button into a menu (a bigger change than the defect, and LIVE-643 put it inline on purpose).

**Consequences.** Each row of the practices table has a 24 px title line, since every row carries the View link. The two controls axe named meet 2.5.8; pr-compare's a11y step on the PR is the reading.
