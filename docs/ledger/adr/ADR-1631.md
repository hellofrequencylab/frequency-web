## ADR-1631: The Welcomer unlocks from the welcomes table: a landed welcome fires a welcome_member event, and the criterion counts the newcomers a member was first to welcome (LIVE-653)

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-09-29 · backlog `LIVE-653` (closed here), filed out of `DEF-DOMAIN` by the `HYG-138` replan · builds on the Welcomes act of [ADR-186](DECISIONS.md) (P3b, `lib/connections/welcomes.ts`) and the currency rule of [ADR-139](DECISIONS.md) · corroborated by `lib/achievements.ts` (`isRelevantEvent`, `isCriteriaMet`, `countFirstWelcomes`, `getFirstWelcomeCount`), `lib/connections/welcomes.ts` (`recordWelcome`) and the pins in `lib/achievements.test.ts` and `lib/connections/welcomes.test.ts` · number assigned by the orchestrator · re-tested first per [ADR-1082](DECISIONS.md)

**Context.** Re-tested on `main` at `c8ad9fae2` on 2026-09-29, from the repo; the one production reading (below) came from the orchestrator, since builders have no database access. The Welcomer (`welcomer`, seeded in `20240118000000`: "Be the first to welcome 5 new members", criterion `{ "type": "welcome_member", "count": 5 }`) is not secret and no later migration deletes it. `isCriteriaMet` returned `false` for `welcome_member` unconditionally, `UserStats` carried no welcome count, and `isRelevantEvent` checked the badge on `post_create`, an act that says nothing about greeting anyone. The premise held. The act the badge names already exists: since ADR-186 a member greets a newcomer in a shared circle from the Pulse card (`recordWelcome`), which inserts one `welcomes` row per (welcomer, newcomer), unique, only for a newcomer who joined within 14 days. Nothing told the achievement engine that row had landed. So the row's own suggestion (a reply or reaction to a newcomer's first post) would have built a second definition of welcoming beside the one members already use. Production holds 0 rows in `welcomes` (orchestrator reading, 2026-09-29), so no member is already past the count and no badge is owed from before this change.

A second drift sat beside the badge. The member help (`content/help/the-quest/zaps-and-gems.md`) and `docs/REWARDS-ECONOMY.md` §4 said a welcome pays 8 gems, up to 3 a day: that is the `gem_config` row `welcome_member`, which nothing awards. The welcome actually pays `connection_settings.reward_welcome` (default 5) once per newcomer through `recordWelcome`. `docs/ONBOARDING-BUILD-LIST.md` §1.2 also still named `app/(main)/feed/chores-actions.ts`, retired with the chores overlay in #2510.

**Decision.**

1. **The act is the `welcomes` row.** `GamificationEvent` gains `{ type: 'welcome_member'; profileId }`. `recordWelcome` fires it for the welcomer after the insert lands and after the gem, awaited and caught, so a failed check never undoes the welcome. A refused or duplicate insert returns before it, so a repeat welcome never re-checks the badge. `isRelevantEvent` checks `welcome_member` on that event only; `post_create` no longer touches it.
2. **The count is "first to welcome", as the catalogue line says.** `countFirstWelcomes` (pure, exported) counts the newcomers for whom no other member's welcome landed earlier; a same-instant tie counts for both. `getFirstWelcomeCount` makes two reads (this member's welcomes, then every welcome of those newcomers) and falls back to 0 on any error, like the playbook and connector counts. `getUserStats` runs it only on a `welcome_member` event, the one event that can move it, so no other gamification event pays the extra reads.
3. **Nothing in the economy's code changes.** The badge pays its catalogue `zaps_reward` (seeded 25) through the existing achievement path, in gems because `welcome_member` is an online act (ADR-139, `currencyForCriteria`). The welcome's own gem (`reward_welcome` from `connection_settings`) is untouched. No migration: the table, its unique constraint and the achievement row already exist.
4. **OWNER RULING 2026-09-29 (~19:35Z): "5 gems, fix the docs".** The code stands: a welcome pays `connection_settings.reward_welcome` = 5 gems, once per newcomer, with no daily cap. The docs move to the code: the help page and REWARDS-ECONOMY §4 say 5, once per newcomer, and REWARDS-ECONOMY notes that the `gem_config` `welcome_member` action (8, 3 a day) is not what pays the welcome. ONBOARDING-BUILD-LIST §1.2 marks the chores reward retired and names `lib/connections/welcomes.ts` as where the welcome lives.

**Rejected.** A reply or reaction to a newcomer's first post as the criterion (a second meaning of "welcome" beside ADR-186's, and one a reply bot could farm). Counting every welcome rather than first ones (the badge's own line says first; changing the line needs a migration and a copy pass). Deleting the badge (the act exists and members use it). Reading the welcome count on every gamification event (two reads on every post, RSVP and log for a stat only a welcome moves).

**Consequences.** ✅ A member who is first to welcome five newcomers earns The Welcomer on the fifth welcome. ✅ The help page and the economy spec state what a welcome really pays. ✅ No backdated badge: production has 0 welcomes, so every unlock comes from a welcome made after this ships. ⚠️ Being second to greet a newcomer earns the welcome gem but does not count toward the badge. ⚠️ Once live, the badge pays gems it has never paid: 25 per member who reaches it, at the seeded catalogue value. ⚠️ The `gem_config` `welcome_member` row stays, unused; retiring it is a separate change.
