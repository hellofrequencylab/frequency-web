## ADR-1745: The operator console is seven job sections, and each link carries its own page's gate

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-14 · owner-directed ("Re-structure as many admin links as possible into the right areas", "Cull it down into a best practice navigation framework … be sure all surfaces are reachable") · amends [ADR-390](DECISIONS.md) (the section-gate stamp), [ADR-979](DECISIONS.md) (Loom Studio's own section), [ADR-927](DECISIONS.md) and [ADR-553](DECISIONS.md) (MENU-CONTRACT, §"The consolidated IA" added) · relates [ADR-827](DECISIONS.md) (Community Resonance)

**Context.** The operator console (`admin_header`, derived from `ADMIN_NAV_SPECS` over `STUDIO_LEAVES`) drew eleven top-level sections. Four existed only because `adminHeaderMenu()` stamped the SECTION's min + staffDomain onto every item inside it, so a tool with a different gate could not be filed under the section it belonged to (ADR-979 made Loom Studio a section for exactly that reason; QR Studio and Market are the same shape). Measured on 2026-10-14: 76 operator leaves, 56 reachable from the console sidebar, 20 reachable only from dashboard cards or deep links; two duplicate links (Market and Market catalog, QR Studio and QR codes, each pair on one URL); a stamped gate that offered every community host a dead Member Roster link (the page admits janitor + members). Three section labels (Dashboard, Programs, Operations) drifted from the Studio worlds the design and the Frequency console icons use. On the Space console, the CRM tab read "Resonance", not NAMING.md's "Community Resonance", and three tab blurbs named tools that sit on other tabs.

**Decision.** (1) `ADMIN_NAV_SPECS` is seven sections: **Overview · Community · Content · Growth · Resonance CRM · Vera AI · Platform** (the five §5b worlds plus the two janitor engines). QR Studio → Growth · Acquisition; Loom Studio and Elements → Content · Assets; Market → Platform · Commerce; Support → Community · Trust & safety; Segments → Resonance CRM · Audiences. Leadership (`/lead`) leaves the operator console; it is a host's own workspace, reached from the member shell. Every group holds at most seven links. (2) Every `STUDIO_LEAVES` row is now reachable from the console, each exactly once (the CRM roster is the CRM landing itself). (3) **Gates move from the section to the leaf.** Each item carries its leaf's own row gate, the one `check:gate-parity` locks to the page's `requireAdmin`; the eleven `FROZEN_GATE_DEBT` rows that surface here carry a `gate` override set to the PAGE side, so the menu never promises what the page denies. A section's landing link keeps the section gate. (4) The Frequency console shows a section when any page in it is reachable (not by the section floor), and draws each section's groups as sub-headings. (5) Space console: the CRM tab is labelled **Community Resonance**; blurbs name what each tab's rows hold. No Space row moved tab.

**Consequences.** `lib/menus/gates.test.ts` pins the seven sections, one-place-per-href, every leaf reachable, and the merged tools' own gates (Loom stays janitor + marketing under Content). `components/frequency/admin/nav-model.test.ts` pins the reachable-page rule. `check:menu` is unchanged (no catalog row added, frozen menu debt unchanged at 23). Visibility changes are corrections toward the page gate: hosts lose links their pages refused (Member Roster, Hubs, Nexuses), staff domains gain links their pages admit (structure staff see Hubs, members staff see Support). No route moved, so no redirect is needed. ⚠️ `admin_header` renders from the DB when it has rows; it had none on 2026-10-14 (scripts/maintenance/menu-drift.mjs), so the code default is live. Not changed, and put to the owner: the `/admin` dashboard's ten `ADMIN_GROUPS` domains, the member-rail Admin labels in `lib/nav-areas.ts`, and moving Space "Your reach" off Content & Programs (an ADR-1313 owner ruling).
