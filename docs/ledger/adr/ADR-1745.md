## ADR-1745: Public page cuts and the message: no "every week", "Find your Circle" is the people CTA

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-10-14 · owner-ruled · amends [ADR-1735](DECISIONS.md) (R7, the home subhead) and [ADR-1197](DECISIONS.md) (the approved CTA set)

**Context.** The 2026-10-13 public page audit read all 85 public routes against the Frequency design's message and found four pages with no settled future (`/stories`, `/the-quest`, `/discover/partners` and its detail, the `/vs` set), three merges that would remove duplicate pages (`/start`, `/the-lab`, `/discover/places`), and two message conflicts. ADR-1735 R7 had ruled the home subhead "Small groups that meet every week, near you. Join free. Businesses host free.", while the design's messaging guideline says never to promise or push "every week". The repo's approved people CTA was "Find your people" (ADR-1197), with "Join free" as the phone header form, while the design's people CTA was "Find your Circle". The owner ruled on 2026-10-14.

**Decision.**
1. **`/stories` is hidden** until three real stories exist. It leaves the header, footer, search, sitemap and llms.txt, and `/stories` answers a temporary 302 to `/the-community`. The route file stays so it can come back by deleting one rule; `lib/marketing/redirect-shadow.test.ts` lists it as the one PARKED shadow and fails if that rule turns permanent.
2. **`/the-quest` merges into `/features#f-zaps`** with a permanent redirect, and every link reads the survivor from `lib/marketing/merged-routes.ts`.
3. **`/discover/partners` and `/discover/partners/[slug]` merge into `/discover/spaces`.** The partners are its `#partners` section in the Frequency body. A partner has no link to a Space in the schema, so both routes redirect permanently to the directory.
4. **`/vs`: Linktree and Calendly are removed** (each redirects permanently to `/vs`), and **Meetup and Facebook Groups are added**. Every claim about another product is a public, checkable fact about how it works. No numbers and no fee rates appear. A claim that could not be stated with confidence was left out.
5. **No "every week", anywhere a member or a prospect reads it.** This amends ADR-1735 R7. The home subhead keeps its meaning without the promise: "Small groups that keep meeting, near you. People join free. Businesses host free." Recurrence options in the product (a series that repeats "Every week") are a setting, not a promise, and are unchanged.
6. **The people CTA is "Find your Circle" everywhere.** "Host a Circle" is the host verb and "Open your Space" is the Spaces verb. Together they are the approved set in `lib/site.ts` (`APPROVED_CTA_LABELS`, three labels). This amends ADR-1197. "Find your people" and "Join free" are retired as CTA labels. They survive as descriptions: "Find your people" as an eyebrow or headline, and "join free" as a fact inside a sentence. "Start free" leaves the operator slot and stays only on plan cards. The phone header's compact form is "Get started", which keeps the 14-character budget.

The audit's merges ship in the same change: `/start` → `/join` (`/start/[flow]` stays for printed codes, and `/beta` goes straight to `/join`), `/the-lab` → `/vision` (its dated plan and FAQ moved first), and `/discover/places` → `/discover/cities`, which becomes one town template that is indexed only above the local floor.

**Consequences.** Two merged pages land on Frequency-only routes. Their redirects therefore split on `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_DESIGN`: they are permanent to the ruled target under Frequency, and a temporary 307 to the nearest DAWN page (`/help/the-quest`, `/about`) otherwise. A later switch to Frequency is never blocked by a cached 308. `lib/site.cta.test.ts` pins the three labels and fails on any retired one in a template CTA. `lib/marketing/comparisons.test.ts` pins the comparison set and the absence of a weekly promise. `app/sitemap.test.ts` fails if any merged or hidden page is listed. Published page documents stored in the database keep whatever copy they were published with and need a re-publish or a hand edit.
