# Frequency — Teaser Infographic Brief (6 flows)

**For:** Claude Design. Paste one flow block as your prompt, or the whole file to batch six.
**What these are:** small "teaser" infographics. Each is a short arrow chain (3 to 4 nodes) that takes one real pain point and resolves it into one real Frequency mechanic. Not full funnel diagrams. The goal is that a skeptic glances at it and thinks "oh, that's actually clever," not "another wellness app."

---

## Where the magic comes from (read before writing any on-graphic word)

The voice is a camp counselor you actually respect: takes the person seriously, takes the activity lightly. Two rules do all the work:

1. **Proper nouns carry the magic. Sentences stay plain.** The world lives in the locked names (Mindless, Zaps, Journey, Circle, Spark, Dispatch, Trophy, Connect, the Market). The words around them sound like a friend texting. "You earned 40 Zaps this week" is the whole style. "Feel the current of your Circle's energy" is death.
2. **Never narrate the reader's feelings.** Name the situation, show the concrete thing, let them feel whatever they feel. "Day 3. You showed up again. That's the whole thing." Not "feel your commitment grow."

Then salt everything with **physical, specific detail and real numbers**. Frequency is an in-person brand: folding chairs, the drive home, five minutes before coffee, a poster stapled to a pole, the awkward first five minutes. Concrete detail is the antidote to kitsch. Numbers beat adjectives ("75 Zaps," "day 3," "+25% focus," "5 minutes"), never vibe-verbs (no "tap into," "drop into," "unlock," "elevate," "transform").

### Hard guardrails (all six)
- **No em dashes** anywhere in copy. Periods, commas, parentheses, or rewrite.
- **Sentence case** headlines and buttons. Contractions always. Max one exclamation point across the whole set, ideally zero.
- **Money is dark by default.** Payouts and paid memberships are flag-gated off in the product today, so **no flow ends on a literal $ / Paid / Sale node.** Revenue is the implied next step, never a claimed one. Business flows land on **Captured**, **Booked**, **Return**.
- **Two readers.** Personal flows speak to the **Seeker** (capable, a bit lonely, allergic to culty/salesy). Business flows speak to the **Latent Leader** (wants to gather people, needs rails, not a pep talk). Each flow tags its reader so the tone lands.
- **Proper nouns to keep exact:** Circle, Journey, Practice, Mindless, "tune out," Zaps, Gems, Spark, Pillar Trophy, Dispatch from Vera, Connect, My Contacts, the Market, Space, Host, Run. Lowercase the common verbs (scan, join, book, show up).
- **Format:** left-to-right arrow chain, one icon + a 1 to 3 word label per node, arrows between. One optional stat as a footnote strip. The six should read as one system (shared node shape, arrow, type scale).

## PERSONAL (reader: the Seeker)

### 1 — The First Win
**Headline:** "Yes, it's meditation. We made it a game so you'd actually do it."
**Card (8 to 12 words):** "Join a Circle, do one Practice, and the game starts."
**Chain:** `Join a Circle → Start a Journey → First Practice → Day 3: Spark`
- **Join a Circle.** A few people doing life on purpose. Joining pays your first Zaps, and Vera drops one welcome line, then gets out of the way.
- **Start a Journey.** A 4-week Mind, Body, or Spirit arc with one Anchor practice you repeat daily. The rails are already built, you just show up.
- **First Practice.** Five minutes before coffee. Logging it earns Zaps (real-world acts earn Zaps, that's the rule).
- **Day 3: Spark.** Three days running lights the Spark milestone. "Day 3. You showed up again. That's the whole thing."
- **Feature this (the magic moment):** the Spark at day 3. The whole teaser builds to a tiny, earned, non-hypey reward that proves the habit is starting.
- **Pain + stat:** most wellness apps keep only 10 to 15% of people to day 7. A fast first win is the fix.
- **Art direction:** warm, momentum building, each node a touch brighter than the last. The Spark ignites on the final node. Palette: campfire.

### 2 — The Focus Ritual
**Headline:** "Get out of your head, and into your life."
**Card:** "A five-minute timer you actually finish, and a nudge for tomorrow."
**Chain:** `Open Mindless → Tune out → Log the Practice → A Dispatch from Vera`
- **Open Mindless.** One timer, two modes: Be Still or Get Moving. A lotus mark, softly pulsing.
- **Tune out.** The fullscreen sit. Breath visualizer and a clock, nothing else. Timed sessions beat self-paced focus by about 25%.
- **Log the Practice.** "Tune back in." Your Airtime ticks up, your streak holds.
- **A Dispatch from Vera.** The reveal at the end hands you one small assignment for tomorrow. One per day, personal, never a guilt trip.
- **Feature this (the magic moment):** the reveal. You finish the sit and Mindless hands you tomorrow's tiny next step. The session doesn't just end, it points forward.
- **Pain + stat:** focus scatters without structure. A timed session lifts focus about 25%, and the reward here maps to real effort, not vanity points.
- **Art direction:** calm and precise. A pulsing ring or lotus motif. Quieter palette than flow 1. The Dispatch arrives like a note left on the table.

### 3 — Never Miss Twice
**Headline:** "One miss is nothing. Two is a pattern. We catch you at one."
**Card:** "Missed a day? Freeze the streak. No shame mechanics here."
**Chain:** `Log a Practice → Miss a day → Vera nudges (no guilt) → Freeze the streak`
- **Log a Practice.** The streak is running. Day 3 already banked a Spark.
- **Miss a day.** Life happens. One missed day barely dents a habit, the science is clear.
- **Vera nudges.** A plain, warm line. A fact or a five-minute invite, never "your Circle misses you 😢."
- **Freeze the streak.** Spend a few Gems on a streak freeze so one off day doesn't reset you to zero. The rule is "never miss twice," and the product actually helps you keep it.
- **Feature this (the magic moment):** the streak freeze. A real, buyable Gem sink that turns the cruelest part of streak apps (the reset) into forgiveness. This is the anti-Duolingo beat.
- **Pain + stat:** a single missed day is fine, but day two is where the old pattern wins. Most apps punish the miss. This one protects you through it.
- **Art direction:** the "miss" node muted or greyed, then the freeze re-locks the streak in cool blue. Forgiving, never scolding.

## BUSINESS (reader: the Latent Leader)
*All three land before money changes hands. Do not draw a checkout, a card, or a dollar sale.*

### 4 — No Lead Left Behind
**Headline:** "The room's full. Now none of them walk out as strangers."
**Card:** "One QR on the table turns a stranger into a name you can reach."
**Chain:** `Your QR on the table → They scan and RSVP → A few questions capture them → Roster you can export`
- **Your QR on the table.** A code by the door or on the flyer. Every scan is logged and attributed the second a phone touches it.
- **They scan and RSVP.** One tap in, one tap RSVP. No clipboard, no "give me your email."
- **A few questions capture them.** Your event Questionnaire asks what you actually want to know.
- **Roster you can export.** Their answers land in a Roster you can export to CSV, then follow up while the night's still warm.
- **Feature this (the magic moment):** the QR square dissolving into a tidy row of names and answers. The stack of business cards you'd never touch, replaced by a list you actually have.
- **Pain + stat:** 80% of event leads are never followed up, and about 88% of paper business cards are binned within a week.
- **⚠️ Accuracy note for the designer:** capture is via the Questionnaire, Roster, and CSV export, NOT an automatic sync into a CRM pipeline on scan. Copy must say "capture them" / "a list you can work," never "auto-files to your pipeline." (Auto-sync is a legitimate "coming soon" if you want to tease it, but flag it as coming, not shipped.)
- **Art direction:** physical-to-digital motion. A QR square by a coffee cup, arrow, a clean row of names.

### 5 — Be the First Responder
**Headline:** "78% of buyers pick whoever replies first. Now that's you."
**Card:** "They ask, a real conversation opens, your phone buzzes. That fast."
**Chain:** `They browse the Market → Send an enquiry → A DM opens instantly → You reply and arrange`
- **They browse the Market.** Your Service is listed as contact-only. No checkout, just "ask the Space."
- **Send an enquiry.** They hit one button.
- **A DM opens instantly.** A real 1:1 conversation opens the moment they send, seeded with their message. You're pinged in the app and by email, "you've got an enquiry."
- **You reply and arrange.** You answer first, while it still matters, and sort the details in the thread.
- **Feature this (the magic moment):** the message bubble arriving the instant the enquiry is sent. No form into the void, no 42-hour email lag. A conversation, immediately.
- **Pain + stat:** reply within 5 minutes and you're 21x more likely to qualify the lead. Industry average reply time is about 42 hours.
- **⚠️ Accuracy note for the designer:** this path is intentionally moneyless today. Payment is arranged inside the DM. End the chain at "arrange," never "pay."
- **Art direction:** speed and immediacy. The enquiry leaves, the DM lands, the phone lights up, all in one beat.

### 6 — One-Timer to Regular
**Headline:** "We don't measure screen time. We measure whether you showed up Thursday."
**Card:** "One event becomes a Run, and a Run becomes a reason to come back."
**Chain:** `They show up Thursday → Join the Space → A Run keeps them → They come back`
- **They show up Thursday.** Check-in marks them as verified, a real person who was actually in the room.
- **Join the Space.** They join, and now there's a home to come back to, not just a one-off.
- **A Run keeps them.** A Circle moving through a Journey together, with a Circle Meetup midweek and a Weekend Gathering already on the calendar.
- **They come back.** The next gathering is already set, so returning is the easy choice.
- **Feature this (the magic moment):** the loop. The last arrow curves back to the start, because the Weekend Gathering is already on the calendar. The reason to return is built in, not begged for.
- **Pain + stat:** selling to an existing customer works 60 to 70% of the time versus 5 to 20% for a stranger. Repeat guests are about 21% of the base and 44% of revenue.
- **⚠️ Accuracy note for the designer:** Space membership takes no charge today. Frame the payoff as belonging and return, not a sale. No revenue node.
- **Art direction:** a loop / return motif, the final arrow curving home. Show the calendar with the next date already on it.

## How to hand this off
- **One teaser at a time:** copy a single `###` block (headline through art direction) into Claude Design.
- **The set:** paste the whole file so the six share node shape, arrow, and type scale.
- **Copy layers, per Frequency's model:** the **Headline** is the skeptic-facing hook, the **Card** is the 8-to-12-word summary, the **per-node lines** are the depth. Use the headline + node labels on the graphic itself, keep the longer lines as alt text or a caption.
