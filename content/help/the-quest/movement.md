Looking for the separate timer for moving practices? It is gone, in a good way. Walks, runs, yoga, strength, stretch, and play all live inside [Mindless](/help/the-quest/on-air) now, as the **Get Moving** mode.

## One timer, two modes

- **Be Still** is for sitting: a meditation, a breath set, a quiet timer.
- **Get Moving** is for the practices where you are actually moving: a walk, a run, a yoga flow, a strength set, a stretch, or open play.

Same fullscreen quiet, same payout at the end.

## Start a moving practice

1. Pick a moving practice, or open **The Quest** in the header and choose **Mindless**.
2. Mindless opens in **Get Moving** with the practice's own length, ready to start. If you opened it yourself, switch to **Get Moving**.
3. Tweak anything you like, then start.

## It can arrive set up for you

Whoever built the practice can set the whole thing up ahead of time: the mode, the length, and for a strength practice the full workout (Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP, or a circuit, with the work, rest, and rounds dialed in). It opens preset and you just start. You can still change any of it before you begin.
