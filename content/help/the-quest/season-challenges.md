Challenges are the season's extra invitations. The main one is the Expression Challenge: a chance to carry a practice into life by making something, speaking, helping, or connecting. Expression can also be private. It does not require a public performance or a particular state of personal balance.

## Find your challenges

Open **The Quest** in the header, choose **My Quest**, and under **Explore** tap **Challenges**. The Season Challenges page lists this season's challenges with your progress on each. Finishing one pays bonus Zaps.

## What an Expression Challenge is

Each of the three seasonal Journeys (Mind, Body, Spirit) has one Expression Challenge attached to it. You complete it at the end of the Journey. It might be a reflection, a piece of creative work, a conversation, or something you make and bring to a Circle gathering. The prompt is specific to the Journey.

**The requirements shown for your Journey apply.** Existing enrollments keep their published contract, which may require this Challenge. Newer optional-expression policies apply only where shown for that enrollment. Your earned recognition and rewards are preserved when policies change.

## How it works

- **No signing up.** Once you have logged enough practice days on a Journey, My Quest points you to its Expression Challenge. Your progress card shows how it relates to completion.
- **Two ways to complete it.** Share what shifted in person at a Circle gathering, or post it solo online.
- **Progress is seasonal.** When the season resets, a new set of Journeys and Challenges begins. Your Gems, Trophies, and Amplitude are safe. See [Season ranks](/help/the-quest/season-ranks).

## What you earn

An Expression Challenge pays based on how you complete it:

- **In person at a Circle gathering**: +50 Zaps
- **Posted solo online**: +30 Gems

Sharing with your community earns Zaps because that act of presence is the whole point. Posting solo still counts and still pays, in the currency of online activity.

## The full picture

Finishing a Journey under its recorded requirements earns:

- **+75 Zaps**
- **A Pillar Trophy** for that Journey (Mind, Body, or Spirit)
- **Gems**, when the Journey's card shows a Gem amount on completion

Finish all three Journeys and you reach Master, the highest rank for the season, and earn the Certificate: a unique cosmetic plus 100 Gems.

## Do it with your Circle

If your Circle is walking the Journey together, the Expression Challenge can become a Circle moment: everyone shares their work at the same gathering. Your personal completion still records to your own progress.
