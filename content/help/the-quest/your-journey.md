A Journey is a guided set of Practices built around an intention. Mind, Body, Spirit, and Expression offer overlapping ways to explore what supports you. Every season of The Quest gives you three Journeys, and they run one after another, about four weeks each.

## Start one

1. Open **The Quest** in the header and choose **My Quest**.
2. Your season's three Journeys sit near the top. Tap the one that is open now. The others read "Opens later this season".
3. Start it on your own, or walk it with your Circle.

Every Journey in the Quest is free to start. The practices are short, real-world things you can fit into a day.

To browse everything else, open **The Quest** and choose **Journeys**. Outside the Quest, a Space can run a Journey of its own as a paid program. A paid one shows its price up front on the Journey's own page, so you always know which kind you are looking at before you start.

## Find your way around

Once you start a Journey, a bar at the bottom of the screen takes you between four views:

- **About** is the Journey's own page: what it is, the path, and who guides it.
- **Course** is your home base. It shows how much you have done, what is next, and every week with its lessons. A week that has not opened yet shows the day it opens.
- **Focus** opens one lesson at a time with nothing else on screen. Tap **Contents** to jump to any open lesson.
- **Library** lists every Journey you are on. The one you were last working on is first, with **Continue** one tap away.

## Journeys that repeat each year

Some Journeys run all year, one month at a time, and start again at month 1 when the year ends. You can join any month. Your Course shows which month you are in and which year, and every lesson you finished stays finished.

## How you finish

**The requirements shown for your Journey apply.** Official Quest participation has a recorded completion contract. Personal practice is not a test of balance.

Official Journeys generally ask for practice on at least **14 different days** inside the stated window. Those days do not need to be in a row. My Quest counts them for you, like "9 of 14 days". An existing enrollment may also require its Expression Challenge. Follow the requirement shown on your progress card. A newer optional-expression policy does not silently change an enrollment you already started.

Other library Journeys have their own structure. You do not need equal activity across four Pillars to make a practice meaningful. Recognition and rewards you already earned stay yours when templates or completion policies change.

## The Expression Challenge

An Expression Challenge can invite you to carry a practice into life: make something, speak, help, or connect. Personal expression can stay private. Official Challenge rewards follow the recorded completion routes:

- **In person at a Circle gathering** pays **+50 Zaps**.
- **Posted solo online** pays **+30 Gems**.

Your progress card says whether this Challenge is required for your enrollment. You do not need to feel balanced before expressing yourself. A private creative act is valid personal expression, even when it does not claim an official Challenge reward. More in [Season challenges](/help/the-quest/season-challenges).

## What finishing earns

The moment a Journey is finished you earn:

- **+75 Zaps**
- **A Pillar Trophy** for that Journey (Mind, Body, or Spirit)
- **Gems**, when the Journey's card shows an amount like "25 Gems on completion". You get them once per Journey, the first time you finish it.

Your season rank moves on its own: one Journey makes you Initiate, two makes you Adept, all three makes you Master. Finish all three and you also earn the Certificate, the season capstone (a unique cosmetic plus 100 Gems). See [Season ranks](/help/the-quest/season-ranks) for the full ladder.

Phases inside a Journey can pay a small Gem reward as you complete them, too.

## Do it with your Circle

You can walk a Journey alongside the people in your Circle, practicing the same things over the same weeks and doing your Expression Challenges together at a gathering. Nobody is ranked against their Circle, and the practices are better with company.
