Your rank is your standing in the current season of The Quest. You do not grind for it. You earn it by finishing Journeys, and there are only four ranks to know.

## The four ranks

- **Ghost**: where everyone starts each season.
- **Initiate**: finish your first Journey.
- **Adept**: finish two Journeys.
- **Master**: finish all three Journeys.

Your rank moves on its own the moment you finish a Journey. There is no Zap threshold to hit and nobody has to approve it.

## Find your rank

Open **The Quest** in the header and choose **My Quest**. Your rank and how many of the three Journeys you have finished sit at the top. **The Vault** shows the same ladder with your step lit.

## What finishing a Journey earns

- **+75 Zaps**
- **A Pillar Trophy** for that Journey (Mind, Body, or Spirit)
- **Gems**, when the Journey's card shows a Gem amount on completion. Those pay once per Journey.

An Expression Challenge can be part of a Journey. **The requirements shown for your Journey apply.** Existing enrollments keep their published contract, and newer policies may make Expression optional. A policy change does not remove recognition you already earned. The Challenge keeps its own reward: **+50 Zaps** in person at a Circle gathering, or **+30 Gems** posted solo online. Private expression is welcome too, it just does not claim an official reward.

## Finish all three: the Certificate

Reach Master by finishing all three Journeys and you earn the Certificate, the season's capstone. It is a unique cosmetic plus **100 Gems**, on top of your three Trophies.

## What resets and what stays

A season runs 13 weeks, then resets so everyone gets a fresh start.

- **Resets:** your season rank goes back to Ghost, and your season Zaps go back to zero.
- **Converts:** those Zaps turn into Gems at 5 to 1 on the way out.
- **Stays:** your Gems, your Trophies, your Awards, and your Amplitude.

## Amplitude: the number that never resets

Every Zap you have ever earned feeds Amplitude, your lifetime total. It only goes up. Hosting-class acts, like hosting an event or starting a Circle, count double toward it. Crossing big totals (1,000, then 5,000, and beyond) adds a permanent Award to your Vault.
