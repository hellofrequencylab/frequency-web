Mindless is where you do the practice, not just log it. Get out of your head, and into your life. One tap, the screen goes quiet, and it walks you through it. When you finish, the Quest pays you on the spot.

## One timer, two modes

- **Be Still** is for sitting: a meditation, a breath set, a quiet timer with a note field.
- **Get Moving** is for the practices where you are actually moving: a walk, a run, a yoga flow, a workout, a stretch, a game of something.

Same fullscreen quiet, same timer, same payout at the end. Pick a practice and Mindless opens in the mode that fits it, and you can switch.

## Open it

- Open **The Quest** in the header and choose **Mindless**.
- Tap the Zap button and choose **Mindless**.
- Tap **Start Practice** on a timer practice in a Journey, or start from a practice's own page.

Mindless opens already set to the right practice and its length, and it remembers your choices for next time.

## What it picks for you

There is nothing you have to choose. You still get a say over how you do it.

- **The practice is chosen for you.** If you have a practice due today, Mindless opens on it, so a tap starts it and banks the right log. Once today's practice is done, or if you have not adopted anything yet, it opens on **Free Practice** so you can keep going as long as you like. A "Logs as" line shows exactly which log you are banking.
- **On a Journey, its week drives the list.** Mindless shows the practices of your current Journey week plus the Anchor, the daily through-line, with a chip saying which week it is, like "Week 2 of 4". Finish or leave the Journey and your own practices come straight back.
- **Minutes.** The timer starts at the practice's own length when it has one, and you can change it. Two is enough. Five is plenty. The stepper goes up to a hundred and twenty.

## The Be Still modes

- **Meditate**, **Stillness**, and **Ritual** are quiet countdowns that frame the sit a little differently.
- **Breathe** gives you a guided breathing pattern with a visual to follow.
- **Journal** runs the same quiet timer with a note field, so you can write while you sit.
- **Just Log** has no timer at all. Jot a quick note about what you did, tap **Log it**, and you are done.

## The Get Moving modes

- **Walk** and **Run** are timed blocks, with optional nudges every few minutes.
- **Yoga** and **Stretch** hold and transition with a cue when it is time to move, so you can keep your eyes closed and follow the sound.
- **Strength** runs work-and-rest rounds and counts you through them. Tabata (twenty on, ten off, eight rounds), EMOM (a round at the top of each minute), AMRAP (one block, as many rounds as you fit), and a circuit are built in.
- **Play** is an open count-up: no target, no buzzer. Start it, do the thing, stop when you are done.

## Start, pause, finish

1. Tap **Start Practice**. A short countdown settles you in first, with the length you are about to do shown below it. Tap **Begin now** to skip it.
2. If the person who built the practice left a warm-up note, it shows on screen as the timer counts you in.
3. While you are tuned out, the screen stays awake and everything else gets out of the way. Tap **Pause** if life interrupts. Paused time does not count.
4. When the timer runs out, tap **Finish** to collect.

The countdown is five seconds by default. Change it to three or ten under **Sounds & Settings**. A practice can carry its own warm-up length too.

## If you stop early

Stop early and it still counts, as long as you got at least halfway: the day is logged, your streak holds, and you bank one Zap right then. The practice then reads **Continue Practice** wherever you would log it, so you can pick up at the time you had left and collect the rest of the Zaps.

Stop before halfway and nothing logs yet. Come back to it when you can. The practice is the point, not the clock.

## If the page closes mid-practice

A long walk with the screen off, a tab the browser drops: it happens. Reopen Mindless and it offers to **Resume** right where you were, with the time you had already done.

## Run a whole session back to back

Some Journey weeks group a few timed practices together, like a warm-up, the main practice, and a cooldown. When they do, the group shows a button like **Start all 3 as one session**. Tap it and Mindless runs them one after another: finish the first, collect its rewards, and it rolls into the next. A small "Practice 2 of 3" line keeps your place.

Each one still logs on its own and pays its own Zaps. Leave any time. Everything you finished is already banked.

## Breathing patterns

When you pick **Breathe**, choose a pattern. A Breathe practice can also arrive with its pattern already chosen by whoever built it. You can still switch.

- **Box**: four counts each way. Steady under pressure.
- **Coherence**: five in, five out. Nice and even.
- **Triangle**: in, hold, out. A simple steadier.
- **3X**: a big breath in, sip a little more, then one long letting go. The body's fastest reset.
- **4-7-8**: long exhale. For winding down.
- **Custom**: set your own counts, three to eight seconds a phase. Skip the hold entirely if that suits your body better.

Follow the rings: they grow when you breathe in, rest while you hold, and settle as you let go. The number in the middle counts each phase down with you.

## Sound, touch, and background

All of these stay off unless you choose them, and your choices are remembered.

- **Bell.** Rings at each phase change, or each minute on the plain timer. Pick its voice: Soft, Warm, Amber, or Bowl.
- **Vibration.** A buzz at the same moments, on phones that support it.
- **Ambient.** Under **Sounds & Settings**, pick a background loop: Forest, Ocean, or Drift. It plays low under the whole practice, fading in as you start and out when you finish.

## Cues while you move

In Get Moving you get audio cues so you do not have to watch the screen: a sound to start, sounds at each interval or transition, and a sound at the end.

On a walk, run, or stretch, hitting your target does not stop the clock. It rings once, then keeps counting up with a line naming the tier you have reached and how many more minutes reach the next one. The extra time counts when you tap **Finish**. Go well past your target and it asks if you are still moving. Tap **Still here** to keep logging. A yoga flow and a Strength set end with their last pose or round.

## The reveal

When the timer ends, swipe through four screens:

1. **Rewards.** Your Zaps land, then any bonuses stack up one by one.
2. **Streak.** Watch the number tick over, see how far the next milestone is, and check your banked freezes.
3. **Stats.** This session, your airtime, how deep you are on this practice, and your Amplitude.
4. **Dispatch from Vera.** Your next assignment: the next step on your Journey, a challenge you can close out, or where to show up this week. Find earlier ones under **Past Dispatches**.

One more swipe past the last card sends it off the screen. In a hurry? Pull any card straight down and the whole thing slides away.

## Questions

### Does a second sit on the same practice pay again?

No. The extra sit still banks airtime and your streak holds, but the practice does not pay twice in a day.

### Do I have to use the timer to log a practice?

No. Use **Just Log** for a quick note with no timer, or log from the practice itself.

### Can I use Mindless without an account?

There is a public timer anyone can run. See [Just the timer](/help/find-your-people/just-the-timer). Logging, Zaps, and streaks need you signed in.
