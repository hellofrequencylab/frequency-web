If you gather people, or want to start, Frequency teaches a small library of courses for exactly that. They are short, you take them at your own pace, and each one tells you how long it takes before you start.

## Find them

Open [Free courses](/courses). The **Free courses** link in the footer of the public site takes you there too.

## Browse the shelves

The library is sorted onto shelves:

- **Business and leadership**: for people who already gather others. What a night costs you, what to charge, and who came back.
- **Hosting**: for hosts, from a first Circle to holding a room with a co-host.

Each card shows the course name, a short summary and how long it runs. A course that costs nothing wears a **Free** tag.

## Start one

1. Tap a card. It opens that course's own page, where you can read the whole path before you commit.
2. Sign in if you have not. You need an account to start.
3. Start it on your own, or walk it with your Circle.

**The Business of Community** has a page of its own with more detail, and it is a good first pick if you are not sure where to begin.

## Know how a course works

Every course here is a Journey, so it works the way Journeys do: phases with short lessons and practices, opening one after another from the day you start. Finishing one earns Gems, and the course page shows how many. The tour of the Journey screens is in [Your Journey](/help/the-quest/your-journey).

Want more than the courses? **All Journeys** at the bottom of the page opens the full list.

## Questions

### Can I take a course with my Circle?

Yes. Start it solo and the phases open from your own start date, or run it with your Circle so you move through it together.

### Can I build a course of my own?

Yes. A course is a Journey, and you can write one. See [Build and share a Journey](/help/sharing/build-a-journey).
