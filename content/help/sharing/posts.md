Posts are how you share a moment: a thought, a photo, a question, a heads-up. A post you write from your home [feed](/help/getting-started/your-feed) lands on your own wall and the community feed. Post from inside a Circle and it goes to that Circle instead.

## Write a post

Tap the **Zap button** to open **Capture a moment**, or use the box at the top of your feed. Then pick a style from the row of icons:

- **Post.** Say something to your people.
- **Poll.** Ask a question and add two to six options to vote on.
- **Ask.** Put a question to your people.
- **Share.** Pick a Practice or a Journey you are on and share it with a line of your own.
- **Photo.** Snap what you're in the middle of.
- **Note.** A quick journal jot: what happened, what you noticed.
- **Connect.** Met someone? Capture their card to add them, or take a fresh profile pic of yourself right there.

When you're ready, tap **Post**. A Note saves with **Save note** instead.

Want bold, italics, a list, or a link? Tap the little **Format** arrow under the text box and the writing tools unfold.

## Know where it lands

Where a post lands depends on where you wrote it:

- **From home.** It goes on your wall and the community feed.
- **From inside a Circle.** It stays with that Circle, so you can be yourself with your group.
- **From a Space.** A Space's [Discussion](/help/groups/talk-on-a-space) tab is that Space's Circle, so a post you write there stays with the people at that Space.

Hosts can reach wider. See [Dispatches](/help/sharing/dispatches).

## React and comment

- **React** to show you saw something and appreciated it. It's quick, and your reaction lands the moment you tap.
- **Comment** to actually talk. **Add a comment** sits right under every post, so you can reply without an extra tap. The best Circles are the ones where people reply to each other.

## Fix or flag a post

Tap the three dots (**More actions**) on a post. On your own post you can choose **Delete post**. On someone else's you can **Report** it. See [Reporting & moderation](/help/safety/reporting).

## Tips

- New to a Circle? A short intro post is a great first move.
- A question gets more replies than a statement. Try **Ask** when you want to hear from people.
