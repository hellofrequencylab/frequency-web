Dispatches are announcements that reach beyond a single post: the way Hosts and leadership share something important with a wider audience. Everyone can read and respond to them. Sending one is for the people who lead.

## Read and respond

- You'll find Dispatches on **Around You**, the first shortcut in the left menu. The newest ones scroll across the top of the page, and **Dispatches this week** opens the full list.
- You can **like** and **comment** on a Dispatch, and **vote** when it is a poll.
- Something off? Tap the three dots (**More actions**) on a Dispatch and choose **Report**.

## Use Around You

**Around You** is your at-a-glance view of what's happening near you, not just Dispatches. Four counts sit at the top, and tapping one switches the panel below it:

- **Upcoming events.** What's happening near you.
- **Circles.** New Circles to join.
- **Members.** People new to the community.
- **Dispatches this week.** The latest Dispatches.

Under the panel are quick doors to host a gathering, start a Circle, or join a conversation in a Channel.

## Send a Dispatch

Sending a Dispatch is a Host-and-above ability. It's meant for the people running Circles and stewarding the community, so it stays signal, not noise. Everyone else shares through ordinary [posts](/help/sharing/posts).

1. Open **Around You** and tap **Dispatches this week**.
2. Tap **New Dispatch**.
3. Give it a title and pick a type:
   - **Post.** A straightforward announcement.
   - **Poll.** Gather quick input.
   - **Challenge.** A prompt to rally around.
   - **Article.** A longer read.
4. Write your Dispatch.
5. Under **Send to**, choose who gets it, then tap **Publish now**.

For something quick, you can also switch the post box to **Dispatch**, choose **Send to**, and tap **Send Dispatch**.

## Choose who it reaches

You can only send a Dispatch to a Circle or wider group you lead.

- **Hosts** can send to their Circle, or to its Hub.
- **Mentors** can also reach their Nexus, the wider region.
- **Everyone** reaches every member site-wide. It is reserved for the Frequency team, to keep it rare and meaningful.
