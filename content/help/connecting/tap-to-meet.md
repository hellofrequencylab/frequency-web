Running into someone from Frequency at a cafe, a market, or a trailhead counts. **Tap to meet** records it, and you both get something for it.

## Record a meet

1. One of you opens **Tap to meet** from Rewards. A code appears: a QR and eight letters. It lasts one minute.
2. The other scans the QR with their camera, or opens Tap to meet and types the eight letters.
3. Press **We met**. You both get 15 Zaps and land in each other's contacts, marked as met in person.

## Know the limits

- Once per pair a week.
- Three meets a day.
- Both of you have to be signed in.
- The code stays on the screen and runs out in a minute, so you both have to be there.

## Questions

### Why does it say Tap to meet is not open yet?

Tap to meet opens together with Regulars. If you see that message, it has not been switched on yet. See [Regulars](/help/getting-started/regulars).

### Where does the contact go?

Into **My Contacts**, private to you. See [Finding people & Circles](/help/connecting/find-people).
