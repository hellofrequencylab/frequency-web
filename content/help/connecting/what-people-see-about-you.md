Your profile shows what you have built and where you show up. It does not show everything to everyone. Three different people looking at the same profile can see three different amounts.

## What anyone can see

The things you have made public: Circles you host, Spaces you run, upcoming events you are hosting, and Journeys or Practices you have published. If it is already open to anyone on Frequency, it counts here.

Private and invite-only groups do not appear, and neither do drafts. A gathering you have not published is not on your profile.

## What a signed-in member can see

The Circles the two of you are both in. This part is about the two of you, so it changes depending on who is looking. Someone in none of your Circles sees none of them.

If you both belong to the same Space, they can also find you on that Space's **People** list.

## What only you can see

Your own full picture, on your own profile: everything you are part of, including the private groups nobody else sees listed.

## Change what shows

Where to find it: your avatar menu, then **Settings**, then **Privacy**.

- **Ghost mode** hides you from nearby lists, the Members directory, search, and a Space's **People** list.
- **Show me in the Members directory** takes you out of the directory and out of search. It does not hide you inside a Space you already joined.
- **Show me on leaderboards** decides whether you appear on your Circle's board and the season's.

More on the location side in [Your location & being found nearby](/help/connecting/your-location).

## Questions

### Why is a number missing from my profile?

If a count would give away something you have kept private, it is left off, not shown as zero. A blank is not a bug. It means there was nothing safe to say.

### What happens if someone blocks me?

You stop seeing each other across Frequency, including the shared Circles list. See [Blocking and deleting your account](/help/getting-started/safety-and-account).
