Know what it is called? Search takes you straight there, without browsing for it.

## Find it

Tap **Search** in the top bar. On a keyboard, press Command K, or Control K on Windows. Type at least two letters and results appear, grouped by kind.

## See what search covers

The chips under the search box narrow results to one kind:

- **People**: members by name or handle.
- **Circles**: by name.
- **Events**: public events. **Workshops & learning** narrows to classes and workshops.
- **Practices** and **Journeys**: the public ones.
- **Spaces**: businesses and organizations in the network.
- **Spotlights**: member pages.
- **Market** and **Listings**: things for sale in the Market, and Classifieds posts.
- **Posts**: public posts.

**All** shows a few of each, with **See all** when there are more.

## Jump to a part of Frequency

Type the name of a place in the app, like Market or Settings, and matching pages appear in a **Go to** panel beside your results.

That side panel also keeps your **Recent** searches on this device, and a **Try** row with quick event filters like **Tonight**, **This weekend**, and **Free**.

## Questions

### Why can't I find someone?

They may have turned off **Show me in the Members directory**, or turned on Ghost mode. Search respects both. Try their exact handle, and check the spelling.

### How do I find what is near me?

Search is best when you know the name. For "what is around me", use **Around You** in the left menu, or the Members directory. See [Finding people & Circles](/help/connecting/find-people).

### How do I search the help center?

Use the search box at the top of help. It only looks through these articles.
