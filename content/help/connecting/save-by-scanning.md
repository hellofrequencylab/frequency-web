Met someone at an event or around town? Scan their personal Frequency code and they land in **My Contacts**, ready for a follow-up.

## Scan a code

1. Ask them to show their personal code. Yours lives under **My Code**, in the **You** menu.
2. Scan it with your phone's camera.
3. Frequency saves them as a contact for you.

## See what gets saved

- The contact is filled in from their public profile: their name, and their title if they share it. Their photo shows because the contact is linked to their profile.
- It is stamped with where and when you met: the event or place if the code carried one, otherwise your city, plus the date.
- From their contact page you can add a note, a tag, or a follow-up reminder.

## Find them later

Where to find it: **Members** in the left menu, then the **My Contacts** tab, then the **QR scan** filter.

## Questions

### Does the other person know I saved them?

No. It works like keeping a business card. The contact is private to you, the person is not notified, and they are not added to any mailing list.

### What if I scan the same person twice?

Frequency updates the contact you already have. It does not make a duplicate.

### What happens if I scan my own code?

Nothing happens. You are not saved as your own contact, so scan away if you want to test it.

### Do I need to be signed in?

Yes. Saving a contact by scanning only works while you are signed in.
