Friends are your direct connections on Frequency. Becoming friends with someone is also what opens private messages between you.

## Find it

Tap **Members** in the left menu, then the **Friends** tab.

## Add a friend

1. Open the person's profile.
2. Send a friend request. They see it as an incoming request.
3. Once they accept, you are friends and you can [message each other](/help/connecting/messages).

Your Friends page keeps **Incoming requests** and **Outgoing requests** in their own sections, so you can accept, decline, or cancel one without hunting for it. A pending request just waits. There is no pressure either way.

## See who to reconnect with

When this is turned on for the community, your friends are grouped by how close you are right now: **Inner orbit**, **Middle orbit**, and **Outer orbit**. The grouping comes from real things you share, like Circles you are both in and events you have both been to.

- A friend who has drifted to your Outer orbit gets a gentle **Say hi** nudge.
- None of this is a score anyone else can see. It is your own private reminder of who to reach out to.

You may also see **People you keep crossing paths with**: people who share your Circles or events but are not your friends yet. One tap sends a request.

## Introduce two friends

Know two people who should meet?

1. On your Friends page, find **Know two people who should meet?**
2. Pick two of your friends and add a line about why they would get along.
3. Tap **Introduce them**.

They each see that you thought of them. When they connect, you earn Gems. Your Friends page also keeps a list of the introductions you have made and the ones made for you.

## See how you are connected

Open a friend's profile and look for **How you're connected**. It is built from what you actually do together, so you never have to write any of it down.

## Invite people in

Use the invite button at the top of your Friends page to bring someone new to Frequency. When they join through your invite, you earn Zaps.

## Questions

### Why can't I message someone?

You need to be friends first. That keeps private messages between people who both said yes. Send a friend request, and you can message once they accept.

### Can I remove a friend?

Yes. That is always your call.

### Can other people see my orbits?

No. They are private to you.
