Gems are the currency you spend, and the Vault Store is where you spend them. Any signed-in member can browse the whole Store and spend what they have earned. Spending is not a membership benefit: the Quest is a side thing we all do together.

## Find it

Open **The Quest** in the top menu, then **The Vault**. Your Vault and the Vault Store live on one page. **Open the Store** in the side panel jumps you down to the shelves.

## Read your Vault

The top of the page is your Vault at a glance:

- **Zaps.** What you have earned this season by showing up. Zaps reset each season, and the tile shows how many Gems they roll into at season end.
- **Gems.** Your spendable balance. Gems are kept across seasons.
- **Streak.** Your current run, your best, and how many freezes you have left.

Below that you will find where your Zaps came from this season, how you earn, your Awards, your Trophies, and the Vault Store itself. How the Vault fits with membership is covered in [Membership & the Vault](/help/membership/the-vault).

## See what's for sale

- **Profile cosmetics.** Borders and flair icons that dress up your profile.
- **Custom titles.** A title shown on your profile.
- **Collectible badges.** Some are season exclusives.
- **Membership credits.** Redeem Gems for free membership months.

A shelf only shows when it has something on it.

## Redeem an item

1. Find an item you can afford. Each card shows its price in Gems.
2. Tap **Redeem**. The Gems leave your balance right away.
3. For a cosmetic, tap **Equip** to wear it. Tap **Remove** to take it off again.

A few things worth knowing:

- You can own each item only once. An item you have reads **Owned** or **Equipped**.
- Some items are limited stock. The card shows how many remain, and reads **Sold out** when they are gone.
- Some items are season exclusives. They stop selling when the season closes, and a few retire on a set date.
- A few items ask for a rank first. Earn the rank, then redeem.
- Not enough Gems yet? The card tells you exactly how many more you need.

## Gift Gems to another member

Gems you have earned are yours to give.

1. Tap **Gift Gems** in the Vault Store.
2. Search for a member by name or handle.
3. Enter an amount and confirm.

The amount leaves your spendable balance and lands in theirs right away. They get a notification that you sent it, and it shows in their earning log too.

- You can only send what you can spend. A gift never takes you below zero.
- Your Amplitude does not change when you gift. Only your spendable balance moves.
- You cannot gift to yourself, and the amount has to be a whole number.

## Check how you earned

Tap **How you earned** at the top of the Vault. It is a running log of every Gem and Zap you have banked, grouped by day, each entry labeled with what earned it. Above the log sit your season Zaps, Gems, streak, and **Amplitude** (your lifetime total, which never resets), plus your four weekly streaks: Attendance, Posting, Hosting, and Activity.

The rule that organizes it all: online care earns Gems, real-world showing up earns Zaps (see [Zaps & Gems](/help/the-quest/zaps-and-gems)). If a number on your profile ever surprises you, the log is where the answer lives.
