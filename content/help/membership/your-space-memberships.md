Joined a Space and want to see it, or leave it? Every Space you belong to, paid or free, is listed in one place in your Settings.

## Find it

1. Open your avatar menu and choose **Settings**.
2. Open the **Membership** section.
3. Scroll to **Spaces you belong to**.

The top of that section is your own Frequency plan (Crew). The list under it is the communities you joined. The two sit next to each other so a payment you make to a community is not mixed up with a payment you make to Frequency.

## Use the list

Each row shows the Space, your tier there, and how it bills.

- Tap a row to open the Space.
- Tap **Cancel membership** to end one you no longer want.
- Tap **Leave the waitlist** if you joined a waitlist while a tier was full.

## Good to know

- A cancelled membership leaves the list.
- The Frequency home Space is never listed here.
- If you have not joined a Space yet, the section says so and offers **Browse Spaces**.
