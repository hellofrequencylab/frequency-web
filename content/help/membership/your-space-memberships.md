When you join a Space, paid or free, it shows up on **Settings → Memberships**. That is the list of communities you belong to, not the Frequency Crew plan.

## What you can do there

- Open the Space.
- Cancel a membership you no longer want.
- Leave a waitlist you joined while a tier was full.

Plan and billing is still your Frequency plan (Crew). The two sit next to each other so a payment you make to a community is not mixed up with a payment you make to Frequency.

## Good to know

- A cancelled membership leaves the list.
- The Frequency home Space is never listed here.
- If you have not joined a Space yet, the section says so and points you at Browse Spaces.
