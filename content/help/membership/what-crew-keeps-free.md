Frequency is free to join, free to find a Circle, and free to host one. That stays true because some members choose to back it. That's **Crew**.

## Questions

### What does Crew pay for?

Crew keeps the free version open for everyone else: the people who just moved, the people who can't pay right now, the person who comes to one Circle and needs it more than they'd ever say. It pays the people and the infrastructure that run Frequency, so the work stays member-funded instead of sold to advertisers.

### What do I get?

Crew carries a few extras: room to host more, Vera without the daily cap, one Boost a month, the Crew badge, and listing what you author in the public library. The full list is in [Crew](/help/membership/crew).

### How much is it?

You pick. Crew is contribute what you want: any monthly amount at or above the floor gets exactly the same Crew. The floor and the suggested amount are on the [upgrade page](/upgrade).

### What if I stop?

Nothing you've earned goes away, and nothing free gets taken from you. You can stop any time: open your avatar menu, choose **Plan & billing**, then **Manage subscription**.
