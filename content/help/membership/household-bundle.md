A household bundle is one membership payment that covers several people. You pay, you
invite the others, and each of them gets full member access on your plan.

## What you get

A bundle comes with **four seats** by default, and yours is one of them. So you plus
three other people.

Everyone on a seat gets the same member access your plan includes, for as long as the
bundle is paid. Their account stays their own: their own profile, their own Circles,
their own practice history, their own streak. A seat is not a shared login. It is their
membership, paid for by you.

## Inviting people

Open **Settings**, then **Billing**, and find your bundle. You will see your seats, who
is in them, and any invites still waiting for an answer.

Send an invite and the other person chooses to accept or pass. Nothing happens to their
account until they accept, and you can take an invite back any time before they answer.

**Invites count against your seats while they are pending.** If you have three seats open
and send three invites, you cannot send a fourth until someone declines or you take one
back. That is deliberate: without it you could invite five people for three seats, and the
last two would be told they had a seat and land on nothing.

You cannot accept on someone else's behalf. Only they can take the seat.

## Only you can manage the bundle

You are the owner because you are the payer. You send the invites, you take them back, and
you remove someone from a seat. Nobody on a seat can invite anyone else or change the
bundle.

## When someone leaves a seat

Their account does not go anywhere. They keep their profile, their Circles, their history,
and everything they have earned. What they lose is the membership access your bundle was
paying for, so they go back to what a free account gets until they join another bundle or
start their own membership.

Freeing a seat means you can invite someone else into it.

## If a payment fails

Nothing happens straight away. When a renewal fails, your bank and our payment provider
retry it over several days, and **everyone keeps their seat the whole time.** A household
does not lose access because a card expired on a Tuesday.

If the payment never goes through and the subscription ends, the seats empty together. Pay
again and you can seat everyone back.

## Questions people ask

### Do the other people have to live with me?

No. "Household" is what it is usually used for, but nobody checks addresses. A Circle that
wants to go in together works the same way.

### Can someone be on two bundles?

No. A person holds one seat at a time. If they are already on someone else's bundle, free
that seat first, then invite them.

### What happens if I already pay for my own membership?

Talk to us before you buy a bundle, so you are not paying twice for the same months.
[Support](/help) can sort it out.

### Can I change how many seats I have?

Not from inside a bundle you have already bought. The seat count is fixed when you buy, so
changing the plan later never re-sizes a bundle somebody is already living on. If you need
a different size, contact [Support](/help).
