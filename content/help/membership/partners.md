**Partners** are local businesses that back the community: the café that hosts your Circle's morning sits, the studio that gives members a discount. They support the mission, and in return members get real, in-person perks.

## Find the directory

Open **You** in the top menu, then **Partners**.

The page leads with **Offers right now**: every live offer, who gives it, and an **Unlocked** mark on the ones you've already claimed in person. Below that sits **All partners**. Each card shows the business, its city, and what kind of place it is. The directory grows as businesses join, so if your city looks quiet, check back.

## Open a partner's page

Open any partner to see who they are: a description, their address and website, and their current **Member offers**. Each offer spells out what members get and on what terms, so there's nothing to negotiate at the counter.

Some partners also run a **Loyalty** card. You collect a stamp each visit, and the reward comes after the number of visits the card names.

## Claim an offer

Offers are claimed in person, on purpose. Partners support real-world community, so the perk lives in the real world too.

1. Show up at the partner's location.
2. Tap their plaque with your phone, or scan their code. The in-app scanner is one tap from the Partners page, and your phone's own camera works too.
3. Claim the offer at the counter. You pick up Zaps for showing up.

See [QR codes & checking in](/help/the-quest/qr-check-ins) for how scanning works in general. Partner codes reward you with the offer rather than a verified practice.
