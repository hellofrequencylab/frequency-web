Stuck, confused, or looking at something broken? You have four ways to get unstuck, and none of them make you leave the app.

## Ask Vera

Vera is Frequency's AI helper: ask her anything, or ask her to help you find your way to an event, a practice or an answer.

- On a computer, press the **Ask Vera** orb at the foot of the right rail.
- On a phone, tap **Menu** in the bottom bar, then **Ask Vera**.
- Or open the chat panel at the bottom of the screen and switch to **Vera**.

## Open Help & support

The chat panel holds Messages, Vera and help in one place. Open it and choose **Help**. In some looks this is a **Help & support** link at the bottom of the panel instead. Inside you will find:

- **Search help…**: type two or more letters and matching help articles appear.
- **Ask Vera**: a plain-language answer in a real conversation.
- **Send us a message**: tell us what you need. The reply shows up right there and by email.
- **Your requests**: every message you have sent us, so you can pick a conversation back up.
- **Report a bug**: when something is broken.
- **Browse the help center**: the full library, by topic.

## Report a bug

1. Open your avatar menu and choose **Report a bug**.
2. Add a short summary, then say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
3. Attach a screenshot if you have one. It is optional, and you can paste or drop it in.
4. Send it.

The form lists the page details it will include, so you can see exactly what goes along with your report.

## Follow up on what you sent

Your support page keeps everything in one place. Find it under **Support tickets**, linked from Settings and from the help center.

- **Your reports** lists what you have sent, with its status and when it was last updated. Tap one to read the replies.
- **Still stuck?** has two buttons: **Report a bug** and **Message support**. A person reads every note.

## Browse on your own

Your avatar menu has **Help & support**, which opens the help center. Every article lives there, sorted by topic, with search at the top.

## Questions

### Is Vera a person?

No. Vera is AI, and the app says so. When you want a person, use **Send us a message** or **Message support**.

### Where do replies to my report go?

Back to the same conversation. Look under **Your requests** in the help panel, or **Your reports** on your support page.
