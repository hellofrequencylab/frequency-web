A Circle is a small local group built around what you practice. Joining one is how you go from browsing to belonging.

## Find a Circle

Where to find it: **Circles** in the left rail. On a phone, tap **Menu**, then **Circles**.

1. Open **Circles**.
2. Search by name or place, or narrow the list with **Format**, **Interest**, and **Sort**. The shelves below start with Circles near you.
3. Open a Circle to see who is in it, what they practice, and when they gather.

A Circle tagged **Featured** is one the Frequency team picked as a good place to start.

## Join and say hello

1. Open the Circle and tap **Join**.
2. Say hello in the Circle. A short note about why you are here goes a long way.
3. [RSVP to the next gathering](/help/getting-started/how-to-rsvp) so the group knows to expect you.

Once you are in, the Circle shows up under **Your Circles** in the left rail, one tap from anywhere.

## Know before you go

- **Online is always there, in person is the bonus.** Every Circle has an online space. Many also meet in person on a standing schedule.
- **Circles stay small on purpose.** Small enough that you are missed when you do not show up. A full Circle does not add a waitlist. It offers **Start a sister Circle**, so a new one with the same shape can open nearby.
- **Not every Circle has a Join button.** Some are kept for the members of a Space, some come with a membership, and some are ones the Host adds people to. Open one of those and the page says which it is and what opens it.
- **You can be in more than one.** Most people settle into one home Circle and visit others.
