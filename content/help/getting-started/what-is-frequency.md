Frequency helps you turn the things you care about into real-world community. You pick what you practice, find a small local group around it, and start showing up, online and in person.

## Get the idea in one minute

Most online communities are a feed and a hope. Frequency has a shape:

- A **Channel** is what you practice: movement, breathwork, holistic health, creativity, human relating, and more. It connects you to people everywhere who care about the same thing.
- A **Circle** is your people, near you. A small group built around one to three Channels, with an always-on space online and usually a standing time to meet in person.
- A **Space** is a place or organization on Frequency: a studio, a cafe, a non profit. Spaces host events and Circles of their own.
- As Circles grow, they seed new ones nearby, and a neighborhood of Circles becomes a whole local community. None of it is appointed from the top.

## See why it works

Frequency is built to get you off the screen and into the room. The Quest rewards the things that build community: showing up, inviting a friend, and supporting local life. The point is the practices, the places, and the people.

## Do these three things first

1. Open **Circles** in the left rail and find one near you. See [how to join a Circle](/help/getting-started/join-a-circle).
2. Open the Circle's next gathering and [RSVP](/help/getting-started/how-to-rsvp).
3. Show up. That is the whole Quest.
