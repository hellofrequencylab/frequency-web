New here and not sure where to tap first? You do not need to learn the whole place. Join one Circle, RSVP to one thing, and let the rest come as you explore.

## Answer the welcome, or skip it

When you join, we ask who you are (pick all that fit), then one optional follow-up in plain words, like "I'm new around here" or "Just a timer, no fluff." Your answer only changes what we show you first. It never appears on your profile and it is not shared with other members.

The welcome also asks how you heard about us. It is optional, and you can skip it.

## Meet Vera

Vera is the guide who keeps this place running. Right after you join, she uses what you told us to point you toward a Circle that fits. She is a bridge to your people, not a chatbot to hang out with: once she has pointed you somewhere real, she steps aside.

Where to find her: the Vera tab at the foot of the right column, next to your Vault. On a phone, tap **Menu**, then **Ask Vera**.

## Find your way around

On a computer, the left rail is your map. From the top:

- **Create**: start an event, a Circle, or a product listing.
- **Your Space**: only if you run one. It opens your Space's console.
- **Around You**: what is near you.
- **Events**: gatherings to RSVP to.
- **Circles**: find and join small groups.
- **Spaces**: studios, cafes, and non profits on Frequency.
- **Messages**: your conversations.
- **Marketplace**: the market, classifieds, housing, and the store.
- **Members**: friends, people, and your contacts.

Under those sit **Your Circles**, and at the foot of the rail, the **You** tab, with quick links to your account, notifications, help, and the site map.

The header carries the rest: **Community**, **The Quest**, and **You** menus, then search, messages, the notifications bell, **Zap**, and your photo, which opens your profile, **Settings**, and **Help & support**.

On a phone, the bar at the bottom has **Feed**, **Events**, and **Marketplace**, and **Menu** opens everything else.

## Work through Get started

The **Get started** box is your short setup list. It sits at the top of the right column, and in your feed on a phone. The steps: pick a name, add a photo, join a Circle, RSVP to an event, then host something of your own.

Open a step to see what to do, or tap **Later** to move to the next one. Steps tick themselves off as you do them. When every step is done, the box goes away by itself.

## Take on your Founder's First Week

The [Founder's First Week](/founder) page lists six moves that turn a sign-up into a real member. Do them in any order, at your own pace:

- **Say something.** Your first post: a hello, a question, a photo from today.
- **React to someone.** A heart or a +1 lets someone know you saw them.
- **Make a friend.** Connect with one person.
- **Join a second Circle.** One Circle is a foothold. Two is a life.
- **RSVP to something.** Say you will be there. Showing up in person is the whole point.
- **Build a 3-day streak.** Log a [practice](/help/getting-started/practices) three days running.

Each task you finish earns **Gems**. Finishing all six earns a bonus and the **Founder's First Week** badge. The page shows how many you have done.

## Relax, you cannot get it wrong

Nothing here is permanent or high-stakes. Join a Circle and leave if it is not right. Skip a step and come back to it. The point is to show up as yourself and find your people. Everything else is detail you will pick up along the way.
