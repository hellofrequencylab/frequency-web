New places take a minute to learn. Here is the whole map on one page, so you always know where a thing lives and how to get back to it.

You may not see every row described here. A few show only when they apply to you, like the door to a Space you run.

## The header

Across the top, on every page:

- **Feed** takes you back to your Feed. When you are already on the Feed, it reads **Home** instead. See [Your feed](/help/getting-started/your-feed).
- **Community** opens Circles, Events, Channels, Business Spaces, Members and Marketplace.
- **The Quest** opens My Quest, Practices, Journeys, Mindless, The Vault and the Leaderboard.
- **You** opens your profile, Journal, My Code, Orders, Partners and Join Crew.
- Then **Search**, **Messages**, your notifications, the **Zap** button, and your avatar.

## The left rail

On a computer, the left rail is your shortcut list. From the top:

- **Create** opens a short menu: **New event**, **New Circle** and **List a product**.
- **Your Space** appears if you run one, and opens its console. If you run several it reads **Your Spaces** and lists them.
- **Around You**: what is happening near you. See [Your location & being found nearby](/help/connecting/your-location).
- **Events**: find one, RSVP, turn up. See [Events & RSVPs](/help/groups/events).
- **Circles**: small groups that keep meeting. See [What is a Circle?](/help/getting-started/what-is-a-circle)
- **Spaces**: the directory of studios, shops and nonprofits. Want your own? See [Create a Space](/help/spaces/create-a-space).
- **Messages**: your conversations and rooms. See [Messages & rooms](/help/connecting/messages).
- **Marketplace**: Market, Classifieds, Housing and the Frequency Store. See [Buying, selling & sharing](/help/connecting/marketplace).
- **Members**: friends, people and your contacts. See [Finding people & Circles](/help/connecting/find-people).

Below the shortcuts, **Your Circles** lists the Circles you belong to, so each one is a single tap away.

## The right rail

Member pages carry a right rail with extras for the page you are on. At its foot sits the **Ask Vera** orb, next to your Vault. See [Get help when you are stuck](/help/getting-started/get-help).

## Search

Press **Search** in the header, or use the shortcut from anywhere: Cmd and K on a Mac, Ctrl and K on Windows. More in [Search](/help/connecting/search).

## Your avatar menu

Tap your avatar, top right, for the things that are about you and your account:

- **View profile**
- **Settings** and **Appearance**. See [Your settings](/help/getting-started/your-settings).
- **Plan & billing**
- **Drafts**: anything you started and have not posted.
- **Help & support** and **Report a bug**
- **Sign out**

## On a phone

A phone has no room for the rails, so a bar along the bottom takes over. It has five buttons:

- **Menu** opens everything else: your profile, **Ask Vera**, tiles for Circles, Messages, My Quest, Spaces, Journeys, Practices, Members and Around You, then your Space, the Create rows, **Capture a moment**, **Invite a friend** and your Circles.
- **Feed**
- **Zap**, in the middle, captures a moment.
- **Events**
- **Marketplace**

If you cannot find something on a phone, it is in **Menu**.

## Questions

### Where did a shortcut go?

Some rows only show when they apply. **Your Space** shows when you run a Space, and the Create rows show when you are signed in. If something you expect is missing, try **Search**, or ask Vera.

### How do I get back to the Feed?

Tap the Frequency logo or **Feed** in the header. On a phone, tap **Feed** in the bottom bar.
