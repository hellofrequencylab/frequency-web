Getting too much, or missing the things you care about? Notifications keep you in the loop without you having to check constantly, and every one of them is yours to turn down.

## See where they show up

- **In the app.** The bell in the header opens your recent activity.
- **Email.** For things worth leaving the app for.
- **Push.** Alerts on your device, once you turn them on for that device.

## Tune them in Settings

Where to find it: tap your photo at the top right, choose **Settings**, then **Notifications**. Changes save as you make them.

- **Pick topics.** Each row is a topic: Dispatches, Events, Replies, Mentions, Updates and messages, Practice reminders, and Roommate matches. The switches across the row pick **Email**, **In-app**, and **Push**. A dash means nothing sends that way yet, so there is no switch to set.
- **Events from Spaces you follow.** A separate email reminder for public events you have not answered. It is off unless you turn it on.
- **Notifications on this device.** Push reaches a phone or computer only after you tap **Turn on notifications** on it. On iPhone and iPad that works from the Home Screen, so [install the app](/help/getting-started/install-the-app) first.
- **Mute a Circle or Space.** Quiet one place without leaving it. There is one switch per place you belong to, and it silences that place everywhere. Your topic switches stay as they are, and you still see everything when you open the app. Confirmations, reminders, and cancellation notices for events you RSVP'd to still reach you.
- **Marketing email.** Its own switch, off unless you turn it on.

Everything sends as it happens. There is no digest option today.

## Change email from the email itself

You do not have to sign in to turn email down.

1. Open a newsletter or update from Frequency and scroll to the footer.
2. Tap **Manage emails**. A page called **Manage your emails** opens, with one switch per kind of email.
3. Turn a kind off to stop it, or back on to start again. Changes save automatically.

Newsletters and updates also carry an unsubscribe link, if you want to stop that kind of email in one tap. From the Manage your emails page, **Manage all preferences** takes you to the full list in Settings.

One-off emails, like a confirmation or a receipt, have the same **Manage emails** link, but it is not personal to you. It opens a page that says the link is invalid or expired, with a **Manage all preferences** button. Tap that, sign in if asked, and open **Notifications** in Settings.

## Know what always sends

Account and security email always sends: sign-in codes, receipts, password resets, and legal notices. Those are not part of these choices, and they are never used for marketing.
