Frequency runs in your browser, and you can also put it on your home screen. It opens like any other app, with no app store and nothing to update.

## On Android, and in Chrome or Edge on a computer

Open **Settings → Install the app** and tap **Install the app**. Your browser asks you to confirm, and Frequency lands on your home screen (or in your apps on a computer).

No button there? Your browser may not offer one, or Frequency is already installed. Look for **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** in the browser menu, or open Frequency from your home screen.

## On iPhone and iPad

Safari has no install button, so it takes three taps:

1. Tap **Share**, the square with an arrow.
2. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Frequency from your Home Screen.

On iPhone and iPad, push notifications only work from the Home Screen. Once you open Frequency from there, turn them on in **Settings → Notifications**. See [Notifications](/help/getting-started/notifications).

## When we mention it

After you RSVP or post for the first time, Frequency may show a small card offering this, once. It never shows on your first visit, and **Not now** puts it away. Settings keeps the option for whenever you want it.
