Frequency runs in your browser, and you can also put it on your home screen. It opens like any other app, with no app store and nothing to update.

## Find it

Where to find it: tap your photo at the top right, choose **Settings**, then **Install the app** in the list of sections.

## Install on Android, or in Chrome or Edge on a computer

1. Open **Settings**, then **Install the app**.
2. Tap **Install the app**.
3. Confirm when your browser asks. Frequency lands on your home screen, or in your apps on a computer.

No button there? Your browser may not offer one, or Frequency is already installed. Look for **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** in the browser menu, or open Frequency from your home screen.

## Install on iPhone and iPad

Safari has no install button, so it takes three taps:

1. Tap **Share**, the square with an arrow.
2. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Frequency from your Home Screen.

## Turn on notifications after you install

On iPhone and iPad, push notifications only work from the Home Screen. Once you open Frequency from there, go to **Settings**, then **Notifications**, and tap **Turn on notifications**. See [Notifications](/help/getting-started/notifications).

## Expect one small reminder

After you RSVP or post for the first time, Frequency may show a small card offering this, once per device. It never shows on your first visit, and **Not now** puts it away. Settings keeps the option for whenever you want it.
