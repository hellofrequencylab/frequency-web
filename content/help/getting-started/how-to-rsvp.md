Open the gathering and tap **RSVP**. That tells the Host to expect you and puts the gathering on your radar. It takes under a minute, and it is the single most useful first action you can take here.

## Find the RSVP button

Where to find it: **Events** in the left rail, or the **Events** tab at the bottom on a phone. A Circle you have joined also lists its next gatherings on its own page.

1. Open the gathering. Its page shows the date, time, and place.
2. Tap **RSVP** at the top. It takes you to the box headed **RSVP**.
3. Pick your answer.

Events also show up on Space pages. Tap one there and a small window opens with the basics and the same answer, so you can reply without leaving the page. Want the full details? Tap **View full event**.

Sometimes the box is not open yet, or has closed. A Host can set dates for when RSVPs open and when they close, and the page says which it is. Closing RSVPs only stops new answers. If you already said you were going, your own controls stay there and you can still change or cancel.

## Pick your answer

There are three, and you can change your answer until the gathering ends.

- **Going** holds you a spot and tells the Host you will be there. Tap it again to undo. Bringing people? Once you are Going, add up to five guests so the headcount is right. Some Hosts ask for their names.
- **Maybe** says you are curious without claiming a spot.
- **Can't go** tells the Host not to expect you.

Some Hosts ask a few questions when you answer. Only the Host sees what you write.

## Get a ticket instead

On a ticketed gathering the box is headed **Tickets**, and the button says **Get tickets**. Your ticket is your Going: buying it puts you in the room and counts you like anyone else.

- Tap **Going** before you have a ticket and it sends you to pick one, because there is nothing to record until then.
- Once you hold a ticket, **Can't go** tells the Host you are not coming. It does not cancel or refund the ticket.
- Some events have a price but still run on RSVP: first come, first served, with the cost paid at the door or covered by a membership. If the hosting Space's membership includes the event, answer Going and your spot is covered.

## Ask to join when the Host approves

Some Hosts approve each person. On those events the button says **Request to join**, and your answer is a request. It waits with the Host until they say yes, and you are not holding a spot until they do. We email you when they reply, so there is nothing to keep checking.

## Join the waitlist when it is full

Some gatherings cap their size on purpose to stay small. When one is full, the button says **Join waitlist**. You keep your place in line, and if someone drops out, the next person moves up on their own. Tap **Going** again to step off the waitlist.

## Know what happens next

You get a confirmation email with the details and an add to calendar link. Put it on your calendar. That one step makes it far more likely you will go. We send a reminder as the date gets close, never a guilt trip.

When you RSVP with just your email, the confirmation may show **Your next step**: one link toward the next room with people in it. It is a suggestion, so skip it if it does not fit.

## Questions people ask

### Do I have to RSVP, or can I just show up?

RSVP first when you can. It helps the Host plan (chairs, snacks, the room), and it gets you the reminder and the calendar link. For a free, open gathering, showing up unannounced is usually fine too.

### Does RSVPing cost anything?

Most gatherings are free and you just RSVP. Some are ticketed, and Hosts can price them as a set amount, pay-what-you-can, a sliding scale, or a donation. If there is a cost, it is shown before you commit. A few tickets are reserved for members of the Space hosting the event. The event page says which, and you can join the membership right there.

### Why can I only see the city, not the address?

Some events show only the city until you RSVP. The exact address appears once you are in. On the map those events show a general area, not a street, so you can tell whether something is near you before you commit.

### What does a number like 1+ on a map pin mean?

One event that runs on more than one date. Open it and the other dates are listed, so a weekly gathering takes one spot on the map instead of crowding out everything around it.

### I RSVP and then chicken out. Is that bad?

It happens, and you can update your RSVP so the Host has an accurate count. The real fix for cold feet is knowing what you are walking into. Read [what happens at a gathering](/help/getting-started/what-happens-at-a-gathering) first. It is almost always less awkward than your head says.

### Do I need an account to RSVP?

No. Open any public event and there is a short form: your email, and your name if you want to give it. Most ticketed events work the same way. If a rate costs money and the Host takes payments, the form takes you to checkout, and the receipt lands in your inbox with a link that makes you an account in one tap, if you want one. The one kind of rate that asks you to sign in first is a rate the Host has set aside for members. The event page says which.

### What does the confirmation email let me do without an account?

It says whether you got a spot or landed on the waitlist. It carries the calendar link and a **Manage your spot** link, where you can add guests, answer the Host's questions, or give the spot back if you cannot make it. Giving it back moves the next person in off the waitlist. That link works until the gathering ends. Reminders come to the same email address, and if the Host cancels, you get told.

### What happens to my RSVPs if I make an account later?

Signing in attaches every RSVP and every ticket under that same email to your account, so they are all in one place. That happens on its own when you sign in, whether the account is brand new or one you already had. Nothing is lost if you wait.

### Someone sent me a personal invite link. Is that different?

Slightly. A friend's personal invite link or QR gives you a form with going or maybe on it, and your details go to the person who invited you. The plain event page asks for less, because it only needs to save you a spot.

### Can I invite people to a gathering?

Yes. Every event gives you your own invite link and QR to share, and the people you bring can RSVP without an account. Bringing people who RSVP, show up, or join also earns you **Zaps** as a Connector. See [Events & RSVPs](/help/groups/events) for how the invite loop works.

### Why did I get "Did you make it?" after a gathering?

The day after a gathering, everyone who said yes gets one short note asking whether they made it. Tap **Yes, I made it** and the Host knows you came. The note also shows when the group meets next, with a link you can send a friend. Members can turn these off with the **Events** email switch under **Settings**, then **Notifications**.

### I RSVPd without an account. Will you email me again?

Once after the gathering: the same "Did you make it?" note, with a button to join free. Joining moves your RSVP into your account, so the next date lands on your calendar. If you do not join, we do not email that address again about it.
