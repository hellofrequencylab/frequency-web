Your feed is home: what is happening with the Circles and people you have joined, plus the next thing worth showing up to. It is not an endless scroll of strangers, and it ends when you are caught up.

## Find it

Tap the Frequency logo, or **Feed** in the header. On a phone, **Feed** is the first tab in the bar at the bottom.

## Read it from the top

The feed runs in this order:

1. **A greeting**, with the date and your area, and a **Search the Feed** box for finding a post.
2. **Get started**, while you still have setup steps left. On a wide screen it sits at the top of the right column instead. See [Your first week](/help/getting-started/your-first-week).
3. **Your shortcuts**: your Circles (or the Spaces you run), Events, Messages, and Practices.
4. **Up next for you**: one pick for something to go to.
5. **Needs you**, when something is waiting on an answer from you.
6. **Find your people**: Circles to look at, shown near the top until you have joined a few.
7. **The box where you post**, then the posts themselves.

On a wide screen, the right column carries your stats and streaks, **Your week**, **Today's goals**, **People to know**, and **Coming up**. On a phone the same panels sit in the feed.

## Choose a lens

Tabs above the posts change the order:

- **Nearby** shows the closest activity. When your area is quiet, it widens on its own. It uses your approximate area, never your exact address, and it only appears once you have set a home base.
- **Resonance** is the default. It blends what is near you, who you resonate with, and what is fresh.
- **Recent** is the plain newest-first stream.
- **Popular** sorts by what people are reacting to and talking about most.
- **Story** is a record of what the community lived, not a scroll to consume. Your own captured moments collect in [your journal](/journal).

## Reach the end

When you have seen what is new, the feed says **You're caught up** and points you at something to go to. That is on purpose. There is no endless scroll.

## Make it yours

- Join more [Circles](/help/getting-started/join-a-circle) to bring more life into your feed.
- React and comment. A feed gets good when people talk to each other.
- Quiet feed? That usually means it is time to join another Circle or show up to an event in person.
- Want to change how far Nearby reaches, or hide the short reason on each post? Open **Settings**, then **Feed**. See [Your settings](/help/getting-started/your-settings).
