Getting more email than you want? You can turn off one kind and keep the rest. Settings always works. Newsletters and updates also carry a shortcut at the bottom of the email.

## Change it in Settings

This path works for every kind of email, every time.

1. Sign in, open your avatar menu and choose **Settings**.
2. Go to the Notifications section.
3. Turn each kind of email on or off. The same grid covers in-app and push notifications, and you can mute a single Circle or Space there too.

The full tour is in [Notifications](/help/getting-started/notifications).

## Use the link in a newsletter or update

Newsletters and updates from Frequency carry a personal **Manage emails** link at the bottom.

1. Scroll to the bottom of the email and tap **Manage emails**.
2. A page called "Manage your emails" opens. You do not need to sign in: the link is yours alone, so do not forward it.
3. Flip the switch next to each kind of email. Changes save automatically.

Other emails, like a reply to a conversation, also show **Manage emails** in the footer, but that link is not personal. It opens a page that says "This link is invalid or expired." with a **Manage all preferences** button. Press it, sign in, and you land in Settings.

## Know what each switch covers

On the email page, you only see switches for email that Frequency can send you today.

- **Dispatches**: Dispatch posts from your Hosts and the wider community.
- **Events**: RSVP changes and reminders before an event starts.
- **Mentions**: when someone @mentions you.
- **Updates and messages**: check-ins after you join, notes from your Hosts and leaders, and newsletters.
- **Housing matches**: when a new room or roommate lines up with what you are looking for.

Turned one off by mistake? Open the page again and turn it back on.

## Unsubscribe from one kind in one go

Where a newsletter or update has an **Unsubscribe** link, that is the fast lane. It opens a page that asks you to confirm, and nothing changes until you press **Unsubscribe**. It stops only that kind of email. Everything else stays as it is.

## Quiet one Space

An email from a Space has its own unsubscribe link. It opens a page for that sender only, where you can keep some topics and drop others, or choose **Unsubscribe from everything**. This does not change what Frequency itself sends you.

## Questions

### The page says "This link is invalid or expired." What now?

Either the link is old, or that email did not carry a personal link. Press **Manage all preferences**, sign in, and change your emails in Settings instead. If you cannot sign in, reply to one of our emails and we will help.

### Do I have to sign in to stop an email?

Not from a newsletter or update: its **Manage emails** and **Unsubscribe** links work on their own. For anything else, sign in and use Settings.
