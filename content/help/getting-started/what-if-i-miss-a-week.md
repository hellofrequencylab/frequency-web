Nothing bad happens. Miss a gathering, miss a week, miss a month: you are still a member, your Circle is still there, and you pick back up whenever you are ready. Frequency has no penalty for stepping away.

## Understand what Ghost means

Ghost is your season rank when you have not finished a Journey yet. It is the starting point, not a scolding. Everyone begins each season as a Ghost. You move up to Initiate when you finish your first Journey, and on from there. If a season goes by and you do not finish a Journey, you stay Ghost. That is a status, not a strike. It costs you nothing.

There is no "inactive" flag that locks you out, no demotion, and no public list of who has gone quiet.

## Know you are missed, not owed

Circles stay small so that you are missed when you are not there. That is the point of a small group: your seat is yours, and people notice it is empty. But "missed" means "we are glad when you come back," not "you owe us." A good Circle welcomes you back without a word about where you have been.

## Keep what you earned

Your durable stuff is safe. Here is what does and does not carry:

- **Gems, Trophies, and Amplitude** never reset. They are yours for good.
- **A streak** can break if you miss your daily practice, but a broken streak just starts a new one. It is a counter, not a judgment, and a banked freeze can bridge a missed day. See [Streaks](/help/the-quest/streaks).
- **Season Zaps** reset at the end of each 13-week season anyway, the same for everyone. Missing time does not single you out.

Nothing of real value disappears because you took a week off.

## Come back

1. Open **Events** in the left rail, or your Circle under **Your Circles**.
2. RSVP to the next gathering.
3. Show up.

That is the entire re-entry process. You do not have to explain, apologize, or catch up on anything. If your Journey is mid-stream, open **The Quest** in the header, choose **My Quest**, and pick up the next practice where you left off.

## Questions people ask

### Will I get nagging notifications if I go quiet?

No. Notifications here carry a fact (a gathering tonight, Zaps you earned) or a short invitation, never guilt, shame, or fake urgency. If you want fewer, turn them down under **Settings**, then **Notifications**. See [Notifications](/help/getting-started/notifications).

### Is it too late to come back if it has been months?

It is never too late. The season you left may have ended and a new one started, which just means a fresh climb. Your Circle, your friends, and your durable rewards are still there. Pick a gathering and show up.

### Do I get demoted for being a Ghost?

There is nothing below Ghost to be demoted to. It is the entry point. Ranks only go up within a season, and they move on their own when you finish Journeys. See [season ranks](/help/the-quest/season-ranks) for the full ladder.
