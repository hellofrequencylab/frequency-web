Go to the same cafe or studio a lot? Some Spaces with a real door run **Regulars**, a stamp card for showing up. Each visit you check in at their code, you get one stamp and 25 Zaps. When the card is full, you get the reward the place chose, as a six-character code you show at the counter.

Regulars is opening place by place. A Space that runs it says so on its page, with a line that starts **Regulars here**.

## Check in

1. At the door, scan the Space's code with your camera, or tap your phone on its tag.
2. Sign in if you are not already.
3. You see your card, and how many visits are left to the reward.

One stamp a day per Space. Check-ins at up to three Spaces a day earn Zaps.

## Find your cards

Your cards live on your [Rewards](/rewards) page. A Space that runs Regulars links to it from its own page, under **Your cards**.

Every card you hold is there, with any reward code waiting to be collected. Under the cards you see the places running Regulars nearest you. No cards yet? Tap **Find a place**.

## Join a hunt

A few Spaces can run a hunt together for a few weeks. Check in at every stop inside the window and the host's reward is yours, plus 100 Zaps. Hunts are still rolling out, so you may not see one yet. When one is live, it shows on Rewards with its stops, and your card there counts them down.

## Read the small print

- A place can change its reward, but a reward you already earned keeps its title and value.
- Your location is read only at the moment you check in, and only when the place asked for it.
