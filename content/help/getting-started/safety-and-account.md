Someone bothering you, or ready to leave? You are in control of who can reach you and of your account. Here is where each control lives.

## Keep your location private

Frequency never shares your exact location. When the app helps people find others nearby, it shows a fuzzed area about a mile across, or your city, never a precise spot on a map.

Where to find it: tap your photo at the top right, choose **Settings**, then **Privacy**. There you decide who can find you nearby, how precise that is, and how far away they can be. Turn on **Ghost mode** any time to disappear from proximity and maps. See [Your settings](/help/getting-started/your-settings).

## Block a member

Open the member's profile and choose **Block**. When you block someone:

- neither of you can start a direct message with the other,
- neither of you can send the other a friend request,
- you are removed as friends,
- they can no longer reply to your posts, mention you, or write on your wall, and
- you stop seeing each other in messages, friends, events, and the market.

## Undo a block

1. Open **Settings**, then **Account**.
2. Scroll to **Blocked members**.
3. Tap **Unblock** beside the person.

## Download your data

In **Settings**, then **Account**, find **Download your data** and tap **Download my data**. The file saves to your downloads.

## Erase your saved drafts

Anything you start in a create flow saves as a draft so you can come back to it. To clear them:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Account**, and scroll to **Erase your saved drafts**.
2. Type ERASE to confirm.
3. Tap **Erase drafts**.

This removes your unfinished drafts only. Anything you already published stays where it is. It cannot be undone.

## Delete your account

1. Open **Settings**, then **Account**, and scroll to **Delete your account**.
2. Type DELETE to confirm.
3. Tap **Delete account**.

This permanently removes your account and your content, and it cannot be undone. If you own a Space on a paid plan, the page tells you that deleting your account ends that plan too. You are signed out right afterward.
