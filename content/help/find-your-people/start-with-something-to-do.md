You don't have to sign up to "find your people." Most people who end up with a crew didn't go looking for one. They went for a run, then went again the next week, and the same faces kept showing up.

## How do I find something to do near me?

1. Open [Events](/events) from the left rail.
2. Narrow it down. Set **Date** to **This week**, and use **Category** to pick the kind of thing you're after, like **Movement** for a run or a walk, or **Social** for a game night.
3. Pick one with a real time and a real place, open it, and tap **Going**.

What you'll find depends on what hosts near you have put on the calendar: a run, a walk, a pickup game, a sauna or cold plunge night, a sober social, a game night.

If you'd rather have something on a standing day, look at [Circles](/circles). A run or walk Circle that meets in person usually keeps a standing time, so you always know where to be.

## Do I have to talk to anyone?

Not much. That's the point of starting with an activity. You run, you play, you sit in the heat. Conversation shows up in the gaps, and it's easier when there's something to do with your hands.

## What if nothing is on near me?

You have two good options:

- Go on your own this week. Open [Mindless](/on-air), switch to **Get Moving**, and time a walk or a run. Check Events again next week.
- Host one. A standing Saturday run is about the simplest thing to start. For a one-off, open **Create** and choose **New event**. For a group that meets every week, see [How to start a Circle](/help/leading/how-to-start-a-circle).

## How do I keep going?

Go back the next week. Same time, same place. Give it three weeks, and faces start to turn into names.
