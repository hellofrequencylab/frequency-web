You don't have to sign up to "find your people." Most people who end up with a crew didn't go looking for one. They went for a run, then went again the next week, and the same faces kept showing up.

## How do I find something to do near me?

Open [Events](/events) and look at this week. Filter by what you're into: a run, a walk, a pickup game, a sauna or cold plunge night, a sober social, a game night. Pick one with a real time and a real place, and tap **Going**.

If you'd rather have something every week, look at [Circles](/circles). A run or walk Circle meets at a standing time, so you always know where to be.

## Do I have to talk to anyone?

Not much. That's the point of starting with an activity. You run, you play, you sit in the heat. Conversation shows up in the gaps, and it's easier when there's something to do with your hands.

## What if nothing is on near me?

Use **Get Moving** in [Mindless](/on-air) to time a walk or a run on your own, and check Events again next week. Or host one: a standing Saturday run is the simplest thing to start. See [How to start a Circle](/help/leading/how-to-start-a-circle).

## How do I keep going?

Go back the next week. Same time, same place. By the third week people know your name.
