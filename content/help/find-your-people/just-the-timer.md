Some people want a timer and nothing else. No streaks to protect, no badges, no chat. Frequency can be that.

## Where is the timer?

It's called **Mindless**. There are two ways in:

- Tap the Zap button and choose the **Mindless** row.
- Or go straight to [Mindless](/on-air). It also sits in the header under **The Quest**.

Then:

1. Pick **Be Still** for a sit or a breathing round, or **Get Moving** for a walk or a run.
2. Set the minutes.
3. Tap **Start Practice**.

Not signed in? The [5-minute breathing timer](/mindless) works without an account and saves nothing.

## Which breathing pattern should I use?

Keep it simple. Breathe in for 4 and out for 6, for a few minutes. Or try the even one: in for 5, out for 5, which works out to about six breaths a minute. In Mindless that even pattern is called Coherence. Neither needs anything but a quiet minute.

## Do I have to do the game part?

No. Your minutes are logged as Airtime, and a finished session earns Zaps whether you look at them or not. You can ignore all of it and just use the timer.

## Is there any evidence behind this?

Slow breathing and short sits are some of the most studied simple practices around. We are not going to promise you a result, and a timer is not medical care. If you want the plain reasoning behind the long exhale, read [How to calm down fast](/help/find-your-people/how-to-calm-down-fast). Then try it and decide for yourself.
