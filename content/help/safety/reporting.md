Frequency is meant to feel safe. If something's wrong, you have direct ways to act, and real people behind the scenes who follow up.

## Report a post or a Dispatch

1. Tap the three dots (**More actions**) on the post or Dispatch.
2. Choose **Report**.
3. Pick the reason that fits best: **Spam**, **Harassment**, **Inappropriate**, **Misinformation**, or **Other**.
4. Add details if they help. They are optional.
5. Tap **Submit Report**.

Your report goes to the moderation queue, where a person reviews it against the [community guidelines](/guidelines). It isn't decided by a bot. You won't see **Report** on your own posts.

## Report a listing

Market and Housing listings carry their own small **Report** link on the listing page. Tap it, pick a reason, and the team takes a look.

## Block someone

Blocking is your own immediate control. It's about your experience and doesn't need anyone's approval.

1. Open the person's profile.
2. Tap **Block**, then **Confirm block**.

Blocking also removes them as a friend and stops direct messages in both directions.

To undo it, open your avatar menu, choose **Settings**, and look under **Blocked members** in the Account section. Tap **Unblock** next to their name. See also [Blocking and deleting your account](/help/getting-started/safety-and-account).

## Know how moderation works

- Frequency is moderated by people. Hosts, Guides, Mentors, and the platform team handle safety, escalating as needed.
- A reviewer can remove what was reported, or dismiss a report that doesn't break the guidelines.
- Repeated or serious harm can lead to a member being suspended.

## Reach a person

If you're worried about someone's safety, don't handle it alone. Talk to a Host, or open your avatar menu and choose **Help & support** to reach the team. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.
