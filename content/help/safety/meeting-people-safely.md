Frequency is built so you meet people the natural way: at a Circle or a public event, around something you both care about. That's also the safest way to meet someone new. There are other people there, and you get to know someone over a few gatherings instead of a single cold meetup.

## Plan a safe first meeting

- **Meet at a Circle or a public event first.** When someone catches your interest, look for a gathering you can both show up to. Meeting in a group is the easiest introduction there is.
- **Keep early meetings public.** A cafe, a park, a class. Somewhere with other people around. There's no rush to make it private.
- **Tell someone where you're going.** A friend, a housemate, anyone. Share the plan and when you expect to be back.
- **Trust your gut.** If something feels off, you don't owe anyone an explanation. Leave.

## Control what others see

- **We never share your exact location.** Others only see a coarse area, never your address.
- **You decide who can find you.** **Ghost mode** hides you from proximity and maps. **Who can find me nearby** limits discovery to your connections, or no one. **Location precision others see** sets how coarse your area looks.
- **Hosts can protect their address too.** Some events show only the city until you RSVP or get a ticket. The exact address appears once you register, and the preview someone sees when the link is shared carries the city only. A host turns this on with **Hide the address until someone registers** when setting up the event.

Where to find it: open your avatar menu, choose **Settings**, then **Privacy**. Your home base lives under **Location**.

## Look at housing or meet a roommate

- **See the place before any money moves.** No landlord or roommate on Frequency should ask for a deposit, a fee, or an app payment before you have seen the home and met in person. Someone pushing you to pay first is the oldest housing scam there is. Tap **Report** on the listing and walk away.
- Roommate matches show a match and a few reasons, never your budget or your answers. Treat a first roommate meeting like any first meeting: somewhere public, on your own schedule.

## Understand invites and saved contacts

- **An invite link points at a real gathering, not a private meetup.** When someone shares their personal invite and you RSVP, you're going to an event with other people there. Meet in the open, around something real, not one-on-one first.
- **Saved contacts stay private.** When you invite people and they RSVP, or when you scan someone's code to save them, that contact is private to you. It is never broadcast, and nobody is added to a mailing list. The same is true in reverse: RSVPing to a friend's invite shares your details with that one person, not a list.

## Act if someone makes you uncomfortable

- **Remove them from your suggestions.** The X on a suggested person takes them out of your suggestions.
- **Block them.** Open their profile and tap **Block**. That removes them as a friend and stops direct messages both ways.
- **Report what they posted.** A report goes to real people who review it. See [Reporting & moderation](/help/safety/reporting).

## Get verified by showing up

You verify yourself the most Frequency way there is: show up to an event and check in at the door. Turning up in the real world is a good sign you're a real person, so your first check-in marks you as a verified member. There's nothing to upload and no form to fill in. Until you've been to one, the rules above still apply: meet in the open, around something real, and take your time.
