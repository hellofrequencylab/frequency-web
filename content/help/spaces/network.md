Network is for one organization that runs many chapters. If your organization has local chapters, circles or teams that each run their own day, Network puts them under one roof while each chapter keeps its own page.

## Choose Network or Collective

Ask who owns the Spaces.

- **Collective** is for separate groups that work together. Each group owns its own Space, keeps its own members and payouts, and shares a calendar and listings with its partners. See [Collective](/help/spaces/collective).
- **Network** is for one organization. You own the parent Space and every chapter, set the standards, and see the whole network.

## See what Network includes

Everything Collective does except Partners, plus the chapter tools below. A Network is one owner, so the Spaces under it are chapters you own rather than partners; a chapter lists its Circles and Journeys with the parent and with other chapters the same way a Collective's member Spaces do.

- **Chapters.** A Network starts with three chapters, each with the Business tools. Each chapter after that is the same flat monthly price, up to 25. See the [pricing page](/pricing).
- **One membership.** A member who joins the network is a member in every chapter.
- **One network calendar and home**, with every chapter's events in one place.
- **A roll-up report** of members, Events and revenue across every chapter.
- **Central admins**, who see the whole network, and chapter leaders, who see their own chapter.
- **Vera AI and a custom domain**, included.
- **0% on your own people, always, plus card processing.** The finder's fee is the same as on Business and Collective: charged once, with a cap, on the first purchase from a customer Frequency introduced. After that the customer is yours. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for the current rate and cap.

## Pick how you run it

- **Partner Network** keeps your chapters in the Frequency community. Your network and every chapter wear the Frequency Partner badge, your events show on the Frequency calendar, and tickets and memberships run through Frequency checkout. You are listed in discovery. You start it yourself, with a 14 day trial. Paying for the year is two months free, and there is a yearly package: ten chapters for the price of eight, with a live onboarding session, priority support and a yearly network health review.
- **Independent Network** is your own community, fully separate from Frequency: your brand, your domain, your members and your data, with no Partner badge and no listing. It starts with a call and includes setup and onboarding. It is a yearly agreement with a one-time setup fee; the [Networks page](/networks) has the figures and the way to reach us.

If you remove the Partner badge or disconnect your chapters from Frequency, your plan moves to Independent pricing at your next renewal. Talk to us before you do.

## Set up a Network

1. Create or pick the Space that will be the parent.
2. Open **Manage**, then **Profile & Settings**, then **Plan and billing**, and choose Network. The [pricing page](/pricing) has the current price and the same starting point.
3. Add your chapters, as below.

Running more than 25 chapters, or wanting an Independent Network, is a call. Start from the [Networks page](/networks) and tell us how many chapters you run.

## Add a chapter

Where to find it: **Manage**, then **Profile & Settings**, then **Plan and billing**. The page is titled **Plan and usage**.

1. Find **Chapters** on that page.
2. Choose a Space you own.
3. Select **Add chapter**.

You must own both Spaces. Select **Detach** beside a chapter to restore its own plan. Nothing it has built is deleted or moved.

## Run your chapters

Your Network keeps a **Network** tab on its Space page, with every chapter, what is up next, and one shared calendar. From there, **Manage chapters** opens the **Chapters** page, which is also a card under **Profile & Settings**. That page holds your chapters and their leaders, your central admins, and chapter events waiting for a yes.

A leader runs one chapter and sees only that chapter's admin. Central admins help run every chapter. Billing stays with the owner.

## Subscribe to the whole Network's calendar

A Network's calendar feed is network-wide. Add `webcal://frequencylocal.com/spaces/<your-slug>/calendar.ics` to Google Calendar or Apple Calendar once and every approved chapter event arrives with the parent's own. A chapter's feed carries only that chapter. More in [Calendar feed](/help/spaces/calendar-feed).
