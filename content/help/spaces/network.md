Network is for one organization that runs many chapters. If your organization has local chapters,
circles or teams that each run their own day, Network puts them under one roof while each chapter
keeps its own page.

## Network or Collective?

Ask who owns the Spaces.

- **Collective** is for separate groups that work together. Each group owns its own Space, keeps its
  own members and payouts, and shares a calendar and listings with its partners.
- **Network** is for one organization. You own the parent Space and every chapter, set the standards,
  and see the whole network.

## What Network includes

Everything Collective does, plus:

- **Chapters.** Ten chapters are included, each with the Business tools. More chapters are priced on
  the [pricing page](/pricing).
- **One membership.** A member who joins the network is a member in every chapter.
- **One network calendar and home**, with every chapter's events in one place.
- **A roll-up report** of members, Events and revenue across every chapter.
- **Central admins**, who see the whole network, and chapter leaders, who see their own chapter.
- **Vera AI and a custom domain**, included.
- **The lowest network fee** on customers the network introduces. Your own people and your tips stay
  at **0%**, as they are on every plan.

## Add a chapter

Open **Plan and usage** on your Network, then find **Chapters**. Choose a Space you own and select
**Add chapter**. You must own both Spaces. Select **Detach** beside a chapter to restore its own plan.
Nothing it has built is deleted or moved.

## Set up a Network

Network is set up with you. Open the [Networks page](/networks) and select **Talk to us**, and tell us
how many chapters you run.
