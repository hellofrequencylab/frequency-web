Launching a Space opens a full-screen launch that walks you through your Spotlight, your Space, a website, your first email, and your first event. You do not have to finish it in one sitting.

## Can I stop partway?

Yes. Press **X** or **Esc** and you land back on the page you came from. A **Finish setup** button stays with you so you can pick up where you stopped. Nothing you entered is lost.

## What happens after I publish?

The launch steps itself away. In its place you get a Setup list with a check-off item for each tool your Space has: booking times, a first product or service, a membership tier, donations, a first email campaign, a capture link, an automation. An item checks itself off only when the real thing exists, and each one opens the tool where you finish it.

## Is there a list for me, not just my Space?

Yes. The last group on the Setup list is **Personal**. It holds the same six Founder's First Week moves you see on your Founder page, so there is one list and one set of numbers.

## What if I only want a personal Spotlight?

Choose **Just my personal Spotlight** when the launch asks what you came to set up. You add your name, one line, and a link, and your Spotlight is live. If a Space is ever what you want, the launch offers it afterward and carries your words along.

## Can I hide the Finish setup button?

Yes. Open it, choose to hide it, and confirm twice. Nothing you built goes away. To bring it back, open **Settings** and turn the Setup list back on.
