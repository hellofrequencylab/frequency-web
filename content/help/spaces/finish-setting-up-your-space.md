You do not have to set up a Space in one sitting. Everything you enter is saved as you go, and there is always a clear way back to what is left.

## Walk the setup steps

A new business or nonprofit Space opens on a short setup walk right after you create it. It has five steps:

1. **Tell people what this is.** A tagline, a short intro and the longer story.
2. **Make it look like you.** Your display name, your logo and your brand color.
3. **How people reach you.** Contact details and links. Fill these in once and every surface reads from them.
4. **What you offer.** The services, classes or sessions people come to you for. You can price and polish them later.
5. **First things.** Start a Circle or add an Event now, or skip ahead.

Each step saves through the same settings you can edit later, so nothing here is locked in.

## Pick it back up later

Left partway? Open your Space, then **Manage**, then **Profile & Settings**. The **Search readiness** card shows how many of the basics are done, with a check beside each one and a plain note on the ones still missing. While there is more to do, **Finish setting up** on that card takes you back into the walk.

When the card says your profile is search-ready, the basics are covered.

## Use the guided launch

Some Spaces start with a guided launch instead: a full-screen walk through your Spotlight, your Space and your website, with optional stops for a first offer, an event, a Circle, a Journey, your people and getting paid. It is opening in stages, so you may not see it yet. If you do, here is how it behaves:

- **Pause any time.** Press the close button or **Esc** and you land back on the page you came from. Nothing you entered is lost.
- **Come back from Finish setup.** A **Finish setup** button stays with you, in the header and in **Manage**, so you can pick up where you stopped.
- **Work down the Setup list.** After you publish, the same button opens a list with a check-off item for each tool your Space has. An item checks itself off only when the real thing exists, and each one opens the tool where you finish it.

The Setup list comes in three groups:

- **Money:** set the weekly times people can book, list your first product or service, open a membership tier, turn on donations, and publish a page for one offer.
- **Reach:** draft your first email campaign, send a campaign to your people, make a capture link for new people, and set up an automation.
- **Personal:** the same Founder's First Week moves you see on your Founder page, so there is one list and one set of numbers.

## Questions

### What if I only want a personal Spotlight?

Choose **Just my personal Spotlight** when the launch asks what you came to set up. You get your own page for links, events and what you are up to. A Space is the step up whenever you want it, and your name, bio and link come along.

### Can I hide the Finish setup button?

Yes. Open it, choose **Hide setup**, and confirm twice. Nothing you built goes away. To bring it back, open **Settings**, find **Privacy and your data**, and press **Bring it back**.

### Do I lose anything if I stop partway?

No. What you entered stays saved, and the walk picks up from where you stopped.
