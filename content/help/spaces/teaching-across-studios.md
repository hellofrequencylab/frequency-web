If you teach at two or three studios, your students are spread across their sign-up systems, and none of those lists are yours. A Space fixes that part.

## What does a Space give me?

A free Space is one page for what you teach: who you are, your own Events, and where people can find you. Students can follow your Space, so they find you wherever you're teaching.

## Can I sell my own workshops?

Yes. Put a workshop on your Space as an Event and sell tickets. See [Get paid](/help/spaces/get-paid) for where the money lands and what the network takes.

## Do I get a student list?

Your Space has a **Contacts** list: the people you track, with private notes on each. Add the students you meet, and the list stays with your Space, not with a studio. See [Your Space Contacts](/help/spaces/space-crm).

## Does this compete with the studios?

No. Keep teaching where you teach. Your Space is the one place your students can always find you.

## What does it cost?

Opening a Space is free. You pay when you start charging and want more room to grow. See [Plans and pricing](/help/spaces/plans-and-pricing).
