If you teach at two or three studios, your students are spread across their sign-up systems, and none of those lists are yours. A Space fixes that part: one page that is yours, wherever you happen to be teaching this month.

## Start with a free Space

A free Space is one page for what you teach: who you are, your own Events, and where people can find you. Students can follow your Space, so they find you wherever you're teaching.

## Questions

### Can I sell my own workshops?

Yes, once your Space is on Business. Put a workshop on your Space as an Event and sell tickets. See [Get paid](/help/spaces/get-paid) for where the money lands and what the network takes.

### Do I get a student list?

Yes. Your Space has a contact list: the people you track, with private notes on each. Add the students you meet, and the list stays with your Space, not with a studio. Open **Manage**, then **Community Resonance**, then **CRM**. See [Community Resonance](/help/spaces/community-resonance).

### Does this compete with the studios?

No. Keep teaching where you teach. Your Space is the one place your students can always find you.

### What does it cost?

Opening a Space is free. You pay when you start charging and want more room to grow. See [Plans and pricing](/help/spaces/plans-and-pricing).
