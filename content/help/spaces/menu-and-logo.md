Your Space page and your website each have their own menu. The Space page menu is the same four
places on every Space. Your website menu is yours to shape.

## Your Space page menu

On Frequency, your Space page menu is **Home, Community, Calendar and Program**, with **Manage**
set apart for you and your team. The platform's own Space has no Community wall, so it shows
Home, Calendar and Program.

- **Program** gathers your public Programs, Journeys and Practices in one place.
- Everything else you offer, like booking, Circles, Memberships or the Shop, is a link on your Home
  page, so it is one tap from Home instead of another tab.
- Your custom links and dropdowns from before still show up as links on Home. Nothing you saved was
  removed.

You do not edit this menu. It keeps the same shape everywhere so people always know where to look.

## Your website menu

Your website keeps its own pages and its own menu. Change it in either place:

- **On Frequency:** open **Manage**, then **Page**, and scroll to **Website menu**.
- **In your website editor:** select the header, then pick **Edit menu and logo**.

Both open the same editor and save to the same menu.

Until you save a menu, it builds itself from your website pages and the sections on its Home page.
Turn a feature on and its link appears.

## Making it yours (Business plan)

On the Business plan you can arrange your website menu any way you like:

- **Reorder** links with the up and down arrows, and **rename** any of them.
- **Choose where each link shows** in **Shown in**: Website and Space Home links, Space Home links
  only, or Website only.
- **Add a custom link** to any https address, an email (mailto:) or a phone number (tel:).
- **Add a dropdown.** It opens a panel of links, each with a short line under it, and an optional
  photo card beside them. On your Space page, each link in a dropdown shows as its own link on Home.

Press **Save menu** when you are happy. Once you have saved, a feature you turn on later joins the
end of your menu by itself, ready for you to move or hide. A link to a feature you switch off, or a
page you delete, quietly leaves the menu instead of leading somewhere empty.

Changed your mind? **Use the automatic menu** clears your saved menu and goes back to the menu that
builds itself.

On the free plan your website menu builds itself from your pages and features, and the editor shows
it locked.

## Your logo

Pick how your name shows in the menu bar. The choice applies to your Space page and your website:

- **Image and name** (the default): your Space image in a circle, beside your name.
- **Name only:** just your name, with the image turned off.
- **Logo:** an image you upload, shown on its own and never cropped. This is the one for a wordmark.

Upload a logo with **Upload a logo**, and it is put to use straight away. Every plan can choose a logo
style.
