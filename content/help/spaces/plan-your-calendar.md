Most gatherings start as a maybe. Plans give your team a place to hold the date, keep notes and to-dos, and decide together before anything shows up for the public.

Where to find it: open your Space, then **Manage**, then **Content & Programs**, then **Calendar**.

## Learn the three stages

Every gathering on its way moves through the same stages.

- **Pencil.** A date you're holding. Nothing is decided. It shows as a dashed hold.
- **Planning.** Decided, and being put together.
- **Production.** Ready to run.

A gathering that falls through can be marked **Cancelled** and stays in your history.

Pencil and Planning dates stay on your team's view only. They never show on the public calendar. A visitor sees an event once you publish it.

## Start a Plan

1. On the calendar, press **Pencil it in**.
2. Tap the day, or up to three days, and give it a title. You can set how long to hold the date, and start from a playbook if you have one saved.
3. Open the Plan to add notes for the team and to-dos, and to move it from one stage to the next.
4. When you're ready, choose **Make it a Production**. That opens the new event form with what you planned already filled in.

Quick add on any day also lets you drop in a **To-do**, a **Private** entry, or an **Unavailable** block.

## Switch views

The calendar has seven views: **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Agenda**, **List**, **Kanban** and **Timeline**. List, Kanban and Timeline show every Plan instead of a stretch of time, and Kanban groups them by stage. The calendar remembers the last view you used.

**Team view** and **Public view** at the top switch between everything your team sees and what a visitor sees.

## Set up the calendar

Press the gear at the top of the calendar. The settings sheet holds how the calendar reads and where it goes.

- **Time.** The zone your calendar runs in. New dates are written in it wherever you happen to be.
- **Sky markers.** Moons, solstices and the Sun's sign. Off until you turn it on.
- **Day notes.** A few quiet words on a day, like Quiet hours on Mondays. Only your team sees them.
- **Sync and feeds.** **Copy public feed** gives you the link anyone can add to their calendar. **Copy team feed** gives you your team's private link.

## Share your private team feed

**Copy team feed** is a private calendar link for your team. It carries everything your team can see, and it's never shown on the public Space page. Keep it to your team. For the public link, see [Subscribe to a Space calendar](/help/spaces/calendar-feed).

In that calendar, a date you're holding shows as a tentative **Hold**. A to-do with a start time shows at that hour.

Copying the link doesn't change it. If it ever leaks, find **Private team feed** lower on the calendar page and press **Make a private feed**. That makes a fresh link, and everyone who subscribed needs the new one.

## Know where two-way sync stands

Feeds are one-way: they send your dates out to a calendar app. Two-way sync is a separate thing, listed under **Two-way sync** in the same settings sheet. Here is what is true today:

- **Google Calendar.** Built, and not switched on yet. The row is there without a Connect button. When it opens, the Space owner or an admin connects it, your events go out to Google, and your team's busy times come back as Unavailable.
- **Outlook.** Not built yet. The feeds work in Outlook today.
- **Apple Calendar.** Apple does not let other apps connect to iCloud calendars, so a feed is the way.
- **Notion.** Notion Calendar shows your Google calendars, so it follows once Google is connected.

Until then, the feeds do the job in any calendar app.

## Use it on your phone

On a narrow screen the calendar opens on **Agenda**, a day-by-day list that reads well on a phone. The sidebar, with the month, what to show and your Plans, opens from the filter button at the top.
