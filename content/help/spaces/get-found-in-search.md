People find a local group by searching for what you do and where you are. The pages that show up are the ones that say both, in plain words, and that other places link to. Four steps cover most of it, and none of them costs anything.

## Say what you do and where

Say your town and what you do in the first lines of your Space description. "Run club in Carlsbad, all paces" tells a search engine and a stranger the same thing. Use the words someone would type, not a clever name.

Keep the title of each event clear in the same way: what it is, then where. Events you publish publicly can show up in search with the date and place, so a clear title is worth the extra few words.

## Fill in the basics

Open your Space, then **Manage**, then **Profile & Settings**. The **Search readiness** card lists the basics a search engine and a stranger both look for, like your name, tagline, story, logo, cover image, what you offer and your links. It checks off the ones you have and says why each missing one matters.

If you have a website, the website builder has **SEO and sharing** for each page's title and description. See [Your website](/help/spaces/your-website).

## Link your Space from everywhere you already are

Put your Frequency page, or your website if you have published one, anywhere you already have a presence:

- Your Instagram bio and any link-in-bio page.
- Your email signature.
- Your own website, if you have one, with a button that says what to do ("See our next gathering").
- A partner's page, such as a studio, cafe or venue you meet at. Ask them for one link and offer the same back.

Each link tells search engines that real people point at your Space. A few good links from places that fit beat many from places that do not.

Your website starts on a free address, `yourspace.frequencylocal.com`. You can use your own domain too. See [Your website](/help/spaces/your-website).

## Claim a Google Business Profile

If you meet at a real place you operate from, such as a studio, a shop or a regular venue you run, claim one. It is what Google shows on a map when someone searches your name.

1. Open [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and sign in.
2. Add your name and category, and choose whether people visit a location or you travel to them.
3. Add your website or Frequency page, your hours and a short description in plain words.
4. Complete Google's verification. It can take a while, so start early.

If you only meet in public places and have no place of your own, skip it. Google can suspend a profile that lists an address you do not operate from.

## Questions

### How long does it take?

It varies, and nobody outside the search engines controls it. New pages are often picked up within days, and links and a profile can take weeks to matter. Keep the page current, keep your events up to date, and keep asking one partner at a time for a link.

### Do I need my own domain to be found?

No. Your Space page and your free website address can both be found. Your own domain is there if you want your name in the address.

### Does a private Space show up in search?

A private Space keeps its page and calendar away from the public, so it is not the kind of page this advice is for. Check who can see your Space in **Profile & Settings**.
