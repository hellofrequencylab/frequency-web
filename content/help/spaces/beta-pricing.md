Frequency is in open beta, and a Collective that starts now gets a lower price than the list price.

## Beta pricing

A Collective is $149 a month at list. During the beta it's **$99 a month**. The offer ends **January 1**. A Collective that starts on it keeps that price, grandfathered, for as long as it keeps the plan.

A Collective that starts before **December 1** also gets the founder badge.

Paying yearly gives you two months free on any plan, so a yearly plan is ten months of the monthly price.

## Open access until 1 December

Until 1 December 2026 the free allowances are switched off. Any Space can use every hosting tool with no limits, whatever plan it's on. Selling still opens at Business, and QR codes keep their published limit.

From 1 December the allowances apply as written, and we'll tell you what changes before it changes.

## Questions

### What happens if I'm already on a plan?

The price you start on is yours for as long as you keep the plan.

### Where do I start?

Open **Manage**, then **Billing**. See [Billing](/help/spaces/billing) for how plans, cards and invoices work, and [Plans and pricing](/help/spaces/plans-and-pricing) for what each plan includes.
