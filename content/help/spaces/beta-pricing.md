Frequency's open beta runs through January 31, 2027. That is the one beta end date for everyone, so you do not have to work out which deadline is yours.

## Know what the beta gives you

Beta access continues through January 31, 2027. The current tools, limits and selling fees for each Space plan are listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). That page reads the live catalog, so it is the place to check a number.

## Understand Founding Year

Founding Year is a paid first-year offer for eligible purchases during the beta. It closes January 31, 2027. Check the [pricing page](/pricing) for current availability, price and terms.

Two things are easy to mix up:

- Founding Member recognition is free and separate from a Founding Year purchase. Joining before launch does not by itself purchase a Founding Year.
- Annual billing and the Founding Year offer have separate terms. Check the [pricing page](/pricing) for current prices and eligibility.

## Check your own plan

Where to find it, for a Space: open **Manage**, then **Profile & Settings**, then **Plan and billing**. Only the Space owner can change the plan there.

For your personal membership, open your avatar menu and pick **Plan & billing**.

## Questions

### What happens if I'm already on a plan?

Nothing changes on its own. Your plan, current price and renewal terms are on your Space's **Plan and billing** page. Any price lock and its terms appear in your billing details.

### Where do I start?

Open **Manage**, then **Profile & Settings**, then **Plan and billing**. See [Billing](/help/spaces/billing) for how plans, cards and invoices work, and [Plans and pricing](/help/spaces/plans-and-pricing) for what each plan includes.

### Is the beta end date different for Spaces and members?

No. January 31, 2027 applies to everyone.
