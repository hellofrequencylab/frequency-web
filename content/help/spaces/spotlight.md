Spotlight is the one link you hand out: a page with your links, in the order you want them. Every member can have a personal Spotlight, and every Space has one of its own.

## Turn on your personal Spotlight

1. Open your avatar menu and choose **Settings**, then **Edit profile**.
2. Find the card called **Your Spotlight page** and press **Turn on your Spotlight**.
3. Press **Edit your Spotlight** to build it.
4. When it looks right, press **Publish**. Until then it is a draft only you can see.

Once it is published, anyone with the link can see it. Your page lives at `/spotlight/your-handle`. Press **Unpublish** any time to take it back to a draft.

## Build it

The editor has four sections, with a live preview beside them.

- **Links.** Press **Add link**, then give it a title, a link and an icon. Use the arrows to reorder. **Highlight** makes one link stand out. The switch on each link shows or hides it without deleting it, and **Schedule** lets a link show from one date and hide after another.
- **Profile.** Add the places people can find you online. Your name, photo and bio come from your profile.
- **Look.** Your theme, fonts and background. Each choice saves as you make it.
- **Frequency.** **Show Frequency features** adds your events, Circles and guestbook under your links. Turn it off for a clean link page. This is also where you turn the page on or off and publish it.

Press **Save changes** when you edit links or your profile section. **Copy link** and **View your Spotlight** appear once the page is published.

## See what gets pressed

Each link shows how many times it was pressed in the last 30 days, and the Links section totals them.

## Know your link limit

The Links section shows how many links you have used out of how many you can have. That number depends on your membership, and Crew adds more room.

## Set up a Space's Spotlight

1. Open your Space's console and choose **Spotlight** in its menu.
2. Flip the switch at the top. It reads "Your Spotlight is hidden" until you do, then "Your Spotlight is live".
3. Use **View your Spotlight** to open it, or **Make a QR code** to print the link.

A Space's Spotlight lives at `/spaces/your-handle/spotlight`. On the same screen you will find:

- **Theme**: one theme for your Spotlight, Space and website. You do not need a website to pick one.
- **Look**: how your theme lays out this page. The preview shows the one you pick.
- **Your own links**: links you add yourself.
- **Clicks in the last 30 days**: which links and Link cards people pressed, most pressed first.

Selling from a Link card needs a plan that takes payments. A Space on the free plan sees the upgrade offer in that spot.

## Questions

### Is my Spotlight public as soon as I turn it on?

No. Turning it on starts a draft. People can only see it after you publish it, and you can unpublish whenever you like.

### Do I need a website to have a Spotlight?

No. Spotlight stands on its own. If you add a website later, they can share one theme.
