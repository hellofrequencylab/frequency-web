A Space is the home your practice, business or group keeps on Frequency. Creating one takes five short steps, and nothing you pick is locked in.

## Find the door

- In the left rail, open **Spaces**, then choose **Create a Space** at the top of the directory.
- If you already run a Space, **Your Spaces** in the left rail lists them, with **Create a Space** for the next one.
- Not signed in yet? Start at [Create a Space](/create-a-space), the quick version below. You build first and make your account at the end.

## Build one in five minutes

[Create a Space](/create-a-space) is the short way in. It asks three things beside a live preview of your Space page: the Space's name and address, whether it is a business or a nonprofit or group, and one line about it. A five-minute clock runs while you build. It is a challenge, not a deadline: past five minutes it counts bonus time and you keep going.

- **Signed in:** press finish and your Space is made right away.
- **Not signed in:** continue with Google or ask for an email link. Your draft waits while you sign in, then your Space is made.

A free Space comes with Crew. Taking payments needs Business; see [Plans and pricing](/help/spaces/plans-and-pricing).

## Build the rest the same way

Create a Space is one of four short builders. Each works on its own, each ends with a card offering the next one, and **I'm done for now** always sits beside it.

- **[Free Spotlight](/free-spotlight)**, about three minutes: your name, one line and your main link, and your Spotlight goes live. See [Spotlight, your link page](/help/spaces/spotlight).
- **[Launch Your Website](/launch-your-website)**, about five minutes: a name, a look and a headline. It goes on your newest Space, or a new Space in that look, and opens at your free address. See [Your website](/help/spaces/your-website).
- **[Email Marketing](/email-marketing)**, about four minutes: who it is from, a subject and the message. It is saved as a draft in your Space's **Marketing** tab and nothing is sent until you send it.

Want all of it in one sitting? **[Launch Your Business](/launch-your-business)** builds your Space, Spotlight, website and bookings in about twelve minutes.

## Walk the five steps

If Vera's guided setup opens instead of the steps below, follow its screens. It covers the same ground, and you can pause and come back to it.

1. **Goals.** Pick everything you want to do here: take bookings, run classes or memberships, teach programs or courses, host events, sell products, build a community, stay in touch by email, raise donations. Pick as many as fit. Not ready to choose? Tap **Not sure yet? Decide later**.
2. **Setup.** We suggest a starting set of tools from your picks. Keep the suggestion or choose another. You can switch it later.
3. **Name.** Give your Space the name people will see, and a handle. Your Space lives at `/spaces/your-handle`, and a handle uses lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. Then choose who can find it:
   - **Listed in the directory**: anyone can find it in the Spaces directory.
   - **Private**: hidden from the directory. Only you and your members can open it.
4. **Look.** Pick a theme. One choice dresses your website, your Spotlight and your Space page. You can skip this and pick later. See [Pick a theme](/help/spaces/themes).
5. **Start.** Check the summary: your Space name, handle, goals, tool setup, look, **Plan** and **Visibility**. Then press **Create my Space**.

## Know what the summary means

- **Plan** reads "Free, upgrade any time" unless you arrived from the pricing page with a paid plan in mind. In that case, checkout comes after setup, not before. Plans are explained in [Plans and pricing](/help/spaces/plans-and-pricing).
- **Visibility** repeats your choice from the Name step: listed in the Spaces directory, or private.

## After you create it

Your Space exists the moment you press the button. From there we walk you through your story, your look, how people reach you, and what you offer. You do not have to finish in one sitting: [How do I finish setting up my Space?](/help/spaces/finish-setting-up-your-space) covers picking it back up.

## Questions

### Do I have to choose a niche?

No. The Goals step is a list of things you want to do, not a category you are stuck in. You can add more any time.

### Can I change the name, look or tools later?

Yes. The steps say so as you go: the tool setup can be switched, the look can be picked later, and your Space's identity is edited from its console afterward.
