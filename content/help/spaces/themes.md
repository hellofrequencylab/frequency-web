A theme is the look your Space wears in public: colors, type, corners and buttons, chosen once. Pick one and your pages start to feel like the same place.

## See the themes

These are the themes in the picker, in the order it shows them:

- **New World**: the Frequency house look, with Night and Day in one theme.
- **Designer**: limewash and daylight, charcoal type, one ochre accent and a quiet serif.
- **Bold**: dark, graphic and direct, with a bright accent and angled cuts.
- **Wildheart**: light, ancient and organic, with parchment, violet and soft petal corners.
- **Network**: paper and deep ink with one solid hero color, pill buttons and real photos up front.
- **Temple**: warm plaster and umber with one clay color, arches and soft corners.
- **Nocturne**: after dark, with deep night grounds, bone type and one violet accent.
- **Honey**: limewash and honey light, made for movement, ritual and the village around it.
- **Services**: night-first and glossy, for people who book, sell and show up for clients.
- **CyberWitch**: concrete and chain-link under blackletter type, with one neon accent. Dark only.

You can browse them with pictures on the public gallery at `/themes`.

## Know what a theme dresses

- **Website**: every page, header, footer and block.
- **Spotlight**: your link page in the full look.
- **Email**: receipts, reminders and newsletters in the same ground, type and button.
- **Space page**: your fonts and your accent. It stays the Frequency page people already know, so members never feel lost.

Bold and Wildheart dress your website today. Their Spotlight and email looks are still on the way.

## Pick one

1. Open your Space's console and choose **Profile & Settings**.
2. Open **Theme and fonts**.
3. Under **Theme**, tap the one you want. It saves the moment you pick it.

A theme brings its own fonts. After you pick, you can still change the fonts, and adjust corners, spacing, header and footer. The option marked "(theme)" is the theme's own default.

The switch **Use the same style on my Space page** keeps your Space page in step with your website. Your website leads. Turn it off to give your Space page its own fonts.

You can also pick a theme in two other places:

- When you [create a Space](/help/spaces/create-a-space), on the Look step. Skipping it is fine.
- On your Space's **Spotlight** screen, under **Theme**. You do not need a website to pick one there.

## Try one on before you commit

In the website editor:

1. Choose **Change theme**.
2. Find a theme and press **Try it on**. Your site is shown wearing it. Your words and photos stay put.
3. Press **Keep this look** to switch, or **Back to mine** to leave things as they were.

If a theme is still in preview for websites, the editor says so, and your live site keeps its current look until that theme is ready.

## Questions

### Will changing themes erase my content?

No. Your words and photos stay. Blocks keep their shape and take on the new colors and type, and undo takes the change back.

### Does my Space page change completely with the theme?

No. The Space page takes your fonts and accent only. The rest stays familiar on purpose.
