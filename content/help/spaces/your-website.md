Your website is your Space's pages turned into a site of its own, with your brand and none of the
Frequency menus. It is built from the same blocks as your Space page, so you edit once and both stay
in step.

## Publish it

Open **Manage**, then **Profile & Settings**. The **Your website** card leads the tab. Select
**Publish website** and it goes live. Once it is live you see its address, **View website** and
**Unpublish**. An unpublished website shows Coming soon.

Every Space can publish. Your website starts on a free address, `yourspace.frequencylocal.com`,
named after your Space.

The same card has a **Website hero** section. That is what your website leads with. Your Space page
keeps its own header.

On the free plan your website is one home page plus your standard feature pages. Extra pages of
your own come with Business.

## Use your own domain

Your own domain, like `yourname.com`, is the custom domain add-on on Business and Non Profit, and it
is included with Collective. Prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

In the **Your website** card, find **Your domain** and enter a domain you own. Some providers show a
**Connect with** button that sets the records for you. Otherwise the card lists the records to add
at your domain provider. It then shows where things stand, from **Waiting on DNS** to **Securing
your site** to **Live**. Use **Check again** if DNS is slow, and **Remove domain** to take it off.

Until your domain is live, your website stays on its free address.

## A site that stands on its own

A visitor never gets bounced back to Frequency. Links to your own pages stay on your website, and
Book or Contact opens your website's own page. The only Frequency links are the ones clearly
labelled as Frequency, like the On Frequency section and the small mark in the footer.

## Pick a theme

There are two themes. Your content stays the same in both; only the look changes.

- **Default**: warm, bright and airy, with soft cards, a frosted menu bar and generous space.
- **Bold**: dark, graphic and direct, with charcoal, a bright accent and angled cuts.

Choosing a theme also sets its fonts. You can pick other fonts after.

## Global styles

On top of the theme you can set four things for the whole site:

- **Corners:** Soft, Subtle or Square.
- **Spacing:** Airy, Balanced or Compact.
- **Header:** Floating pill or Full-width bar.
- **Footer:** Full or Minimal.

The option marked **(theme)** is the theme's own choice. Leave it there and a later theme switch
brings that theme's default. Pick something else and your choice stays when you switch.

You can change the theme and global styles in two places, and both save to the same setting:

- **On Frequency:** in **Profile & Settings**, open **Theme and fonts**.
- **In your website editor:** open **Theme**, then **Global styles**.

Either way, the change shows on your live website straight away.

## One page in the other theme

Your site has one theme, and any page can step out of it. In the website editor, open the page, select
**Page settings**, and under **Page theme** pick **Default** or **Bold**. That page wears the theme you
picked, in that theme's own fonts, and your explicit global styles still hold. Everything else on the
site keeps the site theme.

**Same as site** is the default. Leave a page there and it follows the Theme tab, so switching the site
theme later carries it along. Like the theme, a page theme shows on your live website at once.

## Your brand on your website

Your website header wears your Space's brand: the brand name, the logo and the accent colour you set in
**Identity and branding**. With no brand name set, the header shows your Space name; with no logo, it
shows the name alone. In the website editor you can give the website its own name and logo, and those
win on the website only. Your Space page keeps its own.

## Members see their membership

Someone who is signed in to Frequency and is a member of your Space sees their membership in your
website header, as a small pill with the tier name. It opens your Memberships page on Frequency. It
shows on your free `frequencylocal.com` address; on your own domain the browser keeps the sign-in to
itself, so visitors there see the plain header. Nothing about anyone's other Spaces is shown.

## Keep your Space page in step

Under **Theme and fonts**, **Use the same style on my Space page** is on to start with. Your
website leads, and your Space page wears the same fonts. On a Bold website, your Space page also
takes Bold's accent if you have not picked one, and it keeps Frequency's own layout.

Turn it off to give your Space page its own fonts. Pick them in **Space page fonts**.

## Your menu and logo

Your Space page and your website share one menu and one logo. See [Your menu and
logo](/help/spaces/menu-and-logo) for how to edit them.

## The website editor

Open your website and select **Admin: Website builder** in its menu, or in the Admin section at the
bottom of the page. Visitors see that link too, but only someone who can edit your Space gets in.
If you are not signed in, Frequency asks you to sign in first, then sends you back.

In the editor you can:

- Work through **Pages**, **Layers**, **Add**, **Theme** and **Site**.
- Preview your site on **Desktop**, **Tablet** or **Phone**.
- Edit **Header and navigation**, **Footer**, **Page settings** and **SEO and sharing**.
- Use **Undo**, **Redo** and **Save draft** as you go.

Theme and global styles go live as soon as you pick them. Everything else waits for you to publish.

## Publish from the editor

Select **Publish**. You see what changed, then choose **Publish now** or **Schedule** a date and
time. Each publish saves a version, and **Versions** lets you **Restore to draft** if you want an
earlier one back.

If the editor says your theme is still in preview, publishing saves your version, and your live
website keeps its current look until that theme is ready.

## Bookings from guests

If your Space has a service or open times set up, your website gets a Book page. A visitor books
right there with a name and an email. No Frequency account needed.

The time is held for thirty minutes while the guest confirms it from a link we email them. If they do
not click in time, the hold lapses and the time opens up again. Until they click, it shows in your
bookings as awaiting the guest's confirmation.

Once they confirm, the guest gets a confirmation email with a calendar file and a reminder before
their time. You get your usual booking email with their name and address, and they land in your Space
Contacts. They only get your news if they tick **Keep me posted**.

A few limits for guests:

- A service that takes a deposit cannot be booked by a guest yet.
- A guest cannot cancel or reschedule on their own. You can do it for them, and they get an email.
