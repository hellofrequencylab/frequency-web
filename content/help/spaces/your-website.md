Your website is your Space's pages turned into a site of its own, with your brand and none of the
Frequency menus. It shows the same blocks as your Space page, so you edit once and both stay in step.

Where to find it: open your Space, then **Manage**, then **Profile & Settings**. The **Your website**
card leads the tab.

## Publish it

1. Open **Manage**, then **Profile & Settings**.
2. On the **Your website** card, select **Publish website**.

That is it. The card now shows your address, with **View website** and **Unpublish** beside it. An
unpublished website shows a Coming soon page.

Every Space can publish. Your website starts on a free address, `yourspace.frequencylocal.com`,
named after your Space.

The same card holds two more settings:

- **Website hero**: what your website leads with. Your Space page keeps its own header.
- **Footer credit**: how the bottom of your website mentions Frequency.

On the free plan your website is one home page plus your standard feature pages. Extra pages of
your own come with Business.

## Use your own domain

Your own domain, like `yourname.com`, is the custom domain add-on on Business and Non Profit, and it
is included with Collective and Network. Prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

1. On **Profile & Settings**, find the **Domain and DNS** card, right under the website card.
2. Under **Your domain**, enter a domain you own and select **Connect**.
3. If your provider shows a **Connect with** button, use it and the records are set for you.
   Otherwise the card lists the records to add where your domain is managed, each with a copy button.
4. Watch the status move from **Waiting on DNS** to **Securing your site** to **Live**. The page
   checks again on its own, and **Check again** is there if DNS is slow.

**Remove domain** takes it off. Until your domain is live, your website stays on its free address.

## A site that stands on its own

A visitor never gets bounced back to Frequency. Links to your own pages stay on your website, and
Book or Contact opens your website's own page. The only Frequency links are the ones clearly
labelled as Frequency, like the On Frequency section and the credit in the footer.

## Pick a theme

A theme is the whole look: colors, type, shapes and photo treatment. Your words and photos stay the
same in every theme. The themes you can pick today:

- **New World**: the Frequency house look, with Night and Day in one theme. It leads the list.
- **Designer**: limewash and daylight, plaster grounds, charcoal type and a quiet serif.
- **Bold**: dark, graphic and direct, with charcoal, a bright accent and angled cuts.
- **Wildheart**: light, ancient and organic, with parchment, violet and soft petal corners.
- **Network**: paper and deep ink with one solid hero color, pill buttons and real photos up front.
- **Temple**: warm plaster and umber with one clay color, arches and soft corners.
- **Nocturne**: after dark, with deep night grounds, bone type and one violet accent.
- **Honey**: limewash and honey light, with a gilt arch and a candle-dark night.
- **Services**: night-first and glossy, for people who book, sell and show up for clients.
- **CyberWitch**: concrete and chain-link with one neon accent. Dark only.

Choosing a theme also sets its fonts. You can pick other fonts after. If your site wears an older
theme that is no longer in the list, it keeps that theme until you choose a new one.

## Set your global styles

On top of the theme you can set four things for the whole site:

- **Corners:** Soft, Subtle or Square.
- **Spacing:** Airy, Balanced or Compact.
- **Header:** Floating pill, Full-width bar or Centered.
- **Footer:** Full or Minimal.

In **Theme and fonts**, the option marked **(theme)** is the theme's own choice. Leave it there and
a later theme switch brings that theme's default. Pick something else and your choice stays when you
switch.

You can change the theme and global styles in two places, and both save to the same setting:

- **On Frequency:** in **Profile & Settings**, open **Theme and fonts**.
- **In the website builder:** open **Theme**, then **Global styles**.

Either way, the change shows on your live website straight away.

## Give one page its own theme

Your site has one theme, and any page can step out of it. In the website builder, open the page,
select **Page settings**, and under **Page theme** pick the theme for that page. It wears that
theme's own fonts, and your global styles still hold. Everything else keeps the site theme.

**Same as site** is the default. Leave a page there and it follows the site, so switching the site
theme later carries it along. Like the theme, a page theme shows on your live website at once.

## Put your brand on it

Your website header wears your Space's brand: the brand name, the logo and the accent color. With no
brand name set, the header shows your Space name. With no logo, it shows the name alone. In the
website builder, the **Theme** tab lets you give the website its own logo and accent, and those win
on the website only. Your Space page keeps its own.

## Keep your Space page in step

Under **Theme and fonts**, **Use the same style on my Space page** is on to start with. Your
website leads, and your Space page wears the same fonts.

Turn it off to give your Space page its own fonts. Pick them in **Space page fonts**.

## Edit in the website builder

Two ways in:

- **On Frequency:** open **Manage** and select **Website builder** in the row of links at the top.
- **On your website:** when you are signed in as someone who can edit the Space, an **Admin**
  section in the footer carries a **Website builder** link. Visitors do not see it.

In the builder you can:

- Work through **Pages**, **Layers**, **Add**, **Theme** and **Site**.
- Preview your site on **Desktop**, **Tablet** or **Phone**.
- Edit **Header and navigation**, **Footer**, **Page settings** and **SEO and sharing**.
- Use **Undo**, **Redo** and **Save draft** as you go.

Theme and global styles go live as soon as you pick them. Everything else waits for you to publish.

For the menu and logo, see [Your menu and logo](/help/spaces/menu-and-logo).

## Publish from the builder

Select **Publish**. You see what changed, then choose **Publish now** or **Schedule** a date and
time. Each publish saves a version, and **Versions** lets you **Restore to draft** if you want an
earlier one back.

If the builder says your theme is still in preview, publishing saves your version, and your live
website keeps its current look until that theme is ready.

## Let members see their membership

Someone who is signed in to Frequency and is a member of your Space sees their membership in your
website header, as a small pill with the tier name. It opens your Memberships page on Frequency. It
shows on your free `frequencylocal.com` address. On your own domain the browser keeps the sign-in to
itself, so visitors there see the plain header. Nothing about anyone's other Spaces is shown.

## Take bookings from guests

If your Space has a service or open times set up, your website gets a Book page. A visitor books
right there with a name and an email. No Frequency account needed.

The time is held for thirty minutes while the guest confirms it from a link we email them. If they do
not click in time, the hold lapses and the time opens up again. Until they click, it shows in your
bookings as awaiting the guest's confirmation.

Once they confirm, the guest gets a confirmation email with a calendar file and a reminder before
their time. They land in your Space contacts, and they only get your news if they tick
**Keep me posted**.

A few limits for guests:

- A service that takes payment at booking cannot be booked by a guest yet.
- A guest cannot reschedule on their own. You can cancel a booking for them from your bookings list.
