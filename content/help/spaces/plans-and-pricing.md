People join free. A Space comes with Crew, the personal membership you fund at whatever amount you choose, and hosting it is free.

The beta runs through January 31, 2027. Selling starts at Business, and what Frequency takes on a sale depends on who brought the customer. This page explains the plan ladder and the fee rules; the [pricing page](/pricing) reads prices and rates from the live catalog and shows current terms.

## Find the numbers

The figures live in one place. The [pricing page](/pricing) reads every price, every yearly figure, and every rate straight from the plan catalog, so it is always the number you will be charged. This article deliberately does not repeat them: a number copied here would go stale the day the catalog moved, and the pricing page cannot. The fee rules are written out below, without the figures, under **Know what Frequency takes**.

## Learn the tier ladder

Members join. Crew hosts. A Space runs. Business adds tools. Collective connects. Network unites.

Two of those are personal and four are for Spaces.

- **Member.** Everyone. Free, for joining Circles, going to Events, and finding your people.
- **Crew.** Members who want to back the community and host more. Contribute what you want.
- **Space** (the free plan). Running a community, studio or business page.
- **Business.** A paid plan for Spaces that need its added tools and selling options.
- **Collective.** Separate groups that partner: a hub linking partner Spaces, a shared calendar, cross-listing, and Vera AI. Each partner keeps its own Space.
- **Network.** One organization with chapters: one membership in every chapter, central admins, chapter leaders, a network calendar and a roll-up report.

Two more plans sit beside the ladder:

- **Non Profit** is for verified nonprofits. It does everything Business does, with no finder's fee.
- White-label and client-site arrangements are handled directly. If you need one, [ask us](/help).

A Founding Year is a paid first-year offer for eligible purchases during the beta, and closes January 31, 2027. Its current availability, price and terms are shown on the [pricing page](/pricing). Founding Member recognition is free and separate from a Founding Year purchase. Annual billing has separate terms; check the [pricing page](/pricing) for current details. More in [The Beta offer](/help/spaces/beta-pricing).

## See what a free Space gives you

A free Space is a real plan, not a countdown. The pricing page shows its current tools and limits. Open a Space's page to share its events and invite people to gather.

Open your Space's QR codes settings to download its stock code as PNG or SVG. It opens your public Space page and stays fixed. It does not use your editable QR code allowance.

**A free Space sells nothing.** No paid tickets, bookings, shop or paid memberships. Selling starts at Business. A free Space takes donations only when its owner is a Crew member.

When an allowance is full, the next new thing you add is what waits. Nothing you made is hidden, moved, locked, or deleted, and you can always read and export it.

## See what Business turns on

Business turns on selling: paid tickets, paid memberships, bookings, your shop and donations. Your own people cost 0% on a paid Space plan, always, plus card processing. Check the [pricing page](/pricing) for current availability, tools and fees. Business also raises allowances and includes more operator seats and editable QR codes.

If an item requires a paid plan, Frequency shows the available plans and trial. You can keep using the free plan, and nothing you built is lost either way.

## See what Collective turns on

**Collective is for separate groups that partner.** It includes the Business tools, plus shared listings and a shared calendar with your partners, Vera AI included, five editable QR codes, and higher allowances. The finder's fee is the same as on Business. See [Collective](/help/spaces/collective).

## See what Network turns on

**Network is for one organization with chapters.** It includes everything Collective does except Partners (your chapters are Spaces you own), plus three chapters to start (up to 25), one membership across every chapter, central admins, a network calendar, and a roll-up report. The finder's fee is the same as on Business. You can start it yourself with the 14 day trial. See [Network](/help/spaces/network).

## Know what Frequency takes

**Your own people cost 0% on a paid Space plan, always, plus card processing.** Card processing still applies on every sale, and the seller pays it.

**There is one finder's fee.** It is charged once, with a cap, on the first purchase from a customer Frequency introduced. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for the current rate and cap. After that first purchase that customer is yours: 0%, plus card processing. The finder's fee is the same on Business, Collective and Network. Non Profit pays no finder's fee. A Founding Business keeps its locked rate. On a membership, the finder's fee applies to the first payment only, not to renewals.

**Only a paying account takes money.** A free Space sells nothing: no paid tickets, bookings, shop or paid memberships. Selling starts at Business.

**Donations.** A Space on Business or higher takes donations. A free Space takes donations only when its owner is a Crew member, and nothing else sells on it.

**Tips.** Tips go to Crew members and to people who own a Space on a paid plan. A free Member who is not Crew does not take tips. There is no platform fee on a tip, plus card processing.

Your own Space shows the fee it is on. Open **Manage**, then **Offerings & Money**, then **Get paid**, and look under **Your rate**, beside the account your money lands in. See [Get paid](/help/spaces/get-paid).

## Host from your personal account

Personal accounts host. They do not sell. A Member hosts a Circle and free Events. **Crew** carries a host kit with room for more Circles, Events, guests, and Journeys, and it is contribute what you want. See [Crew](/help/membership/crew). Selling runs through a Space, and it starts at Business. A Crew member can take tips. See the [pricing page](/pricing) for current details.

## Pick a plan

- Stay on the free Space while its tools and limits fit your needs.
- Take **Business** when its added tools and selling options fit your Space, when other people work with you, or when you run a Program others can join.
- Take **Collective** when you and other owners run separate groups that belong together.
- Take **Network** when one organization runs several chapters.
- If you are a verified nonprofit, take **Non Profit** and pay no finder's fee.

You can change plans whenever you like. Where to find it: **Manage**, then **Profile & Settings**, then **Plan and billing**. See [Billing](/help/spaces/billing) for how that works.
