Your Space does not have to pay anything to exist. Hosting is free, and it stays free: you pay when you start charging. Everything your Space does pay lives on one page, **Plan and billing**. This article covers what you find there and how each kind of payment works.

## Find it

1. Go to your Space and open **Manage**.
2. Pick **Profile & Settings**.
3. Open **Plan and billing**.

The owner and admins of the Space can open the page. Only the owner can start, change or cancel a plan, because the bill is the owner's. Teammates who are not admins do not see billing at all.

## Tell billing apart from Get paid

Everything here is what your Space pays. What your Space receives is a different page: **Manage**, then **Offerings & Money**, then **Get paid**. That is where the owner connects the account your money lands in, where you see which of your money paths can take payment today, and where you open your Stripe dashboard to change your bank details. See [Get paid](/help/spaces/get-paid).

The two are easy to mix up because both involve Stripe. Billing is your plan. Get paid is your earnings. Setting up one does nothing to the other.

## Change your plan

Pick the plan you want on the billing page. The card fields open under the button you pressed. A few things worth knowing:

- Business, Collective and Non Profit plans start with a **14 day free trial**, and so does Network.
- Yearly saves two months. A yearly plan costs ten months of the monthly price.
- Moving up takes effect right away. Moving down takes effect at the end of what you have paid for, so you keep what you bought.
- Your plan is what turns on selling. Paid tickets, paid memberships, donations, shop checkout and booking deposits open at Business. A free Space sells nothing, and it takes donations only when its owner is a Crew member. Everything else is an allowance: a free Space has every hosting tool with room to start, and Business and Collective raise the limits. When an allowance is full, only the next new thing waits. Nothing you made is hidden or locked.
- Collective is Business for separate groups that partner: a hub linking partner Spaces, a shared calendar and cross-listing, with each partner keeping its own Space. See [Collective](/help/spaces/collective). One organization with chapters takes [Network](/help/spaces/network) instead.
- Your plan is not the only switch. You can also turn a tool off for your own Space in **Manage**, then **Profile & Settings**, then **Page Tools**, and it stays off whether or not your plan includes it. So if billing says a tool is included and you still cannot find it, look there before you look at your plan.

See [Plans and pricing](/help/spaces/plans-and-pricing) for what each plan includes, and the [pricing page](/pricing) for what each one costs.

## Pay by card

Card payments run through Stripe. Your card details never touch Frequency: Stripe holds them, and we hold a reference.

- Once your Space is on a paid plan, select **Manage subscription** on the billing page. It opens the Stripe portal, where you can update your card, download invoices, change your plan or cancel.
- Renewals are automatic. Your card is charged when the term rolls over, and Stripe sends the receipt.

## Pay another way

Some Spaces settle up directly with us instead: cash, check, or transfer. When that is your arrangement, your billing page shows a **Billing agreement** instead of the Stripe portal, because there is no card to manage.

Your agreement shows:

- The plan it covers, and whether it is billed monthly or yearly
- **Your rate**, the amount you agreed to
- **Paid through**, the date your current term runs to
- **Paid by**, how you settle up

We remind you before it comes due. You get a notice 30 days out, another 7 days out, and one if it slips past the date. The reminders go to the Space owner and to every admin, in the app and by email, so a renewal does not sneak up on you.

When you pay, the crew records it and your paid-through date moves out a full term. The reminders reset with it.

Your agreement is managed by the crew, so it is read-only on your side. If something in it looks wrong, or your plans change, reach out and we will sort it.

## Know what Founding Business means

Some of the first businesses on Frequency carry a **Founding Business** mark on their Space and in the directory. It is recognition for showing up early.

If you were given a founding rate, it holds for as long as you keep the subscription up. That rate is not on offer any more: new Spaces pay the prices on the [pricing page](/pricing).

The badge says who you are, not what you pay. Your rate stays between you and the crew.

## Common questions

### Does anything charge me?

Only if you choose to subscribe. Nothing charges a Space that has not signed up. Free is a real plan with real allowances, so you can run a Space on it for as long as you like and pay when you want more room.

### What happens if I cancel?

You keep your plan until the end of the term you paid for, then the Space drops to Free. Nothing is deleted: your contacts, events, and pages stay where they are, and the tools that need a plan simply wait for you.

### Can two people manage billing?

Two people can see it, one can change it. The owner and admins can open the billing page. Only the owner can start a plan, change it, or open the Stripe portal, since the subscription is in the owner's name.

### Where are my receipts?

In the Stripe portal, behind **Manage subscription** on the billing page. If you settle up directly with us instead, ask the crew and we will send what you need.

### Why can I not find a tool my plan includes?

It may be switched off for your Space. Check **Page Tools** under **Profile & Settings** before you change your plan.
