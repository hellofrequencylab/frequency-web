People come once and drift. Regulars gives the ones who keep coming a reason to be known: a stamp card on your door code, and a reward you choose.

For how members see it, read [Regulars: check in, earn stamps, get the reward](/help/getting-started/regulars).

Where to find it: open your Space, go to **Manage**, then **Check in** in the row of links at the top.

## Check whether it is open for you

Regulars, counter scanners and hunts are opening in stages, so your Check in panel tells you where each one stands.

- If a section says it **is not open yet**, there is nothing to set up today. Your door code and the list of who checked in work the same either way.
- If you see the Regulars settings, you can set up your card. You can save a draft on any plan, and it turns on with the Business plan.

The rest of this page describes each piece once it is open.

## Set up Regulars

In the **Regulars** section of the Check in panel:

1. Name **The reward** and what it's worth. Make it a thing, like a coffee, not a percentage.
2. Pick how many visits earn it, from 3 to 12.
3. Optionally start a new card with one or two welcome stamps, so it begins with progress.
4. Optionally count a check-in only from inside your shop. That one needs a map pin on your profile. Everything else works without one.
5. Turn on **Regulars is on** and press **Save Regulars**.

Each check-in earns the member one stamp a day and 25 Zaps. A full card earns a six-character reward code. When they show it at the counter, hand the reward out from **Rewards to hand out**.

**Your regulars** lists the people who have come three times in 30 days, so you can greet them by name.

## Add a counter scanner

A card reader at your counter lets members tap a Frequency card instead of scanning. It counts like the door code: the same stamp and the same Zaps. It lives in the **Counter scanners** section of the same panel, and members ask for a card from your Space's page.

## Run a hunt

A hunt is a few Spaces sending people to each other. It sits in the **Hunts** section, under Regulars, and you can host one once your own Regulars card is on.

- 3 to 12 Spaces take part, over 2 to 10 weeks.
- You name the hunt and the reward for finishing, then invite each Space by its link. Each one answers from its own Check in panel.
- A hunt needs at least three stops that said yes before it can go live.
- A member who checks in at every stop inside the window earns your reward, plus 100 Zaps.

Pick Spaces your members would walk to. A hunt works because every stop sends people to a neighbor.

## Know what is still coming

A bigger kind of Hunt, with routes, missions and teams that play one run together, is being built. It is not open yet, so there is nothing to switch on in your Space for it today.
