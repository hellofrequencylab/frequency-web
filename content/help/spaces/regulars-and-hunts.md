People come once and drift. Regulars gives the ones who keep coming a reason to be known: a stamp card on your door code, and a reward you choose.

For how members see it, read [Regulars: check in, earn stamps, get the reward](/help/getting-started/regulars).

## Turn on Regulars

Regulars is part of the Business plan and up, and the Space needs a location. Open your Space, go to **Manage**, then **Check in**.

- Set the reward and what it's worth. The reward is a thing you name, like a coffee, not a percentage.
- Pick how many stamps fill a card, from 3 to 12.
- Optionally pre-fill one or two welcome stamps, so a card starts with progress.
- Optionally ask people to be at your Space when they check in.

Each check-in earns the member one stamp a day and 25 Zaps. A full card earns a six-character reward code. Hand the reward out from your check-in roster when they show it at the counter. A reward someone already earned keeps its title and value if you change the reward later.

## Counter scanners

A card reader at your counter lets members tap a Frequency card instead of scanning. It counts like the door code: the same stamp, Zaps and hunts. Members ask for a scannable card on your Space's page, and it shows up under **Member cards**.

## Run a hunt

A hunt is a few Spaces sending people to each other.

- 3 to 12 Spaces take part, over 2 to 10 weeks.
- You invite the Spaces, and each one accepts its stop.
- A member who checks in at every stop earns your completion reward, plus 100 Zaps.
- The public hunt page lists the route, and members see how many stops are left.

Pick Spaces your members would walk to. A hunt works because every stop sends people to a neighbor.
