Tired of checking back to see what is on? Add a Space's calendar to the one you already use, and its upcoming gatherings show up on their own.

## Add it

1. Open the Space and go to its **Calendar** tab.
2. Press **Subscribe to calendar**.
3. Pick **Add to Google Calendar** or **Add to Apple Calendar**, or use **Copy link** for any other calendar app.

The link looks like `webcal://frequencylocal.com/spaces/<space-slug>/calendar.ics`. You only add it once. After that your calendar app refreshes it on its own schedule, which can take a few hours.

## What it carries

- Published events that are public or unlisted, including repeating ones, which arrive as one series.
- A Network or Collective's link is network-wide: it brings its chapters' public events along, so one subscription follows the whole group. A chapter's own link carries only that chapter.
- A cancelled event drops off.

## What it leaves out

- Drafts, and dates a team is still planning.
- Anything from a private Space. A private Space has no public link.

## A feed is one-way

A feed sends events out to your calendar app. Nothing you change there comes back to Frequency. That makes it safe to hand to anyone, and it works in Google, Apple, Outlook and most other calendar apps.

Two-way sync with Google Calendar is a different thing, and it is for hosts. It is built and not switched on yet. See [Plan your calendar](/help/spaces/plan-your-calendar) for where it stands.

## For hosts

To share your Space's link, open your calendar in **Manage**, press the gear, and under **Sync and feeds** choose **Copy public feed**. Your private team feed is separate and shows more. See [Plan your calendar](/help/spaces/plan-your-calendar).
