A lot of the people who hold a neighborhood together are parents. If that is you, kids do not rule you out of hosting. They change the format.

## Pick a format that works with kids

Choose something that still works if a kid wanders in:

- A walk with strollers.
- A park meetup where kids play nearby.
- A morning coffee after drop-off.
- A craft or game night where kids are welcome.

Short and regular beats long and rare.

## Say whether kids are welcome

Say it plainly in the Circle or event description: "Kids welcome" or "Adults only this time." People decide faster when they know.

## Share the load from the first night

Recruit a co-host early. When one of you is on bedtime duty, the other runs it. [Circle roles](/help/leading/circle-roles) let you hand someone real responsibility, so nobody carries all of it.

## Cancel without guilt

It happens. Cancel the event so people get the update, and keep the standing time. One missed week is nothing. See [why groups fizzle](/help/leading/why-groups-fizzle) for what actually ends a group.
