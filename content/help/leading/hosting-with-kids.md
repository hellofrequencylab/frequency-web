A lot of the people who hold a neighborhood together are parents. If that's you, kids don't rule you out of hosting. They change the format.

## What format works with kids?

Pick something that still works if a kid wanders in. A walk with strollers. A park meetup where kids play nearby. A morning coffee after drop-off. A craft or game night where kids are welcome. Short and regular beats long and rare.

## Should I say kids are welcome?

Yes, say it plainly in the Circle or Event description: "Kids welcome" or "Adults only this time." People decide faster when they know.

## How do I keep it from falling on me?

Recruit a co-host from the first night. When one of you is on bedtime duty, the other runs it. Your [Circle roles](/help/leading/circle-roles) let you share the load without anyone carrying all of it.

## What if I have to cancel?

It happens. Cancel the Event so people get the update, and keep the standing time. One missed week is nothing. See [Why groups fizzle](/help/leading/why-groups-fizzle) for what actually kills a group.
