A cluster of Circles in one area is a **Space that contains other Spaces**. Frequency used to call that a Hub (and a cluster of Hubs a Nexus). Those words still show up in staff tools. On the member site they are Spaces, with a parent Space when one Space sits inside another.

## What this means for you

- You still join **Circles**, not the cluster.
- A sibling Circle nearby lives on the parent Space's Circles list.
- Old links that said `/hubs/...` or `/nexuses/...` open the matching Space.

New here? Start with [finding and joining a Circle](/help/getting-started/join-a-circle).
