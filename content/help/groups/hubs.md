Looking for a Hub? It is a Space now. Frequency used to call a cluster of Circles in one area a Hub, and a cluster of Hubs a Nexus. On the member site those are simply Spaces, and one Space can sit inside another.

## What this means for you

- You still join **Circles**, not the cluster.
- Circles a Space runs are listed on that Space's **Circles** page.
- Old links that said `/hubs/...` or `/nexuses/...` open the matching Space.

## Find Spaces near you

Tap **Spaces** in the left rail to browse the directory. Open one to see its Community wall, its calendar, and the Circles it runs.
