Want to talk with the people at a Space? Every Space has a **Community** tab: a wall where the team and its members post, and replies sit under each post.

**Where to find it:** open a Space and tap **Community**.

## Read and post

- Type in the box at the top of the wall to share something with the Space.
- Reply under any post to join the conversation.
- Beside the wall you will see a little about the Space, how many members and followers it has, what is coming up on its calendar, and the Circles it runs.

If the box is not there, the wall tells you why and shows a **See how to join** button. A few common reasons:

- **You are not signed in.** Sign in first.
- **Posting is for members.** The Space keeps posting to its team and members. Join to post.
- **The wall is for members.** Some Spaces keep the whole conversation for the people inside. You will see "This wall is for members" until you join.

## Follow or join

Following a Space lets you read a public wall. Whether followers can also reply and react is up to the Space. Members can do both, and can post unless the Space keeps posting to its team.

## If you run the Space

Open **Community** on your Space and tap **Wall settings**. You choose:

- **Who can read the wall**: **Anyone**, or **Members only**.
- **Who can post**: **Team and members**, or **Team only**.
- **Followers can reply and high five**: a switch that lets followers join in on a public wall.

From the same page you can pin or hide a post, review anything that was reported, and ban someone from the wall. Your team can also mark a post as **Announce**, so it shows on the wall as a notice from the team.

Starting the wall is simple: post first. A first post from you gives everyone else something to reply to.

## What this is not

- **Circles** is the tab beside it. It lists the Circles the Space runs. Each Circle has its own feed and its own members.
- **People** is the directory of the Space's members.
- **Contact** is a different door. Community is where people at a Space talk to each other. Contact reaches the Space itself, privately, through its own form. It shows only on Spaces that have set up a way to reach them.
- Posts on your home feed are separate. The wall is only this Space.

An older link ending in `/discussion` still works. It brings you to the Community wall.
