A Space's conversation lives on its **Discussion** tab. That tab is the Space's own Circle, the same room you would open from Circles. Posts and comments stay there. It is not a second feed, and it is never called Community.

## Finding it

Open a Space you can see. If Discussion is in the menu, tap it. You will see the same composer and the same posts that Circle already has. **Add a comment** sits under each post.

If you have not joined yet, the page tells you how. A waitlist spot is not a seat in the conversation.

## If you run the Space

Discussion appears for you even when the Circle is off, so you can turn it on. That switch lives under Manage, on Circles. Turning it on is what opens the room for everyone else.

People who have not joined the Space do not get a tab over an empty room.

## What this is not

- **Circles** still lists every group this Space runs.
- **Your people** is still the staff roster.
- Posts on your home feed are still your wall. Discussion is only this Space.
