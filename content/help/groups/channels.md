Channels are the topics Frequency is organized around, so you can explore by what you are into, not just where you are. Each Channel holds the Circles practicing it, a forum, and an open room. Some also run a Program you can start a Chapter of.

**Where to find it:** open the **Community** menu in the header and choose **Channels**. There is also a **Channels** link on the Circles page.

## Tune in

Open a Channel and tap **Tune in**. That follows it:

- Forum posts from the Channel reach your feed.
- You can post in the forum and in the open room.

Changed your mind? Tap **Tuned in** on the Channel to tune out. Nothing else changes.

## Look around a Channel

Every Channel has four tabs:

- **Home**: a quick welcome and the latest from the forum.
- **Feed**: the full forum. Talk shop, share, swap notes.
- **Circles**: the Circles practicing this Channel. On a Program's Channel this tab is called **Chapters**.
- **About**: what the Channel covers and how it works.

## Join the open room

Every Channel has one open room, a live chat for the whole topic.

- Anyone can read it.
- Tune into the Channel to post.
- Open it with the **Open room** button on the Channel. Rooms for Channels you are tuned into also show up in your [Messages](/help/connecting/messages).

## The founding topics

The founding Channels are curated by the Frequency team, so the list stays focused:

- Spirituality
- Movement
- Holistic Health
- Human Relating
- Activism
- Creative
- Business Support

## Channels and your Circle

- Every Circle leads with its primary Channel, which tells people at a glance what it is about.
- A Circle can carry up to three Channels. The first one listed is the primary, and the Circle shows up under each of them.
- Hosts pick the Channels in the Circle's settings and can change them later.
- You can also start a Circle from inside a Channel: open it and tap **Start a Circle**.

## Programs and Chapters

Some Channels run a **Program**: a community model with a blueprint anyone can start locally. Each local group is a **Chapter**, a regular Circle running the Program's model in its own city.

- On a Program's Channel, open the **Chapters** tab. Tap **Near me** to sort them by distance.
- Tap **Start a Chapter** to run one where you live.
