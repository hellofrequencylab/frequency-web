A **Program** is a community model with a blueprint. A **Chapter** is one local group running that model where you live. Find a Chapter near you and join it, or start one yourself: you get a private draft built from the blueprint, which you shape and publish when you are ready.

**Where to find it:** open the **Community** menu in the header, choose **Channels**, and open a Program's Channel.

## Understand the two words

**A Program is a [Channel](/help/groups/channels) that carries a blueprint.** The blueprint is a saved setup for a group: what it does together, how often it meets, the format, and what everyone agrees to. Programs are run by Frequency or by a business Space. Either way, the Program lives at a Channel you can open, tune into, and read, same as any other.

**A Chapter is one Circle running a Program's model in its own city.** That is the only thing the word means. In every other way it is an ordinary [Circle](/help/getting-started/what-is-a-circle):

- It has a Host, and that Host is whoever started it.
- It has the same member cap as any Circle.
- It has its own gatherings, feed, members, and settings.
- It sits in the Circles directory like everything else, so people find it by searching or browsing, not only through the Program.

## Find a Chapter near you

1. Open the Program's Channel.
2. Go to the **Chapters** tab.
3. Tap **Near me**.

Your browser asks for your location once, and the list re-sorts closest first. We do not store it. Chapters that have not set a place stay at the end. If you skip the permission, the list stays in its default order and you can still read every Chapter's city on its card.

Found one you like? Open it and join, like any other Circle.

## Start a Chapter

Open the Program's Channel and tap **Start a Chapter**.

1. You get a **private draft** built from the Program's blueprint. Nobody else can see it. You are the Host.
2. You land in the Circle builder with the blueprint already filled in: the name, what the group does, the meeting rhythm, the format, the agreements.
3. Change whatever you want. Set your night, your place, your own words.
4. When it is ready, tap **Publish**. That is when it goes live, joins the Program's Channel, and shows up in the directory.

There is no approval step. The draft is yours from the first tap, and nothing is public until you publish it.

Any signed-in member can start a Chapter. A Chapter is a Circle you host, so it counts toward the Circles you can host: a free Member hosts one, and **Crew** hosts more. New to hosting? Read [how to start a Circle](/help/leading/how-to-start-a-circle) first. All of it applies, because a Chapter is a Circle.

## Know what the blueprint decides

The blueprint decides what your Chapter **starts with**:

- The name and the one line describing it
- What the group does together
- The meeting rhythm and the gathering format
- The suggested size
- The agreements the group holds

The blueprint does **not** decide:

- When or where you meet
- Who joins, or who you host with
- What you post, or what you say to your members
- Anything at all after you publish

You can edit every one of those fields before you publish, and keep editing afterward from the Circle's settings.

## If a Program is paused

The Space or the crew running a Program can pause it. While it is paused:

- Its Channel page is hidden.
- No new Chapters can start.
- Existing Chapters keep everything: your Circle, your members, your gatherings, your feed.

When they resume it, the Channel comes back.

## Questions people ask

### Is my Chapter mine?

Yes. You are the Host of a Circle you created. Your members are your members, your gatherings are yours, and the settings are yours to change. The Program owner does not host your Chapter and cannot post in it or run it for you.

### Do I have to run it exactly their way?

No. The blueprint is a starting point, not a rulebook. It exists so you do not have to invent a format from nothing on a Tuesday night. Change what does not fit your room, your city, or your people.

If your version drifts far enough that it is not really the Program's model anymore, the honest move is to take it out of the Channel and run it as your own Circle. The Frequency crew can also move a Circle in or out of a Channel.

### What if the Program updates its blueprint later?

Nothing happens to your Chapter. It copied the blueprint the moment you started it, so a later update only affects Chapters started after that. Once yours exists, it is yours.

### Can I leave the Program and keep the Circle?

Yes. Open the Circle's settings and change its Channels. The Circle keeps its Host, its members, and its gatherings either way. A paused Program will not take a Circle back in until it resumes.

### Can I start a Chapter if there's already one in my city?

Nothing stops you. Check the **Chapters** tab first, though. Joining the one that exists is usually better for both of you than splitting six interested people across two small groups. Circles are meant to stay small, and a full one can seed a new one nearby.

### How is this different from remixing a Starter Circle?

The mechanics are the same: a blueprint becomes a private draft you own and publish. The difference is where it lands. A Starter Circle makes a standalone Circle. A Chapter joins a Program's Channel, so it shows up alongside every other Chapter and people looking for that model can find yours.

### Who can see my draft before I publish it?

Only you. A draft is hidden from the directory, from search, and from the Program's Chapters list until you publish.
