Reference frame · mobile · OWN-019

The Community, on a phone

One breakpoint, at 900px. Above it the page is the desktop frame; below it these three things move, and nothing else does. The frames are the real page rendered at 390px, not a redraw, so what you see here is what the CSS does.

Above the fold

Hero, then the four Pillars

  • One image request above the fold, and it is the hero. Nothing else loads eagerly.
  • Hero type drops to the clamp floor; the two buttons stack full width and stay above 44px.
  • Pillars go 4-up to 2-up. They stay buttons, not a select: the point is seeing all four at once.

The editorial run

ZigZag stops zigzagging

  • Every beat becomes image then text, in that order, on both beats. The desktop reverse flip is dropped rather than alternated: alternating on one column reads as a mistake.
  • The Statement and the marquee keep full bleed. The marquee speed is unchanged and reduced motion parks it.
  • The ladder rows keep the numeral gutter at 2.6rem, so the five words stay a column and not a list.

The open question, answered

The tour becomes a snap rail

  • The step list moves above the screen and turns into a horizontal snap rail at 78% width, so step two is always half visible and the row reads as scrollable.
  • Tapping a step scrolls nothing: the screen below swaps in place, so the thumb never loses its position.
  • The mock screen keeps all three rows. It is the payload of the page, so it does not truncate.
