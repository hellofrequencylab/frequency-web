A member as an object.

```jsx
<PersonCard avatar={<Avatar name="Sana Idris" size={40} />} name="Sana Idris" handle="sana"
  role="Guide" line="Leads Thursday Sound Bath" rank={<RankBadge rank="adept" />}
  action={<Button size="sm" variant="ghost">Message</Button>} />
```

- No role chip for member or crew — the chip means leadership or the system voice.
- One line of context, not a bio. The bio lives on the profile.
