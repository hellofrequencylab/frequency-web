Foundations · Type

Body · Nunito

Nunito is the readable voice: rounded, warm, and legible small. It carries all body copy and every in-app heading. Headings sit at 700, not 800 — Nunito is already soft, and extra-bold makes it read scholastic. Bold plus negative tracking is what gives it an edge.

Roles

The in-app page title
--text-page-title · 1.5rem / 700
The lead paragraph, which introduces a section without repeating its heading.
--text-lead · 1.25rem / 400
Default body copy at a relaxed 1.65 line height. This is the size most sentences on a marketing page are set in, and the floor for anything a member has to read carefully.
--text-body · 1rem / 1.65
In-app body and descriptions. Denser screens use this step so more fits without shrinking the reading size below the floor.
--text-body-sm · 0.875rem
Meta: counts, timestamps, labels. This is the FLOOR for content.
--text-meta · 0.75rem

Extrabold 800

One numeral or one deliberate emphasis. Not titles.

Headings are 700. 800 and 900 are reserved for a single numeral or one deliberate emphasis.

Negative tracking on titles. --tracking-tight for cards and sections; the display step for page titles.

0.75rem is the floor. Below --text-meta is chrome only — a rank chip, a tab count, a legal line.

Relaxed leading for prose. 1.65 on anything longer than two lines.
