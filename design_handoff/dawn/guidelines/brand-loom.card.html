A Loom scene

Frame a product moment as a screen or card with faint inner cards. Figures stay minimal and are recognizable by pose, never by face.

Always

  • Filled shapes, flat and warm, in the DAWN palette.
  • Amber led, teal accent. Color comes from tokens.
  • Big rounded corners, roomy spacing, calm product-UI feel.
  • One focal point per illustration.

Never

  • Thin line-art only, or an outline style.
  • Gradients, drop shadows for drama, 3D, isometric.
  • Photorealism, faces, fingers, realistic anatomy.
  • Raw hex, clutter, more than one idea per frame.
