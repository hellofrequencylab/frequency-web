Foundations · Space & feel

Radius · by role

Three role tokens carry the feel, and they are the contract a skin retunes — Midnight sharpens control and card without touching anything else. Reach for the role token in anything a skin should be able to restyle; the step scale is for shapes that are not role-bound.

The role contract

--radius-control · 0.5rem

Buttons, inputs, small controls.

--radius-card · 1rem

Cards.

--radius-pill · 9999px

Pills, chips, avatars.

The step scale

--radius-sm · 6px

Chips, badges, tiny date squares.

--radius-md · 10px

Legacy control alias.

--radius-lg · 14px

Legacy.

--radius-xl · 16px

In-app cards and tiles.

--radius-2xl · 24px

Marketing feature cards and framed media.

Role first. A button takes --radius-control, not --radius-md, so a skin can retune it.

Never mix radii on one object. A card and the image inside it share a family, not two unrelated values.

Marketing rounds more. 2xl on feature cards and framed media; in-app stays at xl.

Pill means pill. If it is a chip or an avatar it is fully round, not "almost".
