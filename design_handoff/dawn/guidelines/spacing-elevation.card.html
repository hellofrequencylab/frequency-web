Foundations · Space & feel

Elevation · soft warm depth

Depth is warm and soft, never a hard black edge. Every shadow is tinted near-espresso at low alpha with a wide blur, and each step pairs a tight contact shadow with a wider one — a single big blur is what makes an object look pasted on.

A resting card. The default.
