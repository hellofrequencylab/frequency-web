The three docks

One question decides where a control lives: whose is it — the system’s, mine, or this page’s? Answer that and there is exactly one dock it can go in, which is why nothing in the product is ever offered twice.

Where each dock sits

System · top rightRegion, account, billing, appearance, language, help, sign out.
You · bottom leftProfile, standing, journal, notification prefs — then what you run: Circles, events, listings, Spaces, payouts.
Score or page · bottom rightMember: the Vault — sparks, the stash, streak, season. Operator: this page's stats and switches.

What belongs in each

Top right

The system

  • Which region am I in
  • Account and security
  • Plan and billing
  • Appearance: skin and mode
  • Language, data export
  • Help, shortcuts, sign out

Bottom left

You, and what you run

  • Profile, standing, journal, saved
  • Notification preferences
  • My Circles, events, listings
  • Business Spaces, QR studio
  • Payouts, orders and passes
  • Switch to operator view

Bottom right

The Vault, or this page

  • Sparks and today's loose sparks
  • Stash — the one primary action
  • Streak, freezes, the week
  • Season counts and recent ledger
  • Operator: four page stats
  • Operator: that page's switches

Never twice. A control appears in exactly one dock. The streak left the top bar when the Vault took it.

One shell. All three use the same popover: glass, lift-3, cue-pop, Esc or an outside click to close.

Open inward. Each dock grows toward the middle of the screen, never off its own edge.

Score is never a scold. The Vault has no red, no countdown, and nothing purchasable with money.

Page settings are the page’s. Global admin lives in the rail; the dock holds only what belongs to the screen you are on.

Rails are not docks. The rails carry content. Controls belong to the docks.
