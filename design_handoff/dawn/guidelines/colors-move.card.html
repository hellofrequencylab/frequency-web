Move · the Get Moving accent

Mindless has two doors. Be Still is amber; Get Moving is Move. It is pushed deliberately bluer than the green-leaning teals so a moving timer never reads as a completed one, and it sits opposite amber on the wheel so the two doors are legible at a glance.

The two doors, side by side

Be still

Breath, sit, wind down. Amber.

Get moving

Walk, stretch, shake it out. Move.

Move is a door, not a state. It colours the movement half of Mindless. It never means success, progress or info.

Bluer than success on purpose. If a build makes them look alike, push Move bluer — not success greener.

One door at a time. A practice screen shows one accent; the other door stays neutral until chosen.

Never on chrome. Rails, bars and menus stay amber. Move lives inside the practice surface.
