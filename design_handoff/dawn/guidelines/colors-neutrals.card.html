Foundations · Color

Neutrals · surfaces & text

Every neutral is warm. The in-app canvas is a light cream, content cards are white, and the app frame sits a step down from the canvas so the frame reads as frame and the white content is what pops. The marketing canvas is warmer sand. Text ink is a soft warm charcoal, about 10:1 on canvas — printed, not harsh.

No cold grey. Every neutral carries a warm cast. A #F5F5F5 anywhere reads instantly as a foreign component.

Chrome sits below canvas. The frame is darker than the page it holds, so white content cards read as objects on it.

Hairlines, not rules. One pixel of warm border. Depth comes from the shadow tokens, not from heavier lines.

Subtle is not body. text-subtle is for a legal line. Content never goes below text-muted.
