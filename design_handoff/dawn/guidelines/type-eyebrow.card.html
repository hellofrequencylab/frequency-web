Eyebrow & kicker

Three small devices carry most of the editorial feel. The eyebrow says where you are, in tracked uppercase at locked spacing. The kicker is the italic deck under a headline, and it is where amber lands. The accent marks exactly one keyword — two accents in one heading and neither reads.

The house opening

For hosts, studios and spaces
You have the room
we bring the rhythm

Then the lead, plain and short. Nobody opens a studio to run software.

The parts

Eyebrow

.eyebrow — uppercase, 600, tracking locked at 0.18em. Never re-track it per page.

Kicker

The italic deck. Amber on cream, amber on ink. One line, lower case.

One keyword

Accent a single word per heading, and only when it carries the meaning.

Tracking is locked. --tracking-eyebrow is 0.18em everywhere. An eyebrow that tracks differently per page reads as a different site.

One accent per heading. Two coloured words cancel each other.

The kicker is lower case. It is a spoken half-sentence continuing the headline, not a second title.

Eyebrows locate, they do not sell. "For hosts" — not "The best way to run your studio".
