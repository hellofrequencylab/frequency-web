Group, don’t box

The single fastest way to make an interface look generic is to put a card around every row. A card means "this is a distinct object you can act on". A list of related things is one group: a small tracked title, hairlines, and air. This is the rule the whole in-app register is built on.

When a card IS right

An entity

A Circle, an event, a listing — something with its own page.

A decision

A plan to pick, a Space to claim. Something you choose between.

A distinct object on a page

A stat block, a media frame. Not a row in a list of rows.

One card per object. If two cards sit in a list and behave identically, they are rows.

Group with a title. A tracked label plus space does everything a box does, without the noise.

Hairlines over frames. Separate rows with one warm border, and only between them.

Air is the cheapest tool. Before adding a container, add spacing and see if the problem is gone.
