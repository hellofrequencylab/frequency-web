Foundations · Space & feel

Texture, light & lift

Four families and a law for each. Everything here is decorative: absolutely positioned, pointer-events none, carrying nothing a screen reader needs. The purpose is craft at close range — a dark band with nothing on it reads as a hole.

Lift · how far off the page a thing sits

.lift-1

Rests on the page. Rows, chips, inline panels. No hover travel.

.lift-2

The card. Default for anything clickable on a marketing page — hover raises it 2px.

.lift-3

Floats. One per page: the hero object, the sticky action card, an open dock.

Light · where the amber lands

.sheen

A slow diagonal sweep on hover. Moves light, not the object.

.halo

A tight bloom behind one small object.

.spot

A wide overhead spotlight. Cooler than amber-glow.

.edge-light

A one-pixel warm rim along the top lip.

Texture · one per band, never two

.grain

Still film grain. The only texture that works on cream and ink alike.

.scanlines

Fine light rule, an LED wall up close. Ink only.

.dot-grid

Sparse lattice for schematics and operator pages.

.vignette

Corners pulled down so centred type holds. Photography only.

Shape & seam

.arc-top

The soft shoulder: cream rises out of ink without a seam. One per page.

.rule-amber

A hairline that warms at the centre. Between beats of the same tone.

.light-strip

The signature seam. At a dark/light transition, and nowhere else.

Two shadows per elevation. A 1px contact shadow so the object rests on something, plus one wide soft shadow. A single big blur looks pasted on.

One texture per band. Stacked textures read as noise. The exception is photography, which may carry vignette plus one.

One lift-3 per page. Three floating things read as none.

Sheen answers the cursor, lift answers the hand. Dark bands sheen; cards lift. Never both on one object.

Glass only over movement. A sticky header or a dock over content. Over flat colour it is a tint with a cost.

All of it yields. Every transform and animation is off under prefers-reduced-motion.
