Mechanics

  • Sentence case for every headline and button.
  • No em dashes. Periods, commas, parentheses, or rewrite.
  • Contractions always. One exclamation point per screen, usually zero.
  • Emoji: rare, single, game UI and social only. Never on a practice page.
  • Never narrate the reader's feelings. The number one cause of cringe.

Jargon is permitted deep inside a practice page or an article. Never on a card, headline, notification, caption, or meta description.

The skeptic test. Read it aloud to someone who would say "that's not really my thing." If it does not still sound like it could be for them, rewrite it.
