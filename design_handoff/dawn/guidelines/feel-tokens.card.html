Reach for the role token, not the step scale, in anything a skin should be able to restyle. The step scale (sm / xl / 2xl) stays for shapes that are not role bound: a date square, a marketing media frame.

--motion-base

--motion-slow

Fast for a press or hover tint, base for anything that arrives (the cue-pop beat), slow for a wash or scroll reveal. Nothing in the system exceeds 700ms, and reduced motion collapses all three.
