Foundations · Color

Semantic states

Four states, used sparingly. Success is a teal so "done" stays cohesive with Signal and green never enters the palette. Danger is the rarest colour in the system: it belongs to destruction and failure, never to urgency, scarcity or a streak that lapsed.

The four states

--color-success
#11827A
Done, confirmed, paid. A teal.
--color-warning
#B07515
Needs attention, nothing is broken.
--color-danger
#BA3B30
Destructive actions and real failures only.
--color-info
#2F6FB0
Neutral notice. Rare — most notices are just text.

Their tints

--color-success-bg
#D7EFEA
--color-warning-bg
#F6ECD8
--color-danger-bg
#F7E4E1
--color-info-bg
#E3EDF7

Green never appears. Success is #11827A. A true green would break the warm-editorial palette on sight.

Danger is for damage. Deleting, failing, losing. A missed day is a hollow dot, not a red one.

Never colour alone. Every state carries a word or a glyph too — colour is the second signal, not the only one.

Tints, not borders. Reach for the -bg tint before adding a coloured left border.
