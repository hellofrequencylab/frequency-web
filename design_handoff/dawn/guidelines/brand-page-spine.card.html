The rules that keep the cadence

  • Never two adjacent sections in the same tone. No canvas onto canvas.
  • Max two ink bands per page, one mid-page and one at the close.
  • No ink band without its light-strip seam, top and bottom.
  • Shared vertical rhythm and container widths. Surprise comes from content and imagery, never from spacing that jumps.
  • If a pattern appears twice it is a component. Pages never hand-roll a hero, a button, or FAQ chrome.
  • When a page feels thin, add a beat. Do not pad the copy.
