Rules
Real counts onlyEvery number is people who showed up. No vanity totals, no screen time.
One highlightOne bar, ring or node carries the accent per graphic. Everything else sits on the track.
Label the numberNumbers in Unbounded, labels in Hanken 13px, a time frame on every stat.
Breath for live onlyOnly today's node and live dots breathe. Reduced motion stops counts and loops.
