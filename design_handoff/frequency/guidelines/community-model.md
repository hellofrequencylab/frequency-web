# The community model (owner canon, verbatim)

> Source: owner, October 2026. Treat this as the canonical story of how community and businesses support each other. Quote it, don't paraphrase it, in pitch and marketing contexts. The final "honest note" is internal only and never appears in customer-facing material.

---

Frequency is a Community Collective. Businesses host, people show up, and the network pays attention to whoever actually gathers.

## How the community supports a Space

A Space is your business's home on Frequency. You host free. Inside it you open Circles, the rooms where a group actually meets, and you post Events, which is when the room is open. That's the whole shape, and it's the shape people already understand.

The network does the part a lone business can't do for itself. Every Event you publish lands on the one calendar everyone in your city reads. Every Circle you start shows up where people are looking for their people. Your Space sits in a directory sorted by standing, which means how often you gather and how many people were in the room, not how much you paid. Placement is earned, never sold. Nobody can buy their way above a studio that holds a real class every Tuesday.

The tools are the ones you'd otherwise rent from six companies. A QR code at the door that turns walk-ins into contacts. A free CRM that remembers who came and nudges you to reach out. Memberships you can sell from day one, with the member dropped straight into the Circle they paid for. Ticketing with refunds and a guest door. Programs your Circle moves through together, week by week. A page you can edit without calling anyone. All of it is on by default, and you pay nothing until you start charging.

When money does move, we take a share only of what we brought you. Someone finds you through Frequency and buys, we take a small cut. Your own regulars buy, you keep all of it, always. Non-profits pay nothing either way.

## How people support a Space

Members join free, forever. They come for the thing a feed can't give them: a place to go on Thursday. The app's job is to get them out the door and into your room.

So the game only pays for real-world acts. Check in at your Event, finish a practice with your Circle, bring a friend to their first gathering, and you earn Zaps. Scroll all evening and you earn nothing. A member who shows up three weeks running is worth more to you than a thousand followers, and Frequency is built to make that person.

People also support you the old way. They join your Circle, buy your membership, buy a ticket, tip after a good night, and the money goes to you through Stripe, not through us. They start their own Circles inside your Space, which is how a yoga studio becomes a running club and a book group and a Sunday potluck without the owner planning any of it. And the ones who keep showing up get asked to host, because the network notices who gathers people and hands them the next room.

## The loop

Someone arrives alone. They find a Circle. The Circle meets in your Space. The Space fills. More people find the Circle. That's it. We don't sell reach, we don't sell rank, and we don't sell your members back to you. We make the room easier to fill and take a slice only when we filled it.
