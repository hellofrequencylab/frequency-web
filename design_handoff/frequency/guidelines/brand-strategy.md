# Frequency™ Brand Strategy, v2
### Community Collective

v2 replaces v1 ("The Field Station"), which the owner rejected as too safe. Inputs for this version: the owner's gathering photos and facility concepts (`assets/photos`, `assets/facilities`), the owner's logo (`assets/logo`), the repo voice canon, and the research in §1.

---

## 1. Feeling into the people

### Who they are right now
The core is the "high-functioning lonely": 25 to 44, capable, employed, digitally saturated, wellness-adjacent, allergic to anything culty or salesy. Around them sit the Activity-First Man (comes for the run, the sauna, the game night) and the Latent Leader (already the organizer, wants rails and backup).

The numbers put a floor under the feeling:
- About one in five US adults reports significant daily loneliness. For 18 to 29 year olds it's 29%.
- US men 15 to 34 are 67% more likely than OECD peers to feel lonely on a given day.
- They're voting with their feet for structured in-person contact. Strava clubs nearly quadrupled in 2025.
- They're losing trust in the screen. 40% of US consumers say heavy AI use would lower their trust in a brand they love, double the year before.

### Where they're heading (2027 to 2030)
1. **The feed gets fake.** As more of the internet is generated, the only thing that can't be faked is a room full of people. Being physically there becomes the new status signal, and proof of presence (a check-in, a photo from the circle) becomes social currency.
2. **The body becomes the interface.** This group already tracks sleep, HRV and steps. Next they want to *feel* the change, not just see a chart. They'll respond to a product that matches their state, not one that tells them what their state is.
3. **Range beats calm.** Wellness spent a decade selling calm, and calm alone has gone a bit beige. The edge of the culture is now sober raves, ecstatic dance, sauna socials, breathwork that ends in a dance floor. People want to come *down* and come *alive*, ideally with the same people in the same week.
4. **Belonging needs a ritual.** Generic community doesn't hold. Repeated rituals do: the same time, the same place, the same faces. "Thursday" matters more than "community."

### What they need from Frequency
| Need | What it sounds like | What the brand must do |
|---|---|---|
| **Permission** | "Is this for someone like me?" | Low-stakes doors. Show real, mixed, normal people. No gurus, no six-packs as the default |
| **Regulation** | "I can't switch off." | Moments of stillness that actually slow the screen down: breathing motion, soft light, less on the page |
| **Aliveness** | "I want to feel something." | Moments that are loud on purpose: color, glow, rhythm, a reason to go out tonight |
| **Belonging** | "I want to be missed when I'm gone." | Faces and names, rituals that repeat, a Host who knows you |
| **Agency** | "Don't manage me." | You choose the energy. The app suggests, never pushes |

### How they'll respond to this direction
- **Yes** to light, color and motion that feel like a real room (string lights, a stage wash, golden hour). It reads as *experience*, not *app*.
- **Yes** to a product that changes with them. "It knew I wanted quiet" is a powerful feeling, as long as it's never creepy. So the system shifts on visible signals (time of day, what you opened) and always shows its override.
- **No** to spiritual costume (chakras, mandalas, crystal gradients) and to club costume (neon for its own sake). The skeptic test still rules the copy.
- **No** to anything that feels like it wants their attention. The rave is IRL. The app's energy should point you out the door.

## 2. The idea: **Everything has a frequency**

Frequency already says it in the name. Every place, gathering and practice has an energy, from stillness to full charge. A sunrise meditation circle is a low, slow frequency. A Friday yoga rave is a high one. Most of life sits in between.

**The brand makes energy visible.** The interface tunes itself to the frequency of what you're looking at and what time it is, the way a room changes when the lights go down.

- **Between a yoga temple and a rave.** One end is the temple (breath, stillness, candlelight, the circle). The other is the rave (rhythm, color, bloom, the crowd). Frequency is the whole dial, and the brand moves along it.
- **Community Collective** is the lockup line. It's what Frequency *is*. Campaign lines are separate and change.
- **The dial is the wayfinding.** Instead of color-coding features, the product color-codes *energy*. Mindless and Be Still sit low. Practices, Journeys and Circles sit in the middle. Events and gatherings run high. Utility surfaces (settings, Market, Run mode) sit at neutral, with no aura. You know where you are by how it feels.

### Campaign line candidates (not the lockup)
- **Find your frequency.** Literal, and a bit expected.
- **Come down. Come alive.** Captures the range in four words. *Recommended.*
- **A place to be human.** Already in the owner's concept render; warm, but generic.
- **Tonight, on purpose.** Leans on the spirit line ("Do things on purpose").

## 3. The system: two axes

### Axis 1: Time (automatic, overridable)
- **Day: the coast at golden hour.** Sand, linen, haze, sea glass, terracotta, sun. Taken straight from the beach meditation circle, the park yoga and the hoop dancer. The canvas is warm sand, and photos carry the light.
- **Night: the room with the lights down.** Two candidates for review, shown side by side in the brand book:
  - **A · UV Temple:** void violet, magenta, electric cyan, a touch of lime. From the yoga rave photo and the stage wash in the hall concept. More rave.
  - **B · Thermal:** slat black, amber LED, ember, teal water. From the thermal circuit and lobby concepts. More temple.
- The switch follows local sunset and sunrise. The member can pin day or night (override).

### Axis 2: Energy (by surface)
| State | Used for | Aura | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Still** | Mindless Be Still, Journal, onboarding calm-down, check-in confirmation | cool, soft, low saturation | 8s breath |
| **Flow** | Practices, Journeys, Circles, Messages | warm and balanced | 5s breath |
| **Charge** | Events, live gatherings, Quest moments, marketing hero | saturated, bright, high contrast | 0.5s beat (120 BPM) |
| *(Neutral)* | Settings, Market, Run mode, forms | none | none |

Energy is set by the surface, nudged by context (an event that's live right now runs hotter), and always dampened by reduced-motion settings.

### Signature effects
- **Aura:** a soft, slowly drifting light field behind hero areas and key cards. Three color stops plus a speed, all driven by tokens. It's the brand's main graphic device: no illustrations or patterns, just light.
- **Breath / beat:** one shared pulse (`--pulse`) drives every living element: the Mindless orb, live dots, the "tonight" chip. It's slow in stillness and on-beat in charge.
- **Bloom:** at night, primary actions and live elements glow like light on haze.
- **Glass:** nav, sheets and overlays sit on frosted glass over the aura or a photo.
- **Iridescent: the marketing signature.** A living pearl sweep (pink, lilac, aqua, mint, cream) on one key phrase per headline, the hero button, and rims on hero cards and ads. It reads as both temple (pearl, abalone) and rave (holographic, UV). In product it stays reserved for earned things (trophies, tickets, Crew), so the marketing promise and the in-app reward look the same.
- **Signal line (audio-reactive feel):** a live waveform that represents real activity (how many people are checked in, a gathering's energy). It moves faster when more is happening. It's never decorative noise.
- **Grain:** a light film grain over photos and auras, so everything reads as captured, not rendered.

## 4. Identity

### Logo
The owner's logo: a rounded, hand-cut wordmark with the **Community Collective** lockup line, plus the round **FR** icon. It's one color and flexible. In the system it's applied as a mask, so it can take ink, sand, glow or iridescent fills. Never stretch it, never re-letter it, never add effects other than the approved fills.

### Type
- **Unbounded:** display. Wide, round and energetic, cut from the same geometric cloth as the logo. It carries the charge (headlines, numbers, labels).
- **Instrument Serif italic:** the temple voice. Used sparingly for soft, human moments: a pull quote, a Practice title, "welcome back."
- **Hanken Grotesk:** UI and body. Quiet, legible, neutral, so the other two can perform.

The contrast between a loud wide sans and a soft italic serif *is* the temple-to-rave range in type.

### Color
See §3. Day: sand, linen, ink, with terracotta and sea glass as accents and haze, sun and sage in auras. Night A: UV. Night B: Thermal. Iridescent is shared and reserved for earned moments.

### Imagery
- **Day:** the owner's coastal photos. Golden haze, sand, blankets, barefoot, circles of people. Warm, slightly faded, grain allowed.
- **Night:** string lights, stage wash, UV face paint, slatted wood and amber light.
- **Facilities:** the owner's concept renders, used only as "what a Frequency space can feel like." They're labeled as concepts, never presented as real venues.
- No stock models. People are in the middle of something (mid-breath, mid-laugh, mid-stretch), not posing.

### Shape
Soft and generous: radius 16 to 36 on cards, orbs for anything alive (avatars, the Mindless orb, live dots). Hairline borders that disappear into the light. No hard sticker shadows, no left-border accents.

## 5. Voice

The voice canon is unchanged (plain sentences, proper nouns carry the magic, skeptic test, no em dashes). Energy changes the *tempo* of the copy, not the voice:
- **Still:** short, slow, lowercase-feeling. "Breathe out longer than you breathe in."
- **Flow:** friendly and specific. "Day 3. You showed up again."
- **Charge:** punchy, a time and a place. "Tonight. 9pm. The hall. Bring a friend."

## 6. Labs and the hook: from spectacle to Circle

> **Timing:** Frequency Labs (the physical third spaces) are a vision opening **late 2027**. Until then, the hook runs in partner Spaces (studios, cafés, clubs), and Labs content is *vision* content: it builds the case with the community and with companies for creating the third spaces. Never show a Lab as open. Facility renders are always labeled "Labs vision · late 2027".

The temple-to-rave energy does two jobs.
1. **It's the Frequency Labs experience.** Labs are the physical venues, so this is what it feels like to walk in.
2. **It's the marketing hook.** A yoga rave in a basement with glow sticks and a UV floor stops the scroll in a way "join a community" never will.

The hook only matters if it leads people into the Circle model. The design rule is that **every high-charge moment ends with a low-stakes invitation to something smaller and regular.**

| Step | Energy | What they see | The ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. The hook** | Charge, Night A | B-roll of the yoga rave, UV swirl floor, glow sticks. One huge line + a date | "Save my spot" (free) |
| **2. The room** | Charge → Flow | At a partner Space today (a Lab from late 2027): check-in by QR, wristband glow, the night itself | "Check in" (Zaps, proof of presence) |
| **3. The come-down** | Flow → Still | The last 10 minutes: lights low, everyone lying on mats. The Host says one sentence about Circles | Card on the phone: "Same people, smaller room. Thursday." |
| **4. The Circle** | Flow, Day | A weekly Circle near you, with faces from tonight | "I'm in" |
| **5. The return** | Flow | The next Lab night is a reunion, not a stranger party | "Bring a friend" (referral Zaps) |

Rules that follow:
- **Night A (UV) is the Labs and marketing palette.** It matches the b-roll (red wash, glow-stick cyan and green, UV swirls). Recommendation: Night A for Labs, events in charge, and all hook marketing. Night B (Thermal) for still and flow evenings in the app (sauna, sound bath, wind-down). That's the hybrid.
- **Spectacle never ends on spectacle.** A hook asset always carries the next smaller step ("Then: Thursday Circle, 12 people").
- **B-roll is background, not hero copy.** Use the rave footage darkened and graded under type, with grain. Faces stay candid; never crop to bodies.
- **Day stays the home.** The Circle and the app's daytime live in the coastal palette, so a rave-curious newcomer lands somewhere warm and normal the next morning.

## 7. The community model in the design system

Canon: `guidelines/community-model.md` (the owner's words, verbatim). Design consequences:

- **Energy is earned in the room.** The aura and energy states are already the brand's way of showing aliveness. The same visual language now carries **standing**: a Space or Circle that gathers often, with real people in the room, literally glows brighter. Standing is drawn as the Signal line and an aura intensity on directory cards. It's never a number you can buy, and there's no "promoted" or "sponsored" treatment anywhere in the system.
- **Three shapes, learned once.** Space (the home), Circle (the room), Event (when the room is open). Every surface shows which one you're looking at and how they nest: "Thursday Run Club · a Circle at Cedar Studio."
- **The game pays for real-world acts, so the UI celebrates them.** Check-ins, finished practices and first-time friends get the bloom, the breath and the Zaps reveal. Scrolling gets nothing. There are no streak counters on feeds and no reward animations for online actions.
- **Builder register.** Space owners hear the same plain voice, with proof over pitch: "14 people checked in Tuesday. 3 were new." It always lands on Captured, Booked or Returned, never revenue.
- **Money stays quiet and fair.** When pricing appears, it's one sentence: "Your regulars: 0%. People we bring you: a small share. Non-profits: nothing." No discounts, no strike-throughs, no countdowns.
- **The loop is the hero diagram.** Alone → Circle → Space fills → more people find the Circle. It's drawn as a ring (echoing the round FR icon and the meditation circle), not a funnel.
