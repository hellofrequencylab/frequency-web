01What we believe

Build circles, not walls.

A circle is open. Anyone can step into it, and it gets stronger as it grows. A wall keeps people out and keeps them in. Everything we build chooses the circle.

Open doors

Free, forever. A Space never shows a lock.

Yours to keep

Your people stay yours. Export anytime.

No walled garden

Gatherings anyone can find, invites anyone can open.

Many circles

Belong to as many as you like. They overlap.
