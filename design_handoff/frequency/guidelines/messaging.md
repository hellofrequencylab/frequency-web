# Frequency™ Messaging Framework
### Replaces every earlier content direction. Visual system unchanged.

Prepared as an agency of specialist desks: Research, Insight, Story, Messaging, Channel, and CTA. The brief from the owner: *"We are not explicitly doing yoga or raves. We're the vibe of both. There's something sacred about the work we're doing as a community who likes to party."*

---

## 1. Research desk: what's true right now
- **Loneliness is ordinary.** About one in five US adults reports significant daily loneliness, and young men in the US are among the loneliest in the West (Gallup 2025–26).
- **People lost the rooms, not the need.** Psychology Today, summarizing Harvard and Baylor research, describes religion's community benefits as "products of intentional social design": regular ritual gatherings, shared stories, communal singing, interdependence. Brunch, therapy and boutique fitness don't reliably replace them.
- **The sacred is being unbundled.** Young adults are building meaning from many practices rather than one institution, and the ones who do attend gatherings are attending more often.
- **Marketing is moving from audiences to communities.** 2026 trend reports agree: brands are investing in smaller, more engaged groups where people connect with each other, not just with the brand.
- **Word of mouth is the most trusted channel**, and IRL marketing is back, because audiences can spot what feels generic.
- **AI content costs trust.** Distrust of brands that use heavy AI doubled in a year (Fractl 2026). Provenance (real people, real rooms) is the edge.

## Two markets, one network
Marketing speaks to exactly two markets, always separately:
1. **Community:** people looking for their regulars. Voice: invitation. CTA: Find your Circle.
2. **Community Spaces:** the businesses and organizations that host them (studios, cafés, gyms, shops, clubs, non-profits). Voice: proof. CTA: Open your Space.

**Not in marketing:** Frequency Labs, buildings, facilities, or "our own venue." The Labs are a separate future vision (late 2027) with their own audience (partners and investors). Facility renders never appear in Community or Community Spaces marketing.

## 2. Insight desk: the tension we resolve
> **People want something that matters, with people they like, and they don't want to be told what to believe.**

The temple had the meaning, and the party had the joy. Most of modern life offers one without the other: wellness that's solemn and solitary, nightlife that's fun and forgettable. Frequency lives in the overlap: **gatherings that take people seriously and themselves lightly.**

We don't sell yoga, and we don't sell raves. We sell the thing both are reaching for: **being part of a community, on purpose.**

## 3. Story desk: the narrative
**The arc:** We lost our communities → the need didn't go anywhere → Frequency is the network that brings them back.

**Manifesto (master copy):**
> People have always gathered on purpose. Around fires, over long tables, in loud rooms and quiet ones. We sang, we ate, we danced, we sat with each other when it was hard.
>
> Somewhere along the way, we lost our communities. The feed moved in. We got a hundred contacts and no one to call on a Tuesday.
>
> Frequency is how we get them back. A network of rooms and the people who fill them. Circles that meet every week. Nights that run late. Mornings that start slow. Free to join, and built to get you off your phone.
>
> Sacred, not serious. Gather like it matters.

## Core belief: **Build circles, not walls.**
We build circles, not walls. A circle is open: anyone can step into it, and it gets stronger as it grows. A wall keeps people out and keeps them in. Everything we make chooses the circle.
- **Open doors.** Joining is free, forever. No velvet ropes, no "members only" until a Circle chooses to be small. A Space never shows a lock.
- **Your people stay yours.** Contacts belong to the Space or Host who met them. Export anytime. Regulars are 0%. We never sell your members back to you.
- **No walled garden.** Gatherings sit on an open calendar and public pages that search engines and people can find. Invites work for people who aren't on Frequency yet.
- **Many circles, overlapping.** One studio becomes a run club, a book group and a Sunday potluck. Circles can meet in any Space; people can belong to many.
Copy patterns: "Build circles, not walls." · "Open the door." · "Bring someone." · "Yours to keep." Never use: exclusive, members-only, VIP, locked, unlock.

## 5. Channel desk: how this reaches people now
1. **Invitation, not advertising.** Every asset is an invitation to a specific community at a specific time ("Thursday, 7pm, Cedar Studio. 12 people. Come."). No asset ends on the brand alone.
2. **Hosts are the media.** Hosts are the micro-creators. They post their own rooms on Frequency templates, and the brand amplifies them. The brand voice is the house; the Host's voice is the invitation.
3. **Proof of presence.** The content engine is real photos from real nights, shot on members' phones and graded with Frequency's light. No stock, and no AI people. Every post can be traced back to a gathering that happened.
4. **The night is the ad.** Big nights (music, movement, food, firelight) are the attention moments. Each one is designed to be filmed, and each one closes by inviting people into a Circle.
5. **Word of mouth is the funnel.** "Bring someone" is a first-class action. Referrals pay Zaps, and the first visit with a friend is celebrated.
6. **Search for the pain, not the practice.** Long-form content answers what people actually type at midnight: "how to make friends in a new city," "things to do alone on a weeknight," "third places near me."

## Web surface language
- The town calendar is called **What's happening** in nav, menus, labels and copy. Never "What's on".
- Side toggle labels: **Browse Community** / **Host Community**.
- Hero headlines lead with search terms (local, groups, events, near you) and don't promise a weekly commitment:
  - People: "Find local groups and events *near you.*"
  - Spaces: "Host local events and groups people *come back to.*"

## 7. Word list
**Use:** community, regulars, table, night, morning, regulars, show up, stay late, bring someone, the door, Thursday, on purpose, matters.
**Avoid on the surface:** yoga, rave, temple, sacred (except in "Sacred, not serious"), energy, vibe, tribe, journey, transform, unlock, community as filler.
