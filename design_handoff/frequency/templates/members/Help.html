Common questions
How do I check in?+

Open the event and tap Check in when you arrive, or scan the host's code. Zaps land when the host confirms.

What happens to Zaps at the end of a season?+

Zaps reset and set your rank for next season. Gems stay with you.

Can I hide from leaderboards?+

Yes. Settings, Privacy, then turn off local leaderboards.

How do I start a Circle?+

Go to Circles and tap Start a Circle. You need a place, a time and one other person.
