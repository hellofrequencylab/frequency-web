QuestionsPlainly answered

Before you ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. Finding, joining, checking in, playing and hosting cost nothing.

Where does my money go?

When you buy a ticket, membership or tip, it goes to the Space or Host you bought from.

Do I need an app?

Your phone camera can scan the door QR.

What does it cost to start?

Nothing. Open a Space, run Circles and post nights for free. You only pay a share when someone we bring you buys.

What counts as my regulars?

People who found you on their own, or who you brought in yourself. When they buy, you keep all of it.

Will you sell my members?

Never. Your contacts are yours.

Can money improve my standing?

No. Standing comes from how often you gather and who’s in the room.
