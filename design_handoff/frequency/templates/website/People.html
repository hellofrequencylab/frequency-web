QuestionsThe ones everyone asks

You’re not the only one wondering.

I don’t know anyone.

That’s most people on their first night. Circles are small on purpose, and the Host will introduce you.

I’m not spiritual.

You don’t need to be. Circles run around anything: moving, making, eating, sitting still, going out.

I’m not fit.

Plenty of Circles don’t involve exercise at all. The ones that do say so up front.

Is it free?

Yes. Joining, checking in, playing and hosting are free. Some Spaces sell tickets or memberships, and that money goes to them.
