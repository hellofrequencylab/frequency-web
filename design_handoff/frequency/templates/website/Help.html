For people

Is it really free?

Yes. Finding, joining, checking in, playing and hosting cost nothing.

I don’t know anyone. Will it be awkward?

That’s most people on their first night. Circles are small on purpose, and the Host will introduce you.

Do I need an app?

Your phone camera can scan the door QR. Everything else works in your browser.

How do Zaps work?

You earn them for real-world acts: checking in, finishing something with your Circle, bringing a friend. Scrolling earns nothing.

For Spaces

What does it cost to start?

Nothing. Open a Space, run Circles and post events for free. You only pay a share when someone we bring you buys.

What counts as my regulars?

People who found you on their own, or who you brought in yourself. When they buy, you keep all of it.

How do I rank higher?

Gather more often. Standing comes from how often you meet and who’s in the room, never from money.

Can a regular host under my Space?

Yes. Invite them as a Host and they run a Circle under your Space. You see every check-in.

Money

Where does my money go?

When someone buys a ticket, membership or tip, it goes to the Space or Host they bought from.

How do refunds work?

The host refunds from their event in one click. The money goes back to the original card.

Do non-profits pay anything?

No. Non-profits get every Business feature with no network share.

Your account

Can I take my contacts with me?

Your contacts are yours. We never sell them.

How do I leave a Circle?

Open the Circle and choose Leave. Nobody is notified.

How do I change notifications?

Open Settings from your avatar menu and pick what you hear about.

PoliciesPlain summaries

Build circles, not walls.

Privacy

Your contacts and your members are yours. We never sell them, and we only email you about things you asked for.

Terms

Show up, be kind, and host honestly. Spaces set their own prices and refunds. We take a small share only on people we bring you.

Accessibility

We build to WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard navigation, readable contrast and reduced motion when you ask for it. Tell us when we miss.
