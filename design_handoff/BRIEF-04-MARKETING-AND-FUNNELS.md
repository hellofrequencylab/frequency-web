## 1. The governing law

The primary audience (the high-functioning lonely, brief 02) is **allergic to
funnels**: marketing that *persuades* repels them; marketing that *recognizes*
them converts. Every asset aims for "a magical connection, not an advertisement."

## 3. The funnels

### Consumer (Seeker)

- **Primary beta funnel:** every sitewide "Join the Beta" CTA → passwordless
  sign-in (magic link + Google) → the cinematic beta induction → real member. One
  constant opens or closes the whole funnel.
- **The induction** (throwaway by design, deleted at public launch): 6 beats,
  under 90 seconds, dark-to-light, gated only on **The Oath** — three checkboxes:
  "I agree to break things on this website" · "I agree to submit bug reports and
  screenshots" · "I agree to be a Frequency Web Founder." Framing: "This isn't a
  product yet. It's a promise." Button: "I'm in."
- **Funnel A (Seeker):** entry via home + pain-SEO articles + `/discover` → value
  moment "a room near me, this week" (soft CTA: "Find a Circle near you. Bring
  nothing.") → proof (live stat bars, real first names) → `/start` self-ID quiz
  (account last) → activation. **Activation metric: first RSVP within 7 days;
  attending is the true aha.**
- **Activation surfaces:** Complete Your Profile card (25-Gem drop at 100%),
  "Founder's First Week" 7-task set ending in a Founding Founder badge, Vera
  surfacing the single next best action.

### Operator (Latent Leader / business)

- **Funnel B (Latent Leader):** leader landing + articles → "Your last event
  didn't fizzle because of you" → proof (bylined Host stories) → CTA "Get the
  first-night format" → activation: host a standing Circle, recruit a co-host.
- **Five funnel doors** (`/for/coaches` · `/studios` · `/hosts` · `/communities` ·
  `/nonprofits`): chrome-free pages, fixed skeleton (Hero → AssuranceBar →
  Problem → HowItWorks → Features → LoopDiagram "Every hello, remembered." →
  Pricing → Proof → Mission → FAQ → FinalCTA), one CTA label **Start free** →
  minimal deferred-auth signup → a Space pre-seeded in the niche's mode → land in
  the editor.
- **Enquiry → DM:** contact-only Market service → one button → a DM opens
  instantly seeded with the message → seller pinged in-app + email. Moneyless by
  design; ends at "arrange," never "pay."
- **Event guest → member:** QR scan → RSVP → verified check-in → join the Space →
  a Run (Meetup midweek + Weekend Gathering pre-scheduled) → return.

### Distribution layer

- **Entry Points:** short link + branded QR + print-ready flyer, owned by a
  member, grouped into campaigns. Crew builder: template → 3–4 slots → done in
  under 60 seconds. Templates: Event, Circle, Invite, Waitlist/Beta, Partner,
  Custom. Conversion: scan → signup, attributed (+40 Zaps to the owner).
- **Every visitor gets one question** — *who are you?* — at `/start`: five
  personas (visitor / practitioner / partner / builder / investor), stamped
  forever for per-persona nurture emails.

## 4. The public site

- **frequencylocal.com** — long-form editorial site, same app, own chrome.
- Routes: `/` (splash) · `/the-lab` · `/the-community` · `/the-quest` ·
  `/how-it-works` · `/about` (the Moonlight Beach origin story) · `/pricing` ·
  `/discover` · `/start` · 12 pain-SEO articles + 4 Labs guides · the five
  `/for/*` doors · `/founders`.
- Three-pillar registers: **The Lab** (write into the body: heat then cold, steam,
  cedar, low amber light, the exhale) · **The Community** (belonging: faces that
  light up, known by name, missed when you are gone) · **The Quest** (meaning +
  momentum: becoming someone your people count on).
- Everything ladders to three self-selected roles: **Lead** (start one Circle) ·
  **Practice** (do one practice today) · **Spread** (bring one person).
- `/discover` is the crawlable front door: read-only, city-level location only
  for anonymous visitors, every interaction a sign-in CTA.
- Marketing pages are WYSIWYG-editable in-app; `/` and `/about` are code-locked.
- Page spine when composing: PhotoHero → Lead → alternating beats → proof (stats /
  cards) → ink statement band → BetaCTA. "When a page feels thin, add a beat,
  don't pad copy."

## 5. SEO / AEO

- **AI citation is a primary acquisition channel** — the demographic asks
  machines these questions late at night. All major AI crawlers explicitly
  allowed; `/llms.txt` curated; answer-first chunking (question H2, 1–3 sentence
  liftable answer); schema everywhere; Wikidata entity resolution pending ("until
  then AI describes the pain but won't name Frequency").
- **Nine topic clusters**, pain-first, never practice-first: friendship as an
  adult · can't switch off · loneliness (owns "third place / third space") · life
  after the feed · new in town (+ city variants) · host a gathering · start a
  Circle · why groups die ("structure, not charisma") · crew training. Plus four
  Labs guides (what is a third space, how to run a community space, tools for
  community builders, host a recurring gathering).
- **pSEO with honesty:** city pages only where real Circles exist; empty states
  recruit a Latent Leader, never fake listings. Test: "remove the city name — is
  it still useful?"
- **The data moat:** one citable first-party research piece per quarter (return
  rate, weeks-to-belonging). Answer engines cite original sources; nobody else
  has these numbers.
